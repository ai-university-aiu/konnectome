PART SEVEN - SIGN-OFF

The session was asked to go faster and it did - six slices instead of one, on a pairing rule that turned out to work. But that is not what it will be remembered for.

What it will be remembered for is three separate discoveries that a carefully-written rule had outlived its own reasoning, and that nobody had noticed because the reasoning was good. A promise not to renumber, kept for fourteen versions, protecting a reading nobody performs. A conclusion that a fix was blocked, correct in every step, wrong for fifty-one slices because the thing it went looking for was already there under a different name. And a rule against inventing numbers, extracted from six correct refusals, which quietly grew a clause that would have forbidden Edison.

None of the three was careless. That is the whole point. A well-argued constraint is the cheapest thing in this project to inherit and the most expensive thing to re-examine, because re-examining it costs a session an hour it would rather spend building, and inheriting it costs nothing at all until the day it is wrong.

And the same shape ran through the code. Five slices in a row found a defect that would not have failed - a boundary never crossed, a zero reading as calm, a threshold decided by rounding, a table row that was not a transition, a clip that annihilated. One of them had a test guarding it that had passed for nineteen slices, written well, by somebody thinking clearly, pinning down a behaviour the corpus forbids in as many words.

So the lesson is one lesson at two altitudes. THE DANGEROUS THING IS NEVER THE OBVIOUS MISTAKE. It is the careful piece of work whose ground has moved.

The owner's correction at the end of the day is the right note to hand forward, because it is the cure rather than the diagnosis. When theory runs out, go to the bench. Try things. Keep what works. Write down what you tried and why the survivor won - and sign your name to it, so that the next person can tell what came from the book and what came from you.

That last clause is the whole of the discipline. Not never guess. Never disguise.
