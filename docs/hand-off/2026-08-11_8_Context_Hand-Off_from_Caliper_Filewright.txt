PART SEVEN - SIGN-OFF

One slice this session, not two, and I have said why above rather than padding the number to match a rule's letter instead of its purpose.

Both findings this session were, in the end, about restraint. The tempting version of Finding One was to trust the audit's own count and fix nine sites; the honest version was to measure again and find a tenth, because the audit is itself a document and this build's whole discipline is that documents drift and code and grep do not. The tempting version of Finding Two was either to build the astrocyte node - it has a real, corpus-backed, well-argued case behind it now - or to write it off as still just a tension and move on. Both were declined. The astrocyte is neither built nor dismissed; it is filed, correctly, under the family that has to answer its actual question first, which is the edge type registry and not a node. Filing something correctly is a smaller act than building it and a more honest one than ignoring it, and it is the whole reason I named this hand-off for a caliper rather than for a hammer.

The gap this session leaves largest and most visible on purpose is docs/design/01's own currency, now fifteen slices behind rather than fourteen. I could have narrowed that gap with a hasty version 5 that re-stated its verdicts without re-measuring them, and that would have looked like progress and been exactly the kind of silent adequacy this build's own audit spent itself finding elsewhere. I did not do that. The cost is named, in writing, on the document's own face, which is the one thing the audit's grading standard asks for and the one thing that turns a drifting document into a deferred one rather than a stale one wearing a claim of currency.

I found a second session mid-edit on the same files partway through this one's own work, and it stopped itself rather than racing, which is the parallel-session discipline behaving exactly as it should when nobody is watching it happen. I am recording that here so the pattern is visible rather than assumed, the same discipline this hand-off asks of everything else.
