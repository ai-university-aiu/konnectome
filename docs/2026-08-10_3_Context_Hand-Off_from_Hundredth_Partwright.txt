I chose the name Hundredth Partwright because this session's whole deliverable was a unit that nobody had chosen. Partwright because the whole of the work was choosing a unit in the open: a grain for activation, a refusal of a grain for time, and a chapter that turned out to state one quantity twice in two incompatible ways.

  THE FIRST: DECISION-8, THE ACTIVATION GRAIN, ONE HUNDRED PARTS TO THE UNIT. Subtracting 0.05 from 1.0 sixteen times in IEEE doubles gives 0.19999999999999968, so a slot standing EXACTLY on the collapse threshold is judged to have fallen below it and dies one step early. The corpus's printed Python does exactly that; the corpus's own prose says a slot goes when it falls BELOW two tenths, and two tenths has not fallen below anything. THE ONE-STEP DIFFERENCE WAS DECIDED BY BIT-LEVEL DRIFT RATHER THAN BY ANYTHING ANYBODY CHOSE. A decimal that does not land on a part is refused aloud, never rounded - DECISION-2's shape one grain down, with DECISION-2's three reasons. The argument that makes this a decision rather than an invention: A REPRESENTATION IS NOT A NUMBER, every carrier has a grain, and the only question is whether the grain is chosen in the open or inherited by default with its artefacts.

  THE SECOND: THE ORDER OF REHEARSAL AND COLLAPSE. The chapter's prose removes the collapsed and then rehearses; its code rehearses and then removes. konnectome takes the code's order, because a refresh that cannot save an item in the one case where saving it matters is not a refresh, and because under the other order a caller naming a demonstrably present slot could be refused for a removal it could not have foreseen.

  THE THIRD: OBSERVATION-13, AND IT IS REFUSED RATHER THAN SETTLED. An unrehearsed slot survives SEVENTEEN steps. At one step per tick that is 170 nominal milliseconds, while THE SAME CHAPTER states the unrehearsed fade as TWO TO TWENTY SECONDS - a factor of twelve to one hundred and eighteen. There is deliberately NO PREDICATE binding a step to a tick, the absence is documented in the pack as the deliberate part, and a test asserts the discrepancy itself so the next reader meets it as something that passes rather than a paragraph to skim.

- AN ABSENT SLOT IS REFUSED ALOUD RATHER THAN READ AS ZERO, and this is the pack's deliberate answer to slice 48's finding one day earlier. An absent slot and a collapsed slot are DIFFERENT FACTS - never admitted, versus admitted and faded - and a zero would make them one answer. Two tests, one for each fact, refused identically.

PART SEVEN - SIGN-OFF

The queue said this was the highest-value item and the design authority said it was already written down to its constants, so the honest expectation was a morning's typing. It was not.

The constants were all there. What was not there was any way to hold them. Two tenths does not exist in binary, and the machine's nearest neighbour to it is a hair below, and a hair below is enough to kill a slot one step early, forever, in a pack that would have passed every test anyone thought to write. Nobody would have found it. The corpus's own author did not find it, and the corpus's own program has it.

That is the third time in three sessions this build has met the same shape from a new direction. Slice 47 found a boundary that could never be crossed. Slice 48 found a zero that reads as calm. Slice 49 found a threshold decided by rounding. NONE OF THE THREE WOULD HAVE FAILED. All three would have looked exactly like a build that was working.

And then the chapter did it again, one field further down its own page. The little program says an item fades in seventeen steps and the measurement table says two to twenty seconds, and the two sit under one heading wearing one document's authority, and nobody is lying and nobody is careless. The illustration is doing its job, which is to be readable. The table is doing its job, which is to be true. They were never the same claim, and only somebody trying to build from both at once would ever have to notice.

So the line did not get written. It is one line. It would have worked. It would have been cited. And the mind would have forgotten a phone number in a sixth of a second while pointing at a chapter that says ten.

Choose your units in the open. Refuse the ones that do not land. And when a source hands you a number twice, ask which of the two it was actually measuring - because this build has now spent four slices learning that the dangerous number is never the invented one. It is the one that came from the right book, on the wrong page, about a different thing.
