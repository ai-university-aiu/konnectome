PART TWO - THE FIVE REFINEMENT THEMES

THEME ONE. THE MODE REGISTER, AT EVERY GRAIN. The corpus's most unanimous demand: every layer's modes companion specifies its constructs as hybrid automata - a small register of discrete operating modes, continuous state evolving within the current mode, per-mode transfer functions, an explicit transition table, and fault regimes excluded from the register and watched by supervisor processes instead. STATUS AT SLICE 47 - THIS THEME IS BUILT AT TWO GRAINS AND CLOSED AS A FAMILY. Slices 39, 39, 40, 41, 42, 44 and 46 built all seven of its design authority's Part Nine slices; slices 43 and 45 built two more the authority did not foresee and the family turned out to need.

THEME TWO. THE TWO-SHIFT PRINCIPLE - THE OFFLINE CONSOLIDATION CYCLE. PART FOUR-A - THE FIRST PRESSURE, NEW IN VERSION 3: KONNECTOME CANNOT NAME AN INDIVIDUAL

THIS IS THE FINDING VERSION 3 EXISTS FOR, and it is placed before the older pressures because it is now the largest.

THE CLAIM. Konnectome has CONSTRUCT KINDS and has no way to name a CONSTRUCT INDIVIDUAL. Its archetypes - relay, integrator, oscillator, attractor, gate, comparator - are kinds, instantiated wherever a world happens to want one. Its graph is a flat list of interface terms with no facility to name, group, or address a portion of itself. There is no "the thalamus" in this repository, and no way to say one.

THE CORPUS, BY CONTRAST, ADDRESSES INDIVIDUALS EVERYWHERE AND AT EVERY GRAIN. Every row of every one of Layer 11's twenty-six DOWNWARD ASSIGNMENT sections names a particular thing: the thalamus to faithful relay, the hippocampus to theta-coded encoding with sharp-wave ripple replay suppressed, thalamic reticular cells to rhythmic burst firing, the flip-flop motifs OF THE STATE MACHINERY latched to wake. Layer 8's motifs are typed subgraphs with named roles. Layer 9's regions are 77 named individuals with registers. Layer 7's types are drawn from a catalogue and installed at addressable junctions.

FOUR INDEPENDENT ARRIVALS, WHICH IS WHY THIS IS A PRESSURE AND NOT A TASK.

ARRIVAL ONE, FROM THEME FOUR, AT VERSION 2. Named subgraph views exist only in specification prose. connection_graph exports eleven predicates and not one names, groups or addresses a portion of the graph. The consequence recorded then: the motif catalogue is a BUILD, not a re-use, and Family Five is larger than its one line implied.

ARRIVAL TWO, FROM THE MODE-REGISTER DESIGN AUTHORITY, EARLIER STILL. Its Part Nine defers the region-grain construct BY NAME, on the ground that it requires promoting a territory from a level to a first-class NAMED construct. That deferral has stood unexamined ever since, read as a scoping choice rather than as evidence.

ARRIVAL THREE, FROM LAYER 11, AT SLICE 46, LOGGED AS OBSERVATION-11. The master register's downward assignment table ships EMPTY, and the reason is not that the mechanism is unfinished - it is built and proved against a fixture - but that there is nothing to address. The one row that looks available is the one that must be refused: the corpus says the state machinery's flip-flops latch to wake, and konnectome's gate was validated against the corpus's flip-flop entry, so writing "waking assigns every gate the open mode" is one line and reads like fidelity - and would assign EVERY GATE IN THE REPOSITORY the mode of ONE NAMED FLIP-FLOP.

ARRIVAL FOUR, FROM THE WATCHDOG, AT SLICE 47, AND IT IS NEW HERE AND WAS NOT NOTICED AT THE TIME. Slice 45 built a watchdog and could not wire it to the gate archetype; slice 47 wired it to the two-process governor instead. The reason given was that the governor has a CYCLE and the gate does not, and that is true and sufficient. BUT THERE IS A SECOND REASON UNDERNEATH IT WHICH NOBODY STATED: THE GOVERNOR IS A SINGLETON AND THE GATE IS NOT. There is exactly one two-process governor in a running konnectome, so "the governor" names an individual by accident of there being only one; there are many gates, so "the gate" names a kind and a watchdog aimed at it would be watching all of them at once. The window question and the naming question arrived together and were solved together without either being distinguished.

WHY THE FOUR TOGETHER ARE MORE THAN THE SUM. Each was recorded as a local finding about the family it appeared in - a Theme Four sizing correction, a scoping deferral, a Layer 11 observation, a watchdog wiring choice. NONE OF THE FOUR SESSIONS THAT FOUND THEM SAW ANOTHER. Held together, they say something none of them says alone: THE MISSING CAPABILITY IS NOT A FEATURE OF ANY ONE FAMILY. It sits underneath four of them, and every one of those four will independently rediscover it and independently work around it.

THE ACCIDENTAL WORKAROUND THAT ALREADY EXISTS, AND IT IS THE MOST USEFUL THING IN THIS SECTION. KONNECTOME ALREADY HAS NAMED INDIVIDUALS AND HAS NEVER CALLED THEM THAT: ITS SINGLETON PACKS. There is one neuromodulator bus, one two-process governor, one master register, one offline-consolidation phase, one supervisor channel, one observer. Each is addressable because there is exactly one of it. That is why slice 47's join worked, and it is why slice 46's did not - the master register tried to address ARCHETYPES, which are plural.

THE CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS A REAL SCHEDULING FINDING RATHER THAN A REPHRASING. THE FIRST DOWNWARD ASSIGNMENT DOES NOT HAVE TO WAIT FOR THE REGION-GRAIN CONSTRUCT. It has to wait for a SINGLETON WITH A MODE REGISTER that a global state has a corpus-supported reason to assign. konnectome will have exactly that the moment the working-memory blackboard exists, because there is one board, it has a four-mode register in the corpus, and the corpus states plainly that the board is erased in slow-wave sleep. Version 2 already noticed that Family Four item 4 would be the first downward assignment and called it almost free; VERSION 3 ADDS THE REASON IT IS FREE, which is that a board is a singleton and a gate is not, and that reason is what makes it a general rule rather than a lucky case.

SO THE FINDING SPLITS IN TWO, AND THE TWO HALVES SCHEDULE DIFFERENTLY.

THE CHEAP HALF - ASSIGNING TO SINGLETONS - NEEDS NO NEW CAPABILITY AT ALL and is available as soon as a singleton with a register exists. It is not a slice. It is a property of a slice already high in the queue.

THE EXPENSIVE HALF - ADDRESSING ONE INSTANCE AMONG MANY OF A KIND - IS A GENUINE MISSING LAYER, and it is what the motif catalogue, the region grain, the junction-grain register and any per-instance watchdog all actually need. It is a naming and addressing facility over the connection graph: a way to say THIS gate rather than gates. That is one capability, wanted by four families, and it has never appeared in Part Six as an item because each family described it in its own vocabulary.

WHAT THIS PRESSURE DOES NOT CLAIM. It does not claim the architecture is wrong: nothing in the ten components forbids naming, and Component 1's own specification prose describes named subgraph views, so this is an UNBUILT PROMISE rather than a contradiction. It does not claim a causalontology wall - see Part Five, where it is recorded as a watch and not a routing. And it does not decide the SHAPE of the facility, which is a design authority's job and not this document's: whether a name is a datum beside the graph, a field on an interface term, or a view built by a predicate is exactly the kind of detail this document has learned it is unreliable about.


PART FOUR - HONEST PRESSURES AND NON-CLOSURES

THE ELEVENTH-COMPONENT QUESTION. The global workspace is a capacity-limited broadcast of COGNITIVE CONTENT with an emergent winner-take-all arbiter and a hard ignition threshold. Whether it is an eleventh-component-scale change or another beside-the-skeleton construct is deliberately NOT decided, and the recommended path remains a pilot pack sitting beside the skeleton, with the component question answered by what the pilot teaches. Version 2 sharpened the evidence on both sides and re-affirmed the decision not to decide; version 3 re-affirms it again.

VERSION 3 ADDS ONE PIECE OF EVIDENCE, AND IT LEANS TOWARD BESIDE-THE-SKELETON. The strongest argument for component scale was that the ignited state is HELD and conditions everything below it, which is the shape of the global operating state konnectome deliberately put on the bus at slice 35. THAT SHAPE HAS NOW BEEN BUILT OUT IN FULL at slice 46 - a held state, with a name, a sub-mode, and a mechanism for conditioning everything below it - AND IT REQUIRED NO COMPONENT. If the workspace's held-and-conditioning character is what made it look component-scale, that character has now been demonstrated to be absorbable. THIS IS EVIDENCE AND NOT A DECISION: the workspace's other component-scale argument, that its output changes WHAT EVERY OTHER COMPONENT READS within one tick, is untouched by slice 46 and remains the real question. The pilot's five questions stand unaltered and it still MUST NOT MERGE without answering them.

THE EPHEMERAL-STATE QUESTION. Resolved at version 2 and unchanged: unrecordedness is the DEFINING PROPERTY of working memory, stated three times in one chapter, and an implementation whose slots persisted would be WRONG against the corpus rather than merely unfaithful. The observer records THAT a tick happened, never WHAT was in it, so the ephemeral guarantee is currently free. GROWING THE OBSERVER TO CARRY TICK CONTENT IS A LARGER DECISION THAN THE WORKING-MEMORY PACK ITSELF and must never be smuggled in as a side effect of a memory slice.

VERSION 3 SHARPENS THE OBSERVER HALF OF THAT, because a second reason to leave the observer alone has appeared. OBSERVATION-10 records that the observer's instant advances one nominal second per ordinal tick while DECISION-2 makes a tick ten nominal milliseconds - a factor of one hundred. Nothing is wrong today because the instant is an IDENTITY CARRIER and nothing subtracts two of them, and slice 45's watchdog pointedly measured durations from its OWN history rather than from the trace. THE OBSERVER IS NOW THE ONE PLACE IN THE REPOSITORY WHERE TWO DECISIONS DISAGREE, and the cheap closer is to make its instant's ordinal-only status a REFUSAL rather than a comment. The expensive closer re-mints every content-addressed token occurrence identifier in the repository.

THE ASTROCYTE TENSION. The architecture holds glia as modulatory entries carrying no activation of their own; the Layer 6 corpus tags eight non-neuronal types cognitive, and the protoplasmic astrocyte chapter specifies a computing, gliotransmitter-releasing integrator with state. A tension, not a contradiction - noted, not acted on, at slice 47 as at slice 40.

THE NUMBERING CROSS-REFERENCE. The corpus re-addresses the guiding book's sections 2.10 and 2.11 as Layer 10 constructs. Housekeeping, non-breaking. NOTE THAT THOSE ARE THE VERY SECTIONS WHOSE NAMED-SUBGRAPH PROMISE IS PROSE-ONLY, so this item and Part Four-A touch the same two sections and a session doing either should do both.

THE FIFTH PRESSURE IS CLOSED. Version 2's finding that KONNECTOME HAS NO SECONDS AND THE CORPUS COUNTS IN THEM is discharged by DECISION-2 at slice 43. It was cheap, it was load-bearing, and it was invisible from every summary in version 1 until the corpus beneath two separate themes was read. RECORDED HERE AS A CLOSED PRESSURE RATHER THAN DELETED, because the pattern is the reusable part: a quantity the corpus states everywhere and the architecture has no unit for is a decision, not a gap, and it will not be found by reading a theme.


PART FIVE - WALLS: NONE ROUTED, SEVEN WATCHES CARRIED, ONE ADDED, ONE NARROWED

Applying the Second and Third Commandment gates strictly, the analysis routes NOTHING to either change order today. THAT JUDGMENT NOW SURVIVES THREE ASSESSMENTS, FOUR DEEP READS AND THIRTEEN FURTHER SLICES, six of which built new packs, and not one of which came near a wall.

VERSION 3 STRENGTHENS THE JUDGMENT WITH SOMETHING NO EARLIER VERSION COULD OFFER: the thirteen slices between version 2 and this one added a watchdog, a supervisor, a stabiliser, a master register and a ticks-per-second convention, and EVERY ONE OF THEM ROUTED NOWHERE FOR THE SAME REASON - a value computed inside one runtime and read inside the same runtime travels to nobody and fails the consumer-versus-ontology test. That test has now been applied six times by six sessions and returned the same answer each time, which is a considerably better warrant than a reading.

WATCH-1, the mode kind. A mode register and its transitions are runtime state, expressible as ordinary pack data; only if a faculty must EXPORT its operating posture as a shareable signed record does a causalontology mode kind become a candidate. STANDS, AND IS NOW UNCLAIMED AFTER REGISTERS WERE BUILT AT TWO GRAINS AND A MASTER REGISTER ABOVE THEM. The strongest available negative evidence: the layer whose registers are most obviously a whole-system posture was built and did not need one.

WATCH-2, the episodic event index. A bound what-where-when conjunction with consolidation status, distinct from its constituent token occurrences - the likeliest genuine wall among the memory slices, to be claimed only when retrieval-by-fragment is actually built and actually cannot express it. STANDS, untouched.

WATCH-3, consolidation provenance. STANDS; slice 38 built the offline phase and did not hit it.

WATCH-4, the workspace ignition record. Version 2 sharpened this from a guess into a conditional with a named decision point: it depends entirely on WHAT A CONTENT IS, which is konnectome's decision. If a content is an identifier for a record that already exists, the existing kinds carry the claim and the watch closes without ever being claimed; if a content is a structure with no record of its own, the watch may become real.

VERSION 3 ADDS A SECOND CONDITION TO THE SAME WATCH, FROM PART FOUR-A. If konnectome builds a naming facility, a content may turn out to be A NAMED VIEW OVER THE GRAPH - which is neither of version 2's two branches. That third possibility is worth naming now because it would arrive from a different family entirely, and a workspace pilot that met it unprepared would be deciding a change-order question while thinking about something else. DECIDE WHAT A CONTENT IS BEFORE THE PILOT, and decide it knowing there are three candidate answers rather than two.

WATCH-5, the weight checkpoint. STANDS, re-examined at version 2 and not re-examined here.

WATCH-6, the language pack's second half. STANDS. Rung Two's deepening has not been attempted since version 2.

WATCH-7, THE TYPE CATALOGUE ARTIFACT. The sixty-four junction types with their axes, registers and learning-rule identities are, in the corpus, a catalogue meant for mechanical splice. If konnectome's edge type registry ever needs to travel between agents as one signed, evidence-graded, content-addressed catalogue rather than as pack data compiled into one runtime, that is when a causalontology kind becomes a candidate. STANDS. Family Three is still unbuilt, so this watch has still never been tested.

WATCH-8, THE CONSTRUCT NAME, NEW IN VERSION 3, FROM PART FOUR-A. If konnectome builds a naming facility for individual constructs and a name must ever be SHARED - so that two agents, or a build and its successor, agree that this particular gate is the same gate - then a construct identity becomes a candidate for a content-addressed record. IT IS RECORDED AS A WATCH AND EXPLICITLY NOT ROUTED, and the reason is the gate itself: a name used only to address a construct inside one runtime is ordinary pack data and travels to nobody. THE DECISION POINT IS NAMED SO IT IS NOT PASSED THROUGH BY ACCIDENT: the moment a construct name is required to be STABLE ACROSS RUNS is the moment this watch becomes assessable, and a naming facility could easily acquire that requirement without anyone choosing it.

ONE WATCH IS NARROWED, AND IT IS RECORDED AS A DOWNGRADE RATHER THAN A CLOSURE. Version 2 listed motif-level state - the attractor occupancy that belongs to a view and to no member of it - among three candidate watches CONSIDERED AND REJECTED, on the ground that it is a new state LOCATION and not a new record kind. That reasoning stands and is now better supported: slices 44, 45 and 46 each created exactly such a new state location - a published bias, a windowed history, a widened global state - and none of them came near a record kind. THE REJECTION IS CONFIRMED BY BUILDING THREE TIMES.

Sections C, D, and E of the causalontology change order remain HELD. Nothing in this analysis, in any of the four deep reads, or in any of the thirteen slices since version 2 releases them.

PART SEVEN - CLOSING

The Vision Document Set arrived with fanfare and survives three soberings. It does not break the konnectome that exists; it explains it, and then it points, with unusual convergence, at three things: give the constructs modes, give the mind a night shift, and give thought a stage to ignite on.

THE FIRST OF THE THREE IS DONE. Nine slices, six numbered decisions, two grains, and a formalism the other four themes will now be written in. Version 2 closed by saying what remained was to fill the frame, one slice, one test, one told story at a time. Thirteen slices later the frame has a vocabulary in it, and the honest measure of what that bought is not the vocabulary but what became cheap: every remaining family is easier to describe in this version than it was in the last, and two of them are easier because a slice in a different family happened to build the thing they needed.

WHAT VERSION 3 ADDS TO THAT CLOSING IS ONE UNCOMFORTABLE OBSERVATION AND ONE ENCOURAGING ONE.

THE UNCOMFORTABLE ONE. This document described the same missing capability in four separate places, in four different vocabularies, across two versions and a design authority, and did not notice. Each description was locally correct. Each was recorded in the family where it was found. None of them said the word that would have joined them, because none of the four sessions had read the other three's findings as findings about the same thing. THE FAILURE MODE IS NOT STALENESS, WHICH THIS DOCUMENT ALREADY WARNS ABOUT TWICE. IT IS FRAGMENTATION: a finding filed under the family that discovered it is a finding nobody can add up. The mechanism that eventually caught it was not a reading - it was a slice hitting the same wall from the opposite end of the architecture and someone happening to hold both.

THE ENCOURAGING ONE. Every one of the six numbered decisions was taken because a slice could not proceed without it, and four of the six ended as REFUSALS in running code rather than as paragraphs. A decision written as a refusal cannot be forgotten, cannot be inherited by accident, and cannot be quietly reinterpreted by a later session - which is why three of them have been inherited without argument and none has been relitigated. The corpus could not have supplied any of them; it knows nothing about ticks, windows, poles or grains. THAT IS THE MECHANISM THIS PROJECT HAS FOR THE PART OF THE WORK THE CORPUS CANNOT DO, and thirteen slices are enough to say that it works.

The frame stands. The vocabulary exists. What is missing is smaller than it was and better named: a way to say THIS one rather than these, and three faculties the corpus has already designed and konnectome has not yet built.
