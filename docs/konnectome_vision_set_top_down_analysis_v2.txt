WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT - READ THIS BEFORE BUILDING FROM ANYTHING BELOW

THIS DOCUMENT HOLDS TWO KINDS OF CONTENT, AND THEY DO NOT CARRY THE SAME WEIGHT. Read the distinction before relying on any part of it, because an earlier version of this preamble flattened the two and was corrected on 2026-08-09.

FIRST KIND - THE SUMMARIES. Where this document summarises the Vision Document Set, it is DERIVED and carries no authority over the corpus. SECOND KIND - THE FINDINGS, and they are the larger part. Part One's verdict on the top-level decisions, Part Four's honest pressures, Part Five's walls-none-routed judgment and its watches, and Part Six's suggested slice families are NOT summaries of anything. HOW THIS ANALYSIS WAS MADE

The Eighth Commandment mandates a top-down analysis of the Vision Document Set: read the set from the framework documents downward, assess whether any top-level architectural decisions should be revised in their light, record findings in the ledger, and route any genuine wall through the gated change orders. This document is that analysis. The reading order was the commandment's own: first the two framework documents (THE_FIFTEEN_LAYERS_IN_BRIEF and TFELOAOTHNAES), then the four konnectome-facing top layers (the Layer 12 Faculty Specification, the Layer 13 Tutor Protocol, the Layer 14 Knowledge Environment, the Layer 15 Design Method), then the six heavy layer corpora (Layers 4 and 5, the slow variables; Layer 6, the cells, with the five neuronal volumes and the chapter template; Layer 7, the synaptics; Layer 8, the microcircuits; Layer 9's modes companion; Layer 10, the systems; Layer 11, the global states), each read in full at the outline and modes level and read deeply with representative-chapter sampling at the manuscript level. The baseline was verified green before the reading began: 494 tests across twenty-seven packs, four gates at zero violations, the capstone clean, conformance 137 of 137 in both cousins.

THAT BASELINE LINE IS LEFT EXACTLY AS WRITTEN, and version 2 does not update it. It is the record of a verification act performed before the reading began; its tense already marks it as past; overwriting it with today's figures would falsify the record rather than update it. The baseline at the time version 2 was written was 648 tests across thirty packs, four gates at zero violations, capstone clean, conformance 137 of 137 in both cousins, main at commit be63b59.

The commandment's sobriety clauses governed every judgement below: the set informs and refines, it does not break; nothing here invalidates a passing test, a merged slice, or a green gate; and the Safety Gate stands over all of it.

PART TWO - THE FIVE REFINEMENT THEMES

THEME ONE. THE MODE REGISTER, AT EVERY GRAIN. This is the corpus's most unanimous demand: every layer's modes companion specifies its constructs as hybrid automata - a small register of discrete operating modes, continuous state evolving within the current mode, per-mode transfer functions, an explicit transition table (trigger, direction, timescale), and fault regimes excluded from the register and watched by supervisor processes instead. The demand recurs identically at the cell (386 mode entries), the junction, the motif, the region (77 entries), the system (44 entries), and culminates at Layer 11, where a GLOBAL STATE is defined as a coordinated assignment of modes across everything below - the master mode register. (AUDIT: these three counts are measured and confirmed; the elevation paragraph in Part One formerly disagreed with them and has been corrected to match.) Konnectome AT SLICE 34 ran one rule per construct, always: the gate archetype's binary flip and arousal's settle-to-baseline were the only mode-like behaviours in the repository. (SUPERSEDED AT SLICES 39 AND 40 - the mode_register pack now gives every construct a register and the gate archetype carries a genuine two-mode one; see the STATUS paragraphs below.) The refinement: a construct-level mode register (mode name, active rule and parameters per mode, transition table), with today's single-rule construct as the first-class one-mode case (CORRECTED AT SLICE 39 - this formerly read "the degenerate one-mode case"; the corpus states three times that a register of one is first-class and proudly stated, never degenerate), and with transitions throwable from the bus - because the corpus's second unanimous claim is that mode setting is largely a service purchased from above, through the volume transmission channel the bus already is. One naming hazard, recorded now so it is not discovered later: the data model's existing MODE flag on interfaces and molecules means directed-versus-broadcast topology, which will collide with the operating-mode vocabulary; the collision must be resolved (by qualification or rename) before any mode-register slice. (CLOSED TO ZERO AT SLICE 39 - no such flag was ever in running code; see the STATUS paragraph below.)

ADDENDUM TO THEME ONE (added 2026-08-09, after slice 38; the analysis above stands as written on the date of its own execution). A STANDING OWNER REQUEST, recorded here with the theme it constrains rather than only in a hand-off, because a hand-off is read once and this outlives many of them. When this family reaches its DEMONSTRATION phase - the D of the SPARCD waterfall, mirrored in konnectome_6_demonstration at its then-current version - the owner has asked for A VISUALIZATION CONTROL PANEL SHOWING EVERY MODE OF EVERY CONSTRUCT IN OPERATION, AT EVERY GRAIN LEVEL SIMULTANEOUSLY, IN REAL TIME. Read "every grain" in this theme's own terms, which is what makes the request precise rather than decorative: the cell (386 mode entries), the junction, the motif, the region (77 entries), and the system (44 entries) enumerated above, and the Layer 11 GLOBAL STATE that is defined as a coordinated assignment of modes across everything below - all shown AT ONCE and LIVE, so a watcher can see a bus-thrown transition propagate down the grains as it happens.

THE DESIGN CONSTRAINT THAT FOLLOWS IS NOT A FUTURE FEATURE, AND MUST BE HONOURED FROM THE FIRST MODE-REGISTER SLICE. A live, per-grain, EXTERNALLY OBSERVABLE read of every construct's current mode has to be possible for such a panel to exist at all. A design that treats a construct's mode as private state - readable only from inside the construct's own rule, or reachable only by running the rule - forecloses the panel entirely, and that is far cheaper to honour from the start than to retrofit onto a register that assumed modes were internal. Concretely, and in the house style this repository already keeps: the mode register is a VALUE THE WORLD CARRIES and any caller may read, in the explicit-stateless-value discipline the eligibility-trace store, the running-average store, and the per-territory target store already follow - never a private field hidden inside an engine, and never a fact derivable only by stepping the construct. The same discipline that let slice 32 thread the whole learning body through the live tick without moving a single engine predicate is exactly what will let this panel be built later without reopening the register to expose anything.

THE ADDENDUM'S REACH IS WIDER THAN THIS FAMILY, RECORDED AT VERSION 2 BECAUSE THREE READS TRIPPED OVER IT INDEPENDENTLY. It binds the motif grain (a motif view's own state - the attractor occupancy that belongs to the view and to no member of it - must be externally observable, which is Theme Four's decision 5), the selector (whether a partially settled competition is readable mid-race, which the corpus does not answer and which the addendum answers by default, and deciding it late would be the exact retrofit the addendum warns against), and working memory (a slot must be ephemeral AND observable, which are not in tension - the eligibility-trace store is already exactly that pattern and is the thing to copy). The addendum is therefore a constraint on FOUR families, not one.

The panel ITSELF, however, is a Demonstration deliverable and not a slice of the mode register: it is not to be built speculatively, it must not widen the mode-register slices, and it is not a reason to defer them. What the mode-register slices owe it is the OBSERVABILITY, not the panel.

THEME TWO. THE TWO-SHIFT PRINCIPLE - THE OFFLINE CONSOLIDATION CYCLE. The single most central gap the Vision Set names, reached convergently from below (Layers 4 and 5) and from above (Layer 11). The slow-variables volume says fast change and durable commitment must be separable: a write-allocation gate decides which events graduate to durable weights (spaced repetition consolidates where massed does not), a decaying per-connection tag entitles capture and is CONSUMED when spent, and write-permission over learning is itself a slow stored variable (the freeze fence, the readability gate). The global-states volume says the schedule of all of this belongs to a two-process governor driving an alternation the corpus claims is load-bearing: online computation and offline consolidation, with interleaved replay in the offline phase expressly to defeat catastrophic forgetting, synaptic renormalisation run in that phase, and the warning aimed squarely at the current posture: a synthetic mind built from the corpus's parts but run always-on would be a brain denied sleep. Konnectome's own architecture already promises this - Component 8's text commits to a slow consolidation phase run offline - and the promise is unbuilt; meanwhile the machinery of slices 27 through 33 (eligibility traces, homeostatic scaling with per-territory targets, the observer's complete tick record) is precisely the parts an offline phase would compose. The distance to a first honest slice is short, and the standing pending line (the long-run coupled-loop proof) naturally merges into it.

The ephemeral-state question. Working memory is by definition activity, not record - when the activity stops, the content is gone - while konnectome's discipline is that durable state lives in records. A working-memory pack whose slots are deliberately unrecorded (save through the observer's tick trace, which sees everything) appears consistent with both; recorded here so the tension is met by design, not stumbled into.

PART SEVEN - CLOSING

The Vision Document Set arrived with fanfare and survives sober reading. It does not break the konnectome that exists; it explains it, and then it points, with unusual convergence, at three things: give the constructs modes, give the mind a night shift, and give thought a stage to ignite on. The frame the first thirty-four slices built - forty as this version is written - is the frame the corpus asks for. What remains is to fill it - one slice, one test, one told story at a time.

WHAT VERSION 2 ADDS TO THAT CLOSING, having read four corpora beneath four themes and audited every countable claim above. The frame still stands and no finding was reversed, which is the result that matters most. But the summaries fared worse than the findings did, and they fared worse in a consistent direction: EVERY THEME WAS RELIABLE ABOUT WHAT ITS CORPUS IS ABOUT AND UNRELIABLE ABOUT EVERY DETAIL A BUILDER WOULD NEED. One of them asserted a mechanism in konnectome's own code that has never existed. One told readers for four slices that the corpus's central gap was unaddressed while konnectome was closing it. One named five members of a nine-member list and dropped the two that konnectome cannot express. And a decision nobody had noticed - that konnectome's tick has no seconds in it while the whole cognitive cluster is measured in them - stood between the corpus's numbers and any slice that would use them, invisible from every summary until the corpus beneath two separate themes was read.

That is the Eighth Commandment's own rule, earned rather than asserted: a theme is a summary, not a specification, and the reading beneath it must be written down and cited back. Four such readings now exist where one did. The remaining work of this document is to keep them current - and the lesson of Theme Two, which went four slices stale while Theme One was maintained line by line, is that the theme nobody is currently building is the theme that goes stale unwatched.
