KONNECTOME COGNITIVE ARCHITECTURES COVERAGE  (v1)

The Ninth Commandment's first execution: the coverage survey of the Cognitive
Architectures Test-Bed. Date: 2026-08-09.

Author: D. R. Dison / ai-university-aiu.

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT - READ THIS BEFORE BUILDING FROM ANYTHING BELOW

This document is DERIVED, and it carries no guiding authority of its own. It is konnectome's own reading of the Cognitive Architectures Test-Bed, made because the Ninth Commandment mandates the coverage survey. Its authority is borrowed from the test-bed files it cites and, above them, from the biological blueprint - the Ninth Commandment's rule three is explicit that the Vision Document Set remains the gold-standard and north-star, that the test-bed measures coverage and does not steer the ship, and that where the two disagree the biology leads.

The guiding authority this document sits beneath is stated in full in the matching preamble of docs/konnectome_vision_set_top_down_analysis_v1.txt.

A COVERAGE BUG BELOW IS A FINDING, NOT A SPECIFICATION. Each names a closer, but none of them is a design. Before building a closer, read the corpus material that owns it, and record that reading. The same warning applies here as to the sibling analysis: designing from a summary is the armchair reasoning the Second Commandment forbids.


THE MANDATE (the Ninth Commandment, restated). The local, read-only directory
~/cognitive_architectures holds the study set of published cognitive architectures.
konnectome should be able to PERFORM each and every one of the functions of the
architectures catalogued there. Coverage is measured; the ship is steered by the
biology (the Vision Document Set, the Eighth Commandment). Where a test-bed
architecture and the biological blueprint disagree, the biology leads and the
disagreement is recorded. An architecture found unachievable with the current
konnectome, PrologAI, and Causalontology setup is a BUG, recorded in the ledger and
routed through the gated change orders under the Safety Gate. A kludge, were one ever
made, would be documented as a candidate discovery.

THE DISCIPLINE OF THIS DOCUMENT. The test-bed files are cited, never reproduced:
they are local-only and never become part of this repository. The architectures named
below are published systems of the public literature; naming them and judging
konnectome's coverage of their functions is the survey the commandment orders. And
the armchair rule of the Second Commandment stands over the Ninth: a coverage bug
named here becomes a LEDGER coverage entry with a named closer, but nothing is routed
to a change order until a build actually demonstrates the current setup cannot
express it. This survey read and judged; it built nothing; it therefore routes
nothing, exactly as the Eighth Commandment analysis routed nothing.

HOW THE SURVEY WAS MADE. Baseline verified green first (494 tests across
twenty-seven packs counted pack by pack, four gates at zero violations, capstone
story clean, conformance 137 of 137 in both cousins). The four test-bed text files
(the two books, the patterns fileset, and the combined talk-transcript analyses -
about eight hundred sixty kilobytes of prose) were read IN FULL by four parallel
reading assistants, each judging its file's catalogue against the twenty-seven packs,
the specification, and the Vision-Set top-down analysis; the four ledgers were then
synthesized and cross-checked against the konnectome ledger by the session itself.
The page-image files were not read (their content is the same books in image form).
One honest caveat, in the Copper Atlaswright tradition: a verdict below is a reading
judgment, not a test run; the Layer 12 faculty suites, not this table, are the
acceptance tests.

VERDICT MEANINGS.
 - PERFORMABLE: existing packs demonstrably cover the architecture's functions.
 - EMERGENT: the function arises from existing machinery without a named module -
   the commandment says this is acceptable and expected.
 - PARTIAL: some functions covered, some not; the missing part is named.
 - COVERAGE BUG: the function cannot be performed today; per the commandment this is
   recorded as a bug, with its planned closer named. None is routed to a change order
   yet (the armchair rule); the WATCH entries of the Vision-Set analysis already
   stand guard at the likeliest routing points.
 - DIVERGENCE: the architecture's defining mechanism disagrees with the biological
   blueprint; the biology leads, and the disagreement is recorded rather than built.


================================================================================
SECTION ONE - THE NAMED ARCHITECTURES OF THE LITERATURE
================================================================================

PSI and MicroPsi (Dorner; Bach). Motivation, emotion, and behavior from explicit
homeostatic, social, and cognitive needs; drive reduction; modulator dynamics.
PERFORMABLE - the survey's most complete match. drive_system (needs, urges, set
points), arousal_regulation (modulator dynamics), neuromodulator_bus (the broadcast
modulators, nearly literal), situation_appraisal, empathy and other_minds (the social
needs). All four sources agree.

Active Inference (Friston). A generative model minimizing surprise; homeostatic
priors; action fulfilling prediction. PERFORMABLE at one level, PARTIAL in depth:
prediction_loop (predict, compare, signed error), drive_system (the priors that
defeat the dark room), plasticity_engine (error-driven update), body_interface
(closing the loop). Missing: the multi-tier hierarchy of predictions-of-predictions
and precision-weighted errors.

Behavior Oriented Design / Complete Autonomous Agent (Bryson). Specialized modules,
arbitration separating how from when, conflicting goals with exclusive means.
PERFORMABLE - nearly konnectome's own creed: packs, action_selector, drive_system,
override_controller, tick_engine. The nested-competence hierarchy exceeds the flat
selector; the planned winner-take-all deepening (Vision theme four) is the closer.

Procedural Reasoning System (Georgeff, Ingrand). React-while-pursuing-goals with
survival interrupts. PERFORMABLE: cognitive_cycle, action_selector, drive_system,
override_controller's vitality ranking.

Daydreamer (Mueller). Emotion from real or imagined events; regulation; emotion
influencing behavior. PERFORMABLE for the emotion loop (situation_appraisal,
arousal_regulation, pretend_play, neuromodulator_bus); the emotion-driven EPISODIC
reminding half waits on episodic memory (coverage bug one).

ICOM (the Independent Core Observer Model). Emotion-valued evaluation core under an
independent observer. PERFORMABLE: drive_system, situation_appraisal,
arousal_regulation, the passive observer, the bus, the override. konnectome's
passive observer is the non-homunculus form of the observing core.

The Piagetian Modeler and its successors PAM v2 / PamP2 (Miller). The test-bed's
own lineage: constructivist, diversity-first, about twenty-seven single-purpose
mechanisms over a shared memory, developed along Piagetian milestones. PARTIAL, and
architecturally KIN - the registry-and-scheduler federation of twenty-seven packs
against a stigmergic swarm of about twenty-seven mechanisms; the observation,
motivation, reaction, regulation, coping, and social clusters map nearly
pack-for-mechanism, and the milestone-assessment method IS konnectome's operating
method. konnectome lacks the Modeler's episodic viewpoints, solvers, realities,
detectors, and consolidators (the coverage bugs below); the Modeler lacks
konnectome's neuromodulator bus, override controller, provenance discipline, and
test-gated constitution - and its own reflection loop was never implemented.
DIVERGENCE recorded: the Modeler's single always-on activation-differentiated
memory against the blueprint's two-shift principle and separable fast/durable
stores; the biology leads (and the test-bed's own later essays partly concede the
point - a system that only accumulates is never born).

SOAR (Newell, Laird, Rosenbloom). Operators over problem spaces; impasse-driven
subgoaling; chunking. PARTIAL: operator selection with learned preferences maps to
action_selector plus plasticity_engine; the impasse recursion and trace chunking
have no counterpart (no runtime rule synthesis). DIVERGENCE recorded: uniformity-
first against the blueprint's diversity-first typed cells, edges, motifs, and
modes. IMPORTABLE IDEA, the survey's best in-frame theft: an impasse (a tie or
low-confidence selection) publishing a disturbance record on the bus, giving the
drives something to be curious about.

ACT-R (Anderson). Brain-mapped modules, capacity-limited buffers, declarative
retrieval by activation, subsymbolic attention. PARTIAL: the module-over-substrate
shape and the activation-with-decay calculus are performed (packs, weights,
arousal_regulation); the buffer bottleneck is the absent working-memory blackboard
and declarative retrieval-by-activation waits on episodic memory.

OpenCog (Goertzel). Cognitive synergy: diverse algorithms cooperating over a shared
store, a mini-PSI inside. EMERGENT for the synergy principle - it is konnectome's
own shape - with the attention-allocation economy (ECAN) and evolutionary program
learning absent (coverage bugs three and the self-programming divergence).

IDA and LIDA (Franklin). Global Workspace Theory: coalition competition, conscious
broadcast, broadcast-attached learning. COVERAGE BUG (the defining function): the
bus broadcasts modulator values, not cognitive content; competition, ignition, and
broadcast learning are the absent workspace. Closer: the workspace pilot pack beside
the skeleton (Vision theme five; WATCH-4 guards the ignition record). The blueprint
insists the arbiter be emergent winner-take-all, not a broadcaster-ruler.

Shanahan's cognitive architecture. Internal rehearsal mediated by affect, on a
global-workspace substrate. PARTIAL: the rehearsal-under-affect loop is performable
today (pretend_play, prediction_loop, situation_appraisal, simulated_body); the
workspace substrate is coverage bug three.

NARS (Wang). Reasoning under insufficient knowledge and resources. EMERGENT: the
stance is konnectome's ambient condition (one-tick budgets, evidence-graded records,
weight-graded beliefs, never-invent selection under what is known); the term-logic
letter and budget-driven anytime inference are not implemented. DIVERGENCE noted on
its non-embodiment; and the AIKR budget economy contradicts the dumb scheduler's
every-pack-its-turn fairness, which the blueprint ratifies.

CLARION (Sun). Implicit/explicit dual representation with metacognitive monitoring.
PARTIAL: motivation, emotion, and monitoring map (drive_system, situation_appraisal,
observer, override_controller); the subsymbolic implicit layer has no counterpart.

AERA (Nivel, Thorisson) and the self-programming-seed pattern. Constructivist
runtime self-programming: the system writes, scores, and culls its own executable
models. DIVERGENCE, recorded deliberately and not as a bug: runtime code synthesis
would breach the never-invent invariant, the regression-gate constitution, and the
protected-core discipline at once, and the blueprint's Layer 15 blesses the opposite
- evolution never breaks the running system, with the human in the selection loop.
konnectome's constructivist loop lives in the SPARCD development process, not the
runtime. Any future move toward runtime invention is a constitutional change, not a
slice. The same recording covers genetic modification of behavior structures
(mutation, crossover, culling) and chunk-synthesis generally.

CHEF and the case-based planners (Hammond; Meta-Darmok / Meta-AQUA; Rogue with
Prodigy and Xavier; LIVE). Plan memory, retrieval by similarity, failure
explanation, repair, replanning, self-designed experiments. PARTIAL-to-BUG family:
failure detection and causal explanation exist (prediction_loop, cause_and_effect,
situation_appraisal), the tutor-advice channel exists (Layer 13's protocol,
perturbation_interface), and cause_and_effect already performs a breadth-first
means-end planning trial owned as an intends attitude (slice 21) - so planning is
PARTIAL, not absent. Missing across the family: a case memory of whole plans
(waits on episodic memory), plan repair operators, discrimination-based rule
splitting, and self-designed experiments (the Designer/Experimenter pair). The
blueprint's sanctioned road for deliberation is winner-take-all settling plus
simulation chaining; any richer planner enters only as a PROPOSER whose candidates
the never-invent selector still arbitrates.

InteRRaP (Muller) and the layered agent societies. PARTIAL: situation recognition,
goal activation, and vertical takeover-on-failure map (situation_appraisal,
drive_system, override_controller); peer-agent negotiation is out of scope for a
single mind, and runtime belief retraction beyond weight update lives in
causalontology's assert-grade-retract, exercised by self_provenance.

JCB / JCB-English (Linker). Proof-based conversational knowledge base with trust
levels. PARTIAL: proof over a formal language is PrologAI's native strength, and
causalontology's signed, evidence-graded records are arguably the stronger form of
per-source trust; the conversational front end waits on the language coverage bug.
DIVERGENCE noted: knowledge told, not constructed - against the tutor protocol.

MANIC (Gashler). Unsupervised compression, outcome regression, reinforcement, and
introspective sensors. PARTIAL: the decision loop and - notably - the introspective
sensing are covered (observer and self_provenance are inward-facing senses); the
unsupervised high-dimensional representation learning belongs to the perception
coverage bug.

Meta Control Loop (Sanz) and the reflection/meta-control patterns. Monitor plus
runtime reconfigurer. PARTIAL: monitoring is complete (observer; override_controller
for vitality); the reconfigurer - starting, stopping, rewiring components at runtime
- does not exist, edges toward the homunculus the blueprint rejects, and the test-bed
lineage itself never implemented it. The mode registers with bus-thrown transitions
(Vision theme one) are the sanctioned bounded form.

RCS / NASREM (Albus). Nested sensor-model-planner triples over six timescales.
PARTIAL: one triple exists at the tick grain, and konnectome has fast (tick) and
slow (plasticity, homeostatic scaling) but not nested planning horizons; the
two-shift governor adds a second explicit timescale, not six. DIVERGENCE: strict
command hierarchy against the arbitrated federation.

QN-MHP (Liu; Feyen). Queuing networks of perception, cognition, motor servers.
EMERGENT: the registry running many packs per tick with bus transmission exhibits
the server-network shape; the queuing-theoretic human-performance timing model is an
orthogonal goal konnectome does not pursue.

PACO-PLUS. Shared object-action categories, real-world manipulation. PARTIAL:
categories formed from experience and SHARED as content-addressed records are a
strength (category_formation, causalontology); real-world sensorimotor grounding
waits at the Rung Five seam.

Holland's HUMAN / CRONOS. Mental simulation and episodic memory. PARTIAL: the
simulation half is performable; the episodic half is coverage bug one.

LeCun's objective-driven architecture. World model, actor, configurator. PARTIAL:
prediction_loop, action_selector, drive_system map; the configurator/solver is the
planning gap. DIVERGENCE: minimal-elements uniformity against heterogeneous modules.

Drescher's schema system. Context-action-result schemas from raw sensorimotor data;
marginal attribution birthing new schemas from statistics. PARTIAL: schema-like
learning and failure prediction are covered (cause_and_effect, category_formation,
prediction_loop); the spinoff engine - minting new discriminated rules from
contingency statistics - sits at the never-invent boundary and is recorded with the
divergence above.

Dynamic Interlaced Hierarchies / the transmutation repertoire (Michalski). Shallow
interlaced hierarchies where moving members yields the inference family. PARTIAL:
the hierarchies and several operations exist (category_formation,
concrete_operations); the systematic transmutation operators and a general
prover/refuter do not, and modus tollens wants first-class negation.

Cyc micro-theories / viewpoints (Lenat). Locally coherent, globally contradictory
reasoning contexts. COVERAGE BUG (small): no pack holds two locally consistent,
mutually contradictory belief sets as contexts. Nearest machinery: pretend_play's
one quarantined as-if channel, and the attitude kind's per-holder quarantine.
Closer: a viewpoints refinement of the realities family, if building demands it.

Global-workspace micro-agencies and the LLM-hybrid systems (Micro Agency
architecture; neuro-symbolic-with-LLM; Microsoft Agent AI; Watson / DeepQA;
AlphaStar; Deep Blue). The oracle-substrate family. DIVERGENCE, recorded once for
the family: these depend on large pretrained models, corpus-scale retrieval, or
brute-force adversarial search - substrates konnectome, PrologAI, and Causalontology
do not provide and the blueprint does not ask for. The test-bed's own essays class
the oracles as the passing paradigm ("eidetic oracles") and the developmental path
as the alternative; the biology leads, and konnectome stays on the developmental
path. Were an external oracle ever WANTED, the Layer 14 knowledge-environment seam
(external tools behind guarded interfaces) is where it would stand - a tool, not a
mind. The workspace function of the micro-agency family is coverage bug three.

Image schemas (Johnson, Lakoff) and the perceptual detector suites (icons, geons,
scenes, shapes, objects, locator, mapper). COVERAGE BUG (deferred frontier):
perception is deliberately the minimal honest body seam; no feature detectors, no
spatial mapping. Real perceptual feature learning likely wants a sub-symbolic
substrate or new bindings - to be discovered by building at the Rung Five seam,
where the finding would route honestly if the language or the data structure cannot
express it.

The speaker-hearer language model (Hausser lineage). Grounded acquisition from
sign sequences; comprehension and production. PARTIAL: symbol_exchange grounds and
exchanges symbols both ways at the word level over a genuinely lived vocabulary;
grammar induction and the production half beyond words are absent - WATCH-6 already
stands at the expected routing point (the PrologAI language pack's
comprehension-production pair).

The reinforcement-learning family as regulation (Q-learning, MDP variants,
homeostatic RL). PERFORMABLE in the variant the test-bed itself endorses:
homeostatic reinforcement - intrinsic reward from drive reduction - is exactly
plasticity_engine's reward-modulated three-factor rule fed by drive_system, chosen
over external critics. Hierarchical RL and options await composite-action
structure.

SECTION TWO - THE DESIGN-PATTERN AND MODULE CATALOGUES
================================================================================

The test-bed's pattern language (the fourteen classic patterns, the seven new ones,
and the thirteen-module reference architecture) was judged pattern by pattern; the
tally, with the missing halves named:

PERFORMABLE: Observation (the observer's complete trace is the historical record);
Mind-Body (the honest body seam is this pattern by design); Reaction (never-invent
selection with vitality preemption); Motivation (drives, urges, prioritized
objectives - near-verbatim); Coordination (the single-purpose inference packs over
the shared graph); Coping (appraisal-driven goal management - among the closest
fits); Regulation in its priority-modification form (the correlator is
prediction_loop, the regulator is the three-factor rule); Simulation in its core
(reality-tagged offline rehearsal with action inhibited); the agent-framework
roster (the mechanism-per-pack blackboard discipline IS the arbitrated federation
in another vocabulary); the drive-and-goal module; the action/motor module; the
emotion-and-appraisal module (appraise, stamp, modulate diffusely - the bus is the
diffuse channel asked for).

PARTIAL, missing half named: Deliberation (selection yes; the path-composing solver
is the planning gap); Compensation (detection yes; inverse-action synthesis needs
the proposer); Exploration (consequences learned; no intrinsic curiosity bias -
the epistemic drive is a small pack-in-waiting, the learning-progress statistic its
one new computation); Discovery (hypothesis-and-test as records; no self-designed
experiments); Reflection (monitor yes; modifier deliberately unbuilt); the
sensorium, semantic-memory, procedural-skill, world-model, planner, and learning
modules (each present in embryo, each missing the same three organs below); the
somatic-marker pattern (the STAMPING half is performable today - attach the
prevailing valence and arousal to outcomes as they close, so that when episodic
memory arrives its retrieval bias finds history waiting - a cheap win worth a
future slice's side-carry); the reflective triad (the safety governor is
substantially PRESENT: override_controller in the runtime, the Safety Gate, the
owner-authorization boundary, and the append-only ledger outside it; metacognitive
strategy-switching absent).

COVERAGE BUGS (the recurring three, named once): Reminding and the episodic-memory
module (bug one); Consolidation - compressor, automator, amnestor - and the
learning module's interleaved replay (bug two); Working memory, the attention
arbiter, and the global broadcast (bug three).


================================================================================
SECTION THREE - THE COVERAGE BUG LIST (per the commandment: unachievable means bug)
================================================================================

Recorded here and mirrored in the ledger. None is routed to a change order today:
the armchair rule holds, the WATCH entries stand at the likeliest routing points,
and each bug already has a named closer among the Vision-Set slice families -
which is itself the survey's strongest result: the test-bed demands nothing the
biology has not already demanded.

BUG ONE - EPISODIC MEMORY AND REMINDING. konnectome cannot be reminded: no episode
segmentation, no bound what-where-when record, no retrieval by fragment. Named by
every source (reminding pattern, CHEF, Daydreamer, LIDA, Holland, viewpoints).
Closer: the memory work of the Vision slice families; WATCH-2 (the episodic event
index) guards the likeliest genuine causalontology wall in the project.

BUG TWO - THE OFFLINE CONSOLIDATION CYCLE. No compressor, automator, or forgetter
runs; memory only accumulates. Named independently by all three test-bed lineages
AND by the Vision Set's two-shift principle - four sources on one gap, the survey's
loudest convergence. The parts are staged (eligibility traces, homeostatic scaling,
the observer's record); slice family one is the closer; WATCH-3 guards consolidation
provenance. Where the test-bed runs consolidation always-on inside the waking loop,
the blueprint demands the distinct offline phase with interleaved replay; the
biology leads.

BUG THREE - THE ATTENTION, WORKING-MEMORY, WORKSPACE CLUSTER. No salience arbiter,
no capacity-limited blackboard, no content broadcast with ignition. Named by ACT-R's
buffers, IDA/LIDA, Shanahan, the motivation pattern's attention module, the
simulation supervisor, and the exploration bias alike. Closers: the working-memory
blackboard pack and the workspace pilot (Vision theme five); WATCH-4 guards the
ignition record. The blueprint's constraint stands: the arbiter must be emergent
winner-take-all, never a ruler.

BUG FOUR - PLAN SYNTHESIS AS A PROPOSER. Beyond the slice-21 breadth-first
means-end trial, no multi-step plan construction, repair, or case reuse exists.
Bounded by the constitution: any solver enters as a proposer whose candidates the
never-invent selector arbitrates. Closer: the selector's winner-take-all deepening
plus simulation chaining (Vision theme four), then a proposer slice if building
demands more.

BUG FIVE - PERCEPTUAL FEATURE LEARNING. No detectors, no spatial schemas, no maps;
perception is the minimal honest seam. Deferred to the Rung Five frontier, where
building against a real (or Webots-simulated) sensor stream will discover honestly
whether the language or the data structure lacks anything - the one bug plausibly
carrying a future change-order routing.

BUG SIX - LANGUAGE PRODUCTION BEYOND THE WORD. Grammar and the production half.
WATCH-6 stands at the expected routing point (the reused language pack's
comprehension-production pair). Closer: the Rung Two deepening against the Layer 13
tutor protocol.

BUG SEVEN (SMALL) - CONTRADICTION CONTEXTS. Locally coherent, mutually
contradictory belief sets (micro-theories) have no home; the attitude kind
quarantines per holder and pretend_play quarantines one as-if world, but no general
viewpoint mechanism exists. Recorded; a realities/viewpoints refinement is the
closer if building hits it.

RECORDED DIVERGENCES (biology leads; not bugs): runtime self-programming and
structural self-modification (AERA, the seed pattern, genetic behavior operators)
against the never-invent invariant and Layer 15's gated evolution; the single
always-on uniform memory against the two-shift principle and the
working-memory-as-activity doctrine; budget-driven anytime inference (AIKR) against
the dumb scheduler's ratified fairness; the runtime reconfigurer and the
broadcaster-ruler against the no-homunculus federation; the oracle substrates
(LLM, corpus QA, adversarial search) against the developmental path; command
hierarchies (RCS) against the arbitrated federation; uniformity-first (SOAR,
LeCun) against diversity-first.

IMPORTABLE IDEAS WORTH A FUTURE SLICE'S MARGIN: the SOAR impasse as a bus-published
disturbance record (feeds curiosity, cheap, in-frame); the somatic-marker stamping
half now, so episodic retrieval bias will find history waiting; the epistemic drive
with a learning-progress statistic (error decrease, not raw error, so noise bores);
the test-bed's unified plan/gap/goal/competence vocabulary if planner and reasoner
ever land in one slice family; and its "competence is understanding" framing, ready
for the capstone narrative.


================================================================================
SECTION FOUR - THE TALLY AND THE HONEST CONCLUSION
================================================================================

Across the four sources, roughly sixty distinct architectures, patterns, and modules
were catalogued and judged. The shape of the result: konnectome PERFORMS or exhibits
EMERGENTLY the motivation, emotion, regulation, coping, reaction, observation,
social-cognition, and homeostatic-learning families nearly in full - the
drive/appraisal/plasticity/bus quartet is the best-covered territory, and PSI, the
test-bed's own Piagetian lineage, behavior-oriented design, and homeostatic
reinforcement learning are substantially performable today. The distance to the
rest of the catalogue concentrates in the SAME three organs the Eighth Commandment
analysis already named absent - episodic memory, the offline consolidation cycle,
and the attention/working-memory/workspace cluster - plus the bounded planner and
the deferred perceptual frontier. Closing bugs one through three would flip the
verdict on roughly a dozen catalogue entries at once.

The survey's governing finding, stated so the next builder can stand on it: THE
TEST-BED DEMANDS NOTHING THE BIOLOGY HAS NOT ALREADY DEMANDED. Every coverage bug
maps onto a Vision-Set refinement theme that predates this survey; two independent
literatures, read months apart by different sessions, converge on one gap list. The
frame held against the atlas; it holds against the test-bed too. And where the
test-bed and the biology disagree - invention at runtime, memory without sleep,
budgets against fairness, rulers against federations - the recorded answer is the
commandment's own: the biology leads.

No kludge was made this survey, so no kludge document exists yet; the obligation is
noted for whenever one first is.
