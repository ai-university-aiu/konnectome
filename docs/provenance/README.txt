Created 2026-08-10. Constitution version 16.


WHAT IS IN HERE, IN ONE SENTENCE

The record of the programme that ran BEFORE konnectome and produced the foundation konnectome was built on - five documents konnectome did not write, describing work konnectome did not do, kept because konnectome stands on the result.


WHY THIS IS A THIRD KIND OF DOCUMENT

This repository already sorts documents two ways. /docs/archive/ holds SUPERSEDED VERSIONS - dead drafts of documents that still exist in better form. The top level of /docs/ holds LIVE WORKING DOCUMENTS - the ledger, the tutorial, the design authorities, the current hand-off.

THESE FIVE ARE NEITHER.

They are not superseded versions: not one of them has a successor that replaced it. Connectome_Master_Plan_v2 has no v3. The close-out report has no v2. Putting them in /docs/archive/ would say they were superseded, and they were not - the programme they belong to FINISHED.

They are not live working documents either: no rule, no slice, and no hand-off instructs anyone to read them, and none should, until somebody needs to know WHY the foundation is shaped as it is.

THEY ARE INHERITED RECORD. That is the third kind, and this directory is what it looks like on disk.


THE PROGRAMME, IN ONE PARAGRAPH, SO NOBODY HAS TO RECONSTRUCT IT

Before konnectome there was a TEN-WAVE PROGRAMME whose subject was PrologAI itself. Its purpose was stated bluntly in its own plan: it was NOT an attempt to build an Artificial General Intelligence. It was AN INSTRUMENT FOR DISCOVERING REQUIREMENTS - what functions, faculties, primitives and packs PrologAI still lacked in order to be a complete programming language for building any brain region, interface, neurotransmitter or hormone described in Nature's Cognitive Architecture.

Its deliverable was never code. It was THE PROLOGAI REQUIREMENTS LEDGER. The plan says so in its own words: a repository that fails to build but yields precise, well-argued requirements is a SUCCESS, and a repository that builds beautifully and teaches us nothing about PrologAI's gaps is a FAILURE, however pretty it is.

Waves 0 through 9 DISCOVERED what PrologAI lacked. Wave 10 IMPLEMENTED the whole Ledger back into the language. And then the programme closed, on a boundary its own close-out report states exactly: when Wave 10's nine stages are merged green, PrologAI rests on Causalontology, and the two together are the foundation for THE NEXT REPOSITORY, KONNECTOME.

THAT SENTENCE IS THE WHOLE REASON THIS DIRECTORY EXISTS. konnectome is not a member of that programme. It is what the programme was clearing the ground for.


THE FIVE DOCUMENTS

• Connectome_Master_Plan_v2.txt - THE PROGRAMME'S PLAN. Twelve parts. States the purpose, the six planned repositories, the sequencing, and the decisions taken along the way - stigmergy for loop closure, PrologAI over Python, Causalontology as the grounding rule. Version 1 is in docs/archive/ , moved there by the close-out sweep, which is the one part of this cluster that IS a superseded version.

• PROJECT_CLOSE_OUT_QA_REPORT_v1.txt - THE CLOSING SCOREBOARD. The final quality-assurance pass over Wave 10, over Waves 1 through 10 as a set, and over every project document. It is the document that draws the boundary between the programme and konnectome, and it is the one to read first if you only read one.

• WRITEUP_FOR_WAVE_1_TO_WAVE_10.txt - the narrative of the ten waves.

• PrologAI_Requirements_Ledger_v1.txt - THE PROGRAMME'S ACTUAL DELIVERABLE, and the most consequential file in here. THE FOURTH COMMANDMENT NAMES IT BY NAME as the model konnectome's own ledger was built on: konnectome's ledger is "the running scoreboard of the build, exactly as PrologAI_Requirements_Ledger_v1.txt was for the language". If you want to know why konnectome records every wall it hits before routing it anywhere, this is the document that taught it to.

• CAUSALONTOLOGY_3_0_0_REVIEW_AND_4_0_0_ASSESSMENT.txt - the review of the data structure at 3.0.0 and the assessment that became the 4.0.0 change order. Cited by docs/Causalontology_4_0_0_CHANGE_ORDER_v3.txt as the source of its four candidate gaps, which makes it the one document in here with a live descendant still in use.


THE DORMANT SIBLING REPOSITORIES - RECORDED HERE BECAUSE NOTHING ELSE RECORDS THEM

The Master Plan names six repositories. EIGHT EXIST on the local file system, all under the ai-university-aiu organisation: connectome-amygdala, connectome-arbiter, connectome-atomic, connectome-cerebellum, connectome-hippocampus, connectome-loops, connectome-proto-agi, and connectome-strata. Three of those eight - the amygdala, the cerebellum and the hippocampus - are not in the plan's list of six, so the family grew past its own plan before it stopped.

ALL EIGHT ARE DORMANT. Each carries between three and eight commits, and every one of them stops at the same commit on the same day: "Adopt Causalontology 3.0.0", 2026-07-22. konnectome has run forty-seven slices since.

THIS IS WRITTEN DOWN BECAUSE NOTHING IN KONNECTOME MENTIONS THEM. The Constitution names causalontology, PrologAI and Mentova as the cousin repositories and is silent on these eight. Forty-seven slices passed without a word about them. That is not necessarily wrong - the programme closed, and konnectome is what replaced it - BUT AN UNRECORDED INHERITANCE IS THE KIND OF THING THAT GETS REDISCOVERED EXPENSIVELY. If any of the eight holds a Ledger entry, a design decision, or a measurement that konnectome has since re-derived or is about to, nobody currently has a way to know.

WHAT NOBODY HAS DONE, STATED SO IT IS A GAP AND NOT AN OVERSIGHT: nobody has read those eight repositories against konnectome to see whether anything in them is a finding konnectome does not have. That is a real piece of work, it is nobody's current task, and it is recorded here rather than in the queue because it is provenance archaeology rather than a build slice.
