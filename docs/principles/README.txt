WHAT IS IN HERE

The three documents the FIRST COMMANDMENT names, in the order it names them.

• THE_ROADMAP_TO_AGI_AND_ASI_MANUSCRIPT.txt

• AGI_FOR_EVERYONE_OUTLINE.txt

• AGI_FOR_EVERYONE_MANUSCRIPT_v2.txt

The Commandment's own sentence is the whole reason they are together: konnectome "will be built and maintained with the principles of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI) in mind, as specified in the following three (3) documents".


WHAT THIS DIRECTORY IS NOT, WHICH IS HOW TO TELL IT FROM ITS THREE NEIGHBOURS

/docs/ now holds five sub-directories that are easy to confuse if the distinctions are not stated, so they are stated here.

• NOT docs/archive/ . That holds SUPERSEDED VERSIONS - dead drafts of documents that still exist in better form. These three are current. Note that AGI_FOR_EVERYONE_MANUSCRIPT.txt, the predecessor of the _v2 in here, IS in docs/archive/ , correctly, and did not follow its successor into this directory. THE ELEVENTH COMMANDMENT NAMES EXACTLY ONE ARCHIVE and there is no docs/principles/archive/ .

• NOT docs/provenance/ . That holds the record of a CLOSED programme that nobody is instructed to read. These three are LIVE GUIDANCE: the First Commandment is a standing instruction to build with their principles in mind. The version 14 test separates them in one question - would a session ever be told to read this file? For provenance the answer is no. For these it is yes.

• NOT docs/neuroscience/ . That directory's own README states that its boundary is AUTHORSHIP AND NOT SUBJECT. These three pass the authorship half - konnectome did not write them - and fail the subject half, because they are about Artificial General Intelligence and not about the nervous system. Putting them there would have made that directory's name a lie.

• NOT the top level of /docs/ , which is now reserved for konnectome's OWN live working documents: the ledger, the tutorial, the build log, the design authorities, the analysis, the change orders, and the current hand-off. These three are inherited.

SO THE CATEGORY IS: INHERITED, LIVE, AND NOT NEUROSCIENCE. That combination had no home until this directory, and docs/neuroscience/ is its nearest sibling - same class, different subject.


WHY THE DIRECTORY IS CALLED principles

It is the First Commandment's own word: konnectome is built with the PRINCIPLES of AGI and ASI in mind.

Two alternatives were considered and rejected for stated reasons.

• docs/agi/ would put a bare acronym where the FOURTEENTH Commandment requires whole words. konnectome is a Whole-Word System, not an abbreviation system.

• docs/vision/ would collide head-on with "the Vision Document Set", which is the Constitution's term for the NEUROSCIENCE corpus next door. Two different sets under one word is precisely the naming collision the mode-register design authority spent a page resolving, and repeating it in a directory name would have been careless.


HOW THESE THREE RELATE TO EACH OTHER

The OUTLINE and the MANUSCRIPT are a pair: the manuscript follows the outline, part for part. The manuscript is at version 2 - a strict superset of its predecessor, expanded roughly four and a half fold, carrying an appendix the earlier one did not have, with 868 of the predecessor's 869 substantive lines appearing VERBATIM. That was CHECKED rather than assumed before the Constitution's pointer was moved to it at version 9, and the check is recorded in the Commandment itself.

The ROADMAP is separate and is not part of that pair.


THE STANDING RULE ABOUT THESE FILES

They are SOURCE. konnectome reads them, is guided by them, and never edits them - the same discipline as docs/neuroscience/ .

AND ONE HONEST LIMIT, RECORDED IN THE CONSTITUTION AND REPEATED HERE. Being named by the First Commandment does not make a claim inside these documents verified. The Commandment says konnectome is built with their PRINCIPLES IN MIND; it does not say every sentence in them is true. A bigger book is a bigger source, not a checked one.
