HOW TO READ THIS OUTLINE

This outline records the complete heading structure of the manuscript, from front matter through the closing experiments.

Level 1 is the PART, of which there are six, numbered in words from ONE to SIX, plus the front matter that precedes Part One.

Level 2 is the CHAPTER, of which there are forty-five, numbered continuously from 1 to 45 across the whole work, together with the Author's Note, the Foreword, and the Introduction.

Level 3 is the SECTION, shown as a bullet line beginning with the solid bullet character.

Level 4 is the SUB-SECTION, shown as a bullet line beginning with the hollow bullet character, and appears mainly inside the experiment chapters.

Definitions of the four core terms as the manuscript uses them:

Natural Intelligence (NI) means any form of intelligence that has arisen through biological evolution and development in living organisms.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) means computational systems designed to perform tasks that require intelligence when performed by humans.

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) means a hypothetical AI system capable of performing any cognitive task that a human can perform at a comparable or superior level.

Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI) means a hypothetical AI system whose cognitive capabilities exceed human performance across every domain of relevance.

The book has two halves.

Chapters 1 through 25, organized as Parts One through Five, form the book proper: the study of natural intelligence, the gap analysis against current AI, the research agenda, the governance implications, and the research resources.

Chapters 26 through 45, organized as Part Six, are twenty proposed scientific experiments, each submitted for peer review consideration and each following the same eight-section scientific skeleton: Abstract, Introduction and Motivation, Research Questions, Hypotheses, Methodology, Controls and Confounds, Limitations, Significance and Expected Contributions, and References.
