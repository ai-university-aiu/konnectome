FULFILMENT AUDIT OF THE VISION-SET TOP-DOWN ANALYSIS

The audit commissioned by the owner on 2026-08-09 and recorded in the SCHEDULED AUDIT note of docs/admin/konnectome_ledger.txt, which is its authoritative specification. Run on 2026-08-11, at slice 70, main 66236c9.

Version 1. The first time this audit has been run.

THE QUESTION IT ANSWERS, in the commissioning note's own words: whether docs/archive/konnectome_vision_set_top_down_analysis_v1.txt WAS FULFILLED - "that is, whether the findings konnectome recorded when it read the corpus were actually acted upon, or whether they were written down and then quietly bypassed."

THE GRADING STANDARD IS THE COMMISSIONING NOTE'S AND WAS DECIDED IN ADVANCE, which is the whole reason it can be trusted here: "A theme, watch, or family that was DELIBERATELY DEFERRED WITH A RECORDED REASON counts as FULFILLED - the analysis asked for a decision and a decision was made. A theme, watch, or family that was SILENTLY BYPASSED counts as NOT FULFILLED, and the absence of a recorded reason is itself the finding." NOTE WHAT THIS STANDARD DOES NOT ASK. It does not ask whether a theme was BUILT. An unbuilt theme with a recorded reason passes; a built theme is not thereby exempt from having its reasons recorded. THE SUBJECT IS THE RECORD, NOT THE PROGRESS.

WHERE THIS DOCUMENT LIVES, AND WHY IT IS NOT WHERE THE COMMISSION SUGGESTED. The commissioning note suggested the name docs/konnectome_vision_set_fulfilment_audit_v1.txt, and that suggestion is now unfollowable: the top level of /docs/ was later restricted to exactly four files - the ledger, the build log, the tutorial, and the current hand-off - and the design documents were numbered into docs/design/ . The suggestion was explicitly a SUGGESTION and not a requirement, and the audit follows the live convention instead.

IT IS NUMBERED 11 AND ITS READING POSITION IS BESIDE 03. The directory's numbers are its reading order, and by that rule this document belongs immediately after 03, the fidelity audit, because the two are the same kind of thing - quality control on 01, from two different angles. INSERTING IT THERE WOULD RENUMBER SEVEN DOCUMENTS AND REQUIRE A POINTER SWEEP ACROSS THE LEDGER, THE HAND-OFF CHAIN AND THE DESIGN DOCUMENTS THEMSELVES, and that is a larger and riskier act than this audit should perform in passing - which is the same reasoning that keeps Finding One's repair out of this document. THE RENUMBERING IS THEREFORE DECLINED, WITH ITS REASON RECORDED HERE AND IN THE DIRECTORY'S README, which is what the directory's own rule asks for: "THE NUMBERS ARE A JUDGEMENT AND NOT A LAW ... if the queue turns, they should be renumbered rather than quietly left to lie." This audit's judgement is that the queue has not turned; only a document has been added.

METHOD. Every claim below was traced to the ledger, the build log, the design authorities, or the running repository, and each is cited to the slice that produced it. Where the audit could not find evidence it says NO EVIDENCE FOUND rather than inferring. Counts quoted as "today" were taken from the live repository and not from any document that describes it.

WHAT HAPPENED. The north-star gap analysis, written at slice 51, escalated the astrocyte from a tension to a specific architectural gap as its finding A4, and attached the corpus counter-evidence that the earlier treatment had never had: that the protoplasmic astrocyte "fires no action potentials whatsoever" and contacts two million synapses "without firing a single spike"; a runnable leaky-integrator model with a threshold and a reset; and, decisively, that its D-serine supply "GATES WHETHER NEARBY SYNAPSES CAN UNDERGO LONG-TERM POTENTIATION AT ALL". Its conclusion: the corpus demands a node with genuine continuous state, a genuine threshold, and a mode register whose publication is a PLASTICITY PERMISSION OVER OTHER EDGES, and "nothing in konnectome and nothing in either design authority provides for it". That is a different claim from "a tension", and it is a much more expensive one, because a permission over other edges is not a modulatory entry.

AND THE DOCUMENT THAT OWNS THE PRESSURE NEVER RECEIVED IT, WHILE STATING THAT IT HAD. Version 4 says it "READ THE NORTH-STAR ANALYSIS IN FULL and reconciled its findings against this document's". A4 is not cited anywhere in version 4, and version 4's astrocyte paragraph is a two-line restatement of version 1's with no reference to any of the counter-evidence. A4 is also absent from BOTH standing queues. So the escalation exists, in writing, in a document written by this build, and the only place it is not is the place a reader would look for it.

WHY THIS IS GRADED AS A FINDING RATHER THAN AS A DIFFERENCE OF OPINION. The grading standard turns on whether a reason was recorded. NO REASON WAS RECORDED - not a note that A4 was considered and judged not to change the tension, not a deferral, not a disagreement. There is simply a claim of full reconciliation and one finding that was not reconciled. A deliberate refusal of A4, written down, would have been fulfilment. Silence is not.

THE NUMBERING CROSS-REFERENCE - NOT FULFILLED. THIS IS THE AUDIT'S PRINCIPAL FINDING AND IT IS RECORDED IN FULL AS FINDING ONE BELOW.
