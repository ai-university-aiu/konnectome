PART TWO - THE EDGE TYPE SCHEMA, AS THE CORPUS ACTUALLY WRITES IT

This part is a SUMMARY and loses to the corpus.

THE UNIT. A junction type is defined at docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_SYNAPTICS_OUTLINE.txt line 16: "a synaptic type is a recurring junctional design - a characteristic combination of messenger, machinery, geometry, and partners - just as a Layer 8 motif is a recurring wiring diagram."

THE COMPOSITION RULE, which is the schema's most consequential single sentence and the one konnectome is least prepared for, is at docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_SYNAPTICS_OUTLINE.txt line 18: "One physical synapse in the tissue is typically several of the entries below at once: a single cortical excitatory contact is a glutamatergic synapse (Part One) on a dendritic spine (Part Two) wrapped by an astrocyte into a tripartite ensemble (Part Seven)."

A real edge therefore carries a SET of types, one per axis, not a single type token.

THE ORGANISING AXES. There are EIGHT, and they are the eight parts of both the outline and the manuscript.

• Axis one, MESSENGER SYSTEM (Part One, entries 1 to 16) - sixteen values.

• Axis two, GEOMETRY AND TARGET (Part Two, entries 17 to 25) - nine values.

• Axis three, NAMED GIANT DESIGN (Part Three, entries 26 to 37) - twelve values.

• Axis four, ELECTRICAL AND MIXED COUPLING (Part Four, entries 38 to 42) - five values.

• Axis five, LIFE-CYCLE AND PLASTICITY STANDING (Part Five, entries 43 to 45) - three values.

• Axis six, NEUROEFFECTOR AND NEUROSECRETORY ROLE (Part Six, entries 46 to 52) - seven values.

• Axis seven, GLIAL AND NON-NEURONAL PARTNERSHIP (Part Seven, entries 53 to 57) - five values.

• Axis eight, ENDOCRINE JUNCTIONAL EQUIVALENT (Part Eight, entries 58 to 64) - seven values.

Sixteen plus nine plus twelve plus five plus three plus seven plus five plus seven is sixty-four.

Laid ACROSS all eight axes, not as a ninth axis, is the COGNITIVE TAG: two values, (COG) and (NON-COG), twenty-seven and thirty-seven respectively.

THE PER-TYPE FIELDS. Every one of the sixty-four chapters runs "twenty-three fixed aspects" (docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_SYNAPTICS_MANUSCRIPT.txt, introduction, the paragraph beginning "Each chapter runs through twenty-three fixed aspects").

The blocks are, in the manuscript's own order and capitalisation: WHAT IT IS AND WHAT IT TRANSMITS; THE JUNCTIONAL ARCHITECTURE; THE MOLECULAR MACHINERY; THE PARTNERS (THE LAYER 6 CAST); SIZE AND SCALE; NUMBER AND DISTRIBUTION; THE TRANSFER FUNCTION; TEMPORAL SIGNATURE; SHORT-TERM DYNAMICS; LONG-TERM PLASTICITY; RELIABILITY AND NOISE; ENERGY BUDGET; DEVELOPMENT AND ASSEMBLY; EVOLUTIONARY PROVENANCE; SUPPORT SYSTEM; HOW IT COLOURS ITS MICROCIRCUITS; DISEASES AND DISORDERS; SYSTEMS INTERFACE; THE KONNECTOME NOTE; ENGLISH READABLE CODE; CODE SAMPLE; WHAT MAKES IT UNMISTAKABLE.

Of those, EIGHT are load-bearing for an edge type registry and are stated here field by field.

FIELD ONE, THE TYPE NAME. Every chapter's KONNECTOME NOTE opens by proposing a connection-type name in snake case.

Chapter 1 proposes fast_excitatory_learning_connection (docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_SYNAPTICS_MANUSCRIPT.txt line 256).

Chapter 4 proposes fast_shunting_inhibitory_connection (line 938).

Chapter 26 proposes motor_effector_relay_connection (line 5706).

The sixty-four notes do NOT yield sixty-four distinct names: electrical_coupling_connection is proposed at Chapter 38 (line 8247) and re-proposed by name at Chapter 58 (line 11778) and Chapter 59 (line 11941), and hormone_broadcast_connection is proposed at both Chapter 48 (line 9912) and Chapter 64 (line 12762).

Chapter 19 proposes TWO names in one note - branch_gating_inhibitory_connection "with a sibling fast_interneuron_drive_connection for the excitatory-onto-aspiny form" (line 4161).

FIELD TWO, SIGN DISCIPLINE. Written in THE TRANSFER FUNCTION block, and it takes more than two values.

Chapter 1: "Sign positive" (line 156).

Chapter 4: "Sign negative in the adult", and then, critically, "The character is therefore DIVISIVE as much as subtractive: with little driving force the synapse adds conductance rather than current, dividing the neuron's response to simultaneous excitation - shunting inhibition - while at depolarised potentials the same synapse pulls genuinely down" (lines 840 and 842).

Chapter 42, the ephaptic coupling site, is signed but sign-INVERTING with zero delay and zero storage (docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_SYNAPTICS_MANUSCRIPT.txt line 8905, and docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_OUTLINE.txt line 354).

Chapter 38, the electrical synapse, is SIGNLESS and SYMMETRIC: "a continuous scaled copy of the sender's voltage rather than a discrete event, delivered symmetrically to both parties" (line 8247).

So sign discipline reads at least: positive; negative-subtractive; negative-divisive (shunting); sign-inverting field; symmetric and signless.

Chapter 4 adds a further sentence konnectome has no field for at all: "Placement completes the arithmetic: somatic and initial segment contacts divide the output, dendritic contacts divide a single branch's input" (line 844).

FIELD THREE, FIXED-OR-LEARNABLE. Written in the LONG-TERM PLASTICITY block, and the corpus states the negative case as an ASSERTION, not an omission.

Chapter 26: "This junction is built not to learn, and says so proudly: its adult transfer function is fixed at suprathreshold, because a motor exit that adjusted its weight would make movement unrepeatable" (line 5634, the LONG-TERM PLASTICITY block).

Its SYSTEMS INTERFACE STATE block says: "no plastic weight - the weight is fixed above threshold by design" (line 5690).

Its KONNECTOME NOTE says: "a message carries a spike timestamp and the guarantee of execution, WITH NO ADJUSTABLE WEIGHT FIELD" (line 5706).

FIELD FOUR, THE LEARNING RULE IDENTITY AND ITS TARGET VARIABLE. Distinct laws named in the chapters read: NMDA-receptor-dependent long-term potentiation and depression with a spike-timing window of about twenty milliseconds, pre leading post (Chapter 1, LONG-TERM PLASTICITY); presynaptic potentiation stored as RELEASE PROBABILITY rather than as a postsynaptic weight (Chapter 2, docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_MANUSCRIPT.txt Entry 2, "Potentiated presynaptic maintenance - The Raised-Release Weight"); supervised error-gated depression with an external teacher (Chapter 30's supervised_error_broadcast_connection, whose note says its "delivery triggers weight updates on OTHER connections rather than adjusting its own", line 6398, paired with Chapter 31's supervised_weight_matrix_connection, "a separate supervised_error_broadcast_connection (chapter 30) is AUTHORISED to update", line 6610); retrograde depression (Chapter 15); conductance plasticity on an electrical edge, "a trainable averaging coefficient" (Chapter 38, line 8249); homeostatic inhibitory plasticity, where "inhibition learns to match local excitation" (Chapter 4, line 862); and DELAY as the learned variable (Chapter 55, line 11156).

FIELD FIVE, THE TIME-CONSTANT PROFILE. Written twice - narratively in TEMPORAL SIGNATURE and as a structured list under SYSTEMS INTERFACE, bullet TIME CONSTANTS.

Chapter 1's is: "AMPA decay 1 to 3 milliseconds; NMDA decay 50 to 200 milliseconds; facilitation 50 to 300 milliseconds; depression recovery 0.5 to 1 second; long-term potentiation expression minutes to years" (line 248).

The profile is therefore a NAMED SET of constants spanning nine orders of magnitude, not one number.

FIELD SIX, SHORT-TERM DYNAMICS. A separate block from long-term plasticity, and the corpus is emphatic that it is not a mode change but a within-mode state.

Chapter 1: "Both facilitation and depression occur, SET BY RELEASE PROBABILITY: high-probability contacts (probability around 0.5 to 0.9) depress ... while low-probability contacts (around 0.1 to 0.3) facilitate as residual calcium accumulates" (line 172).

Chapter 26 rules on the boundary directly: "Depression and post-tetanic potentiation are WITHIN-MODE dynamics absorbed by the margin, not mode changes" (docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_MANUSCRIPT.txt Entry 26, THE TRANSITION TABLE).

FIELD SEVEN, THE SYSTEMS INTERFACE, which is the corpus's own machine-readable block and the closest thing in the whole corpus to a konnectome pack declaration.

Six sub-blocks, identical in every chapter: SUBSCRIBES (INPUTS); PUBLISHES (OUTPUTS); STATE; PARAMETERS; TIME CONSTANTS; FAILURE MODE.

The STATE and PARAMETERS split is the corpus's own answer to what belongs to the edge instance and what belongs to the edge type.

FIELD EIGHT, THE ENDPOINTS. THE PARTNERS (THE LAYER 6 CAST) names what may stand on each side, and it is not always two neurons.

Chapter 53's note proposes "a slow edge from an astrocyte node ONTO A SYNAPTIC EDGE" (line 10694).

Chapter 57's proposes "a transient edge from a microglial node ONTO ANY SYNAPTIC EDGE, whose message carries an audit score and whose rare payload is DELETION of the audited edge" (line 11528).

Chapter 62's proposes self_feedback_connection, "an edge from a node back to itself" (line 12428).

Chapter 64's proposes a one-to-many edge whose cleft is the bloodstream (line 12762).

So the endpoint field's value space is at minimum: node-to-node; node-to-self; node-to-edge; node-to-many-nodes.


PART THREE - TYPE VERSUS MODE AT THE JUNCTION GRAIN

This part is a FINDING.

THE PRECISE RELATIONSHIP, IN ONE SENTENCE: at the junction grain the TYPE is the fixed identity of a relation and the MODE REGISTER is one of the type's fields, drawn per type as a subset of a shared, corpus-wide mode vocabulary, and it is the TYPE - not the mode - that decides whether an update rule exists at all.

The evidence for each clause.

FIRST, THE REGISTER IS A FIELD OF THE TYPE, AND IT IS KEYED TO THE TYPE. Every one of the sixty-four entries of docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_OUTLINE.txt is stamped "keyed to synaptics catalogue Chapter N", and the modes manuscript introduction says each entry is "PASTE-READY: formatted exactly as a MODES AND MODE TRANSITIONS section for its source chapter ... so a future merge into the synaptics catalogue is a mechanical splice."

The mode register is literally a twenty-fourth aspect block waiting to be spliced into the type's chapter.

SECOND, THE VOCABULARY IS SHARED AND THE SUBSET IS PER TYPE. The modes manuscript introduction gives "The universal menu for junctions, FROM WHICH EACH TYPE DRAWS ITS OWN SUBSET", and lists ten oppositions: silent versus functional; facilitating versus depressing; potentiated, baseline and depressed; evoked synchronous, asynchronous and spontaneous miniature release; kiss-and-run versus full-collapse; tonic versus phasic; open, closed and rectifying; coupled versus uncoupled; intact versus retracting; safety-factor versus fatigued.

THIRD, THE REGISTER LENGTH IS DETERMINED BY THE TYPE'S LEARNING STANDING. The modes manuscript states it outright: "the learning junctions are mode-rich and the fidelity junctions are mode-poor."

The measured distribution over the sixty-four entries of docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_OUTLINE.txt is: four registers of one, fourteen of two, thirty-one of three, eight of four, seven of five.

None of zero, none of six or more.

The four registers of one are Entries 26, 27, 28 and 42 - the neuromuscular junction, the calyx of Held, the endbulb of Held, and the ephaptic coupling site - and three of the four say "A register of one, stated proudly."

All four are (NON-COG).

FOURTH, AND THIS IS THE CLAUSE THE PARENT SESSION MOST NEEDS: THE JUNCTION REGISTER IS NOT A FLAT LIST. It is a PRODUCT OF ORTHOGONAL SUB-REGISTERS, and the corpus says so in its very first entry.

docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_MANUSCRIPT.txt, Entry 1: "This is the mode-richest junction in the catalogue, a learning contact that occupies several discrete operating regimes AT ONCE - a silent-versus-functional axis, a facilitating-versus-depressing short-term axis, and a potentiated-versus-baseline-versus-depressed maintenance axis."

The five listed register entries are therefore a FLATTENED PRESENTATION of a two-by-three-plus-silent structure, not five mutually exclusive states.

The Theme One design authority already noticed the general shape and left it open as its unresolved point seven (/home/ccaitwo/konnectome/docs/design/05_mode_register_design_authority_v1.txt line 185: "WHETHER CONCURRENT MODES ARE A GENERAL FACILITY OR AN EXCEPTION ... coexisting-by-geometry registers exist at the junction grain, but the corpus neither counts them nor marks them syntactically").

The junction grain is where that question stops being theoretical, because Entry 1 is the FIRST entry of the volume and the type konnectome's own edges most resemble.

FIFTH, THE BOUNDARY IS ADMITTED SOFT BY THE CORPUS ITSELF. /home/ccaitwo/konnectome/docs/design/05_mode_register_design_authority_v1.txt line 181 records it, and the deep read confirms it from the source: docs/neuroscience/LAYER_07_MODES_OUTLINE.txt Entry 43 says the silent synapse is a "cross-reference Chapter 1, OF WHICH THIS IS THE AMPA-LACKING MODE", and Entry 17, the axospinous synapse, lists "Silent - the Mute Room ... (catalogued fully at entry 43)."

So Chapter 43 is simultaneously a TYPE (Part Five, entry 43, with its own KONNECTOME NOTE proposing silent_eligible_connection) and a MODE of Chapter 1 and Chapter 17.

The corpus will not decide which, and says so by cataloguing it both ways.

THE ORDERING CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS THE SAME ONE THE THEME ONE READ REACHED, NOW INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED FROM THE LAYER 7 SOURCE.

THE EDGE TYPE REGISTRY MUST LAND BEFORE THE JUNCTION-GRAIN MODE REGISTER.

Three separate reasons, any one of which would be sufficient.

• REASON ONE, THE CONTAINMENT REASON. A junction's register is filed under its type and is drawn as a subset per type. Build the register first and you must invent a container for it; the container IS the type record, so you would build the type registry anyway, unnamed and untested.

• REASON TWO, THE SIZE REASON. The register's LENGTH is a consequence of the type's learning standing - mode-rich if it learns, mode-poor if it must not. konnectome cannot honestly assign a junction a register of one until it can say the edge is a fidelity type, and today it has no field in which to say that.

• REASON THREE, THE AUTHORITY REASON, which is the sharpest. Chapter 26's register of one is derived from its LONG-TERM PLASTICITY assertion, and Chapter 30's teaching edge is "authorised to update" Chapter 31's weights. Authority over a weight is a property of a TYPE PAIR, not of any mode. A mode register cannot express it at all.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT MEAN. It does not mean the mode-register family stalls.

The remaining Theme One slices at the CONSTRUCT grain - the bus throwing a transition, the supervisor channel, the stabiliser, the master register - touch nodes and the global state, not junctions, and are unaffected by this ordering.

The ordering finding is scoped exactly to the JUNCTION-GRAIN register: the point at which konnectome would give an EDGE a mode register is the point at which the edge type registry must already exist.


PART FOUR - WHAT THE CORPUS DOES NOT SETTLE

These are konnectome's OWN decisions. The corpus is genuinely silent or genuinely self-contradictory on each, and the Second Commandment forbids guessing them from the armchair; each is to be decided by the slice that trips over it, and recorded in the ledger when it is.

1. ONE TYPE PER EDGE, OR A SET OF TYPES PER EDGE. The corpus says a real synapse is several entries at once and that the parts are axes rather than bins. konnectome must decide whether interface/5 grows one Type argument or an axis-keyed list. The cheapest honest first slice takes ONE, and the decision must be recorded as a decision, not slipped in as an assumption.

2. WHETHER THE COGNITIVE TAG IS A SCHEMA FIELD AT ALL. It is a role annotation that "changes with the address" and is not one of the eight axes. konnectome must decide whether it stores it, derives it from the learnability field, or refuses it as a corpus editorial device.

3. WHETHER SIGN IS A TYPE FIELD OR REMAINS THE SIGN OF THE WEIGHT. konnectome today has no sign field; sign is whatever the weight's sign happens to be, and nothing forbids a learning rule from flipping an inhibitory edge excitatory. The corpus never permits that. Making sign a type property is a genuine behaviour change and must be pinned carefully.

4. WHETHER DIVISIVE (SHUNTING) TRANSMISSION IS AN EDGE PROPERTY OR A NODE PROPERTY. Chapter 4 puts the division in the junction and then says placement completes the arithmetic. konnectome's total-input fold is additive by construction (connection_graph_accumulate/4). A divisive edge cannot be expressed in that fold at all without deciding where the division applies.

5. WHETHER LEARNABILITY IS A TYPE PROPERTY OR A MODE. Theme One's elevation note calls it a junction-grain mode (/home/ccaitwo/konnectome/docs/archive/konnectome_vision_set_top_down_analysis_v1.txt line 37: "an edge type's fixed-or-learnable property (Theme Three) is a junction-grain mode"). The Layer 7 corpus puts it in LONG-TERM PLASTICITY, a type-level block, and Chapter 26 makes it an assertion about the type. THIS DEEP READ FINDS FOR THE TYPE, and records that this contradicts the analysis's own elevation note, which is a summary and loses.

6. WHETHER SILENT IS A TYPE OR A MODE. The corpus catalogues it both ways and declines to choose. konnectome must choose before the silent-edge life-cycle slice, and the choice determines whether unsilencing is a mode transition or a type change.

7. WHETHER A CONCURRENT (MULTI-AXIS) REGISTER IS A GENERAL FACILITY. Entry 1 requires it; Entry 26 forbids needing it. The corpus never marks it syntactically.

8. WHETHER DELAY IS LEARNABLE. Chapter 55 says one type's learned variable is its own transmission delay. konnectome's delay is an integer tick count in interface/5 and its delay lines are sized from it at construction (connection_graph_delay_lines_new/2), so a learnable delay is a structural change, not a numeric one.

9. WHETHER THE TYPE REGISTRY CARRIES AUTHORITY (WHO MAY UPDATE WHOSE WEIGHT). Chapters 30, 31, 53 and 57 all require it. The corpus names the relation but never gives it a field.

10. HOW MANY TYPE NAMES THERE ARE. The sixty-four KONNECTOME NOTEs yield fewer than sixty-four distinct names, because three chapters share electrical_coupling_connection, two share hormone_broadcast_connection, and one chapter proposes two names. konnectome must decide whether its registry key is the chapter number or the proposed name; they are not one-to-one.

11. WHERE SHORT-TERM DYNAMICS LIVE. Facilitation and depression are within-mode state on a per-EDGE-INSTANCE basis, set by a per-type parameter (release probability). Slice 40 settled the per-kind versus per-instance question for CONSTRUCTS (/home/ccaitwo/konnectome/docs/archive/konnectome_vision_set_top_down_analysis_v1.txt line 48); the same question is open and unsettled for EDGES.


PART FIVE - WHAT KONNECTOME HAS, SCORED HONESTLY

Held against Part Two's eight fields. The score is low, and it is low in an honest way: everything konnectome has is correct as far as it goes, and almost none of the schema is expressed.

THE EDGE TERM. packs/connection_graph/prolog/connection_graph.pl, the header comment above connection_graph_state_get/3: "An interface is the term interface(From, To, Weight, Delay, Kind)."

FIELD ONE, TYPE NAME. ABSENT. There is no type field. Kind takes exactly two values, transmissive and computational, and the discrimination is a single literal comparison in connection_graph_accumulate/4: "( Kind == transmissive -> ... ; Acc = Acc0 )". Computational interfaces are carried in the data and contribute nothing, as the file's own header states.

FIELD TWO, SIGN DISCIPLINE. ABSENT AS A FIELD, PRESENT ONLY AS THE SIGN OF A NUMBER. connection_graph_accumulate/4 computes "Acc is Acc0 + Weight * SourceActivation". There is no sign field, no shunting, no symmetric edge, and the fold is unconditionally additive. connection_graph_total_delivered/3 has the same shape on the delayed path.

FIELD THREE, FIXED-OR-LEARNABLE. PRESENT BY ACCIDENT, NOT BY DECLARATION. packs/plasticity_engine/prolog/plasticity_engine.pl says in its header: "the engine mutates the Weight of transmissive interfaces and leaves computational ones untouched." Learnability is therefore an exact synonym of transmissive, which is also the synonym of conveys-signal. Three distinct corpus properties are collapsed onto one flag. A fidelity edge that transmits but must never learn - Chapter 26, the corpus's proudest single case - CANNOT BE EXPRESSED IN KONNECTOME TODAY. This is the single sharpest gap the deep read found.

FIELD FOUR, LEARNING RULE IDENTITY. ONE RULE, UNNAMED, FOR EVERY EDGE. plasticity_engine_weight_change/5 is the whole of it: Coincidence is Sending * Receiving; RawWeightChange is Coincidence * ThirdFactor; WeightChange is RawWeightChange * LearningRate. The third factor is read at the receiving territory through neuromodulator_bus_level_territory/4. It is a faithful three-factor dopamine-gated Hebbian rule and it corresponds to exactly one corner of the corpus's Chapter 9 dopaminergic case. There is no rule identity field, no target-variable field, and no authority relation.

FIELD FIVE, TIME-CONSTANT PROFILE. ONE CONSTANT, AND IT IS AN INTEGER TICK COUNT. Delay in interface/5, checked by connection_graph_check_delay/1, which refuses anything below one tick. There are decay-like constants elsewhere - the eligibility trace's fade in plasticity_engine_trace_step/5, the running average's smoothing in plasticity_engine_average_step/4 - but they belong to the engine, not to the edge, and are not a per-type profile.

FIELD SIX, SHORT-TERM DYNAMICS. ABSENT ENTIRELY. There is no release probability, no facilitation, no depression, no per-edge state of any kind beside the weight. The eligibility trace in plasticity_engine_trace_new/2 is the nearest thing structurally - a per-learnable-interface store in the explicit-stateless-value discipline - and it is a plasticity variable, not a transmission variable.

FIELD SEVEN, THE SYSTEMS INTERFACE BLOCK. NO EDGE ANALOGUE. konnectome's packs declare their exports; edges declare nothing.

FIELD EIGHT, ENDPOINTS. NODE-TO-NODE ONLY. From and To are construct names, resolved by connection_graph_state_get/3 through memberchk on a Name-Value state. An edge whose target is another edge - required by Chapters 53 and 57 - has no representation, and connection_graph_advance_line/4 throws existence_error(construct_activation, From) for any From that is not a named construct, which is exactly correct behaviour and exactly the wall.

WHAT KONNECTOME HAS THAT IS BETTER THAN THE SCORE SUGGESTS, and it is not nothing.

• A CLEAN, TOTAL REFUSAL DISCIPLINE. Four separate apply predicates each end in a clause that throws domain_error naming the exact rule it will not run: plain_step_construct_kind, modulated_step_construct_kind, delayed_step_construct_kind, delayed_modulated_step_construct_kind. A type registry that widens these can be proven not to have widened them silently.

• THE MODE REGISTER PACK IS ALREADY THE RIGHT SHAPE TO IMITATE. packs/mode_register/prolog/mode_register.pl carries a constructor that judges before it hands back (mode_register_new/6), a checker that refuses every block aloud (mode_register_check/1), a shape guard that refuses a hole (mode_register_shape/6), and a per-kind lookup with an explicit pass-through for unregistered kinds (mode_register_construct_rule/2). An edge_type pack built to the same template will be recognisably a sibling.

• THE ARCHETYPE PACK HAS ALREADY DONE THE HARD HALF ONCE. archetype_gate_automaton/2 takes the current mode as an ARGUMENT while archetype_gate_register/1, archetype_gate_transitions/1 and archetype_gate_transfer/2 stay per kind. That is exactly the per-instance versus per-kind split an edge type registry needs, already built and already tested.

• EVERY PER-EDGE STORE ALREADY FOLLOWS THE EXPLICIT-STATELESS-VALUE DISCIPLINE. plasticity_engine_trace_new/2 builds one entry per learnable interface and threads it through the tick as a value. A per-edge short-term dynamics store would be the third store of that exact shape.

SCORE, PLAINLY: one of the eight fields is present as a real field (the time constant, in its thinnest possible form), one is present as an accident of another flag (learnability), one is present as the sign of a number rather than a property (sign), and five are absent.


PART SIX - CONSTRAINTS AND CONTRADICTIONS ON THE CURRENT DESIGN

Findings, stated as findings. None of these is a bug, none invalidates a passing test, and none is to be fixed speculatively.

CONSTRAINT ONE, THE TRIPLE-DUTY FLAG. Kind == transmissive means, simultaneously and inseparably: it conveys a signal; it is learnable; it owns a delay line. The three come apart in the corpus at Chapter 26 (conveys, does not learn) and at Chapter 41, the pannexin hemichannel (publishes a plume with no address field, so it conveys to no named target). Any widening must decide which of the three the new type field takes over, and pin the other two.

CONSTRAINT TWO, THE ADDITIVE FOLD IS LOAD-BEARING. connection_graph_accumulate/4 and connection_graph_accumulate_delivery/3 both fold with a plain addition from a zero seed. Sign discipline that is only additive-with-signed-weights is expressible; divisive, symmetric, and field-coupled transmission are not, and cannot be added without changing what a fold means. This is the deepest structural constraint the deep read found and it should be met by design, at the point a shunting type is actually needed, not before.

CONSTRAINT THREE, THE DELAY IS FIXED AT LINE-CONSTRUCTION TIME. connection_graph_delay_lines_new/2 sizes each buffer as Delay minus one and seeds it with silence. Chapter 55's learnable delay would require resizing a line mid-run, which the current design deliberately does not permit. Recorded, not acted on.

CONSTRAINT FOUR, LEARNING AUTHORITY IS GLOBAL AND IMPLICIT. plasticity_engine_step/5 applies the one rule to every transmissive interface. There is no predicate anywhere that asks whether THIS edge may be updated BY THAT agent. Chapters 30, 31, 53 and 57 all require exactly that question to be askable. It is not a bug; it is the honest consequence of one rule for all.

CONSTRAINT FIVE, A CONTRADICTION BETWEEN TWO KONNECTOME DOCUMENTS, NOT BETWEEN CODE AND CORPUS. The analysis's Theme One elevation note (line 37) says learnability is a junction-grain MODE. Part Three of this document finds, from the Layer 7 source, that it is a property of the TYPE. Both are konnectome documents; the elevation note is a summary within a theme paragraph and this is the deep read the same document demands. This document is the later assessment on the narrow point and should be followed; the elevation note's larger claim - that Theme One is the spine and the other themes hang from it - is untouched and is confirmed, since the register hangs off the type exactly as a field hangs off a record.

CONSTRAINT SIX, THE TYPE REGISTRY IS NOT ITSELF A MODE REGISTER AND MUST NOT BE BUILT AS ONE. The temptation, given a freshly built mode_register pack, is to model a type as an automaton. The corpus forbids it: a type does not transition. Chapter 26's transition table says "There is no operating-mode transition in health; the junction is deliberately monostable" - about its MODE, while its TYPE is not the sort of thing that could transition at all. The two packs should be siblings, not one wearing the other's clothes.

CONSTRAINT SEVEN, NAMING. Type names are proposed by the corpus in snake case ending in _connection, while konnectome's edge term is called an INTERFACE and its two values are KINDS. The Theme One naming-collision resolution (ledger, Naming-collision note, closed at slice 39) reserved MODE for the corpus sense. No comparable reservation exists for TYPE, KIND, or INTERFACE, and the three are about to be needed at once.


PART SEVEN - THE SUGGESTED SLICE FAMILY, SMALLEST FIRST

The design authority suggests; the owner disposes. Each item is one ordinary test-first pack slice behind the usual gates. None touches a cousin. Where an item touches merged code, its behaviour is pinned identical by a named test, in the house style slices 39 and 40 already established.

SLICE ONE. THE EDGE TYPE REGISTRY, WITH KONNECTOME'S ONE TYPE STATED PROUDLY. A new pack, edge_type, built to the mode_register pack's template: a constructor that judges before it returns, a checker that refuses every field aloud by name, a shape guard that refuses a hole, and a per-type lookup with explicit pass-through. It holds ONE type, konnectome's own, with its position stated on every field Part Two names - name, sign discipline, fixed-or-learnable, rule identity, time-constant profile, endpoint shape. NO ENGINE IS TOUCHED, no edge term changes, and not one number moves. The test that matters is the one that reads konnectome's own type back out of the registry and finds it complete.

SLICE TWO. THE INTERFACE CARRIES ITS TYPE, AND THE TWO KINDS BECOME TWO TYPES. interface/5's Kind position is read THROUGH the registry, exactly as mode_register_construct_rule/2 is read through the register today. transmissive and computational both become registry-drawn type names. Every existing refusal keeps the exact error term it throws now. The pinning test is the whole existing connection_graph and plasticity_engine suite, unchanged, plus one new test asserting that an unregistered type passes through to the engine's own refusal rather than to a second authority.

SLICE THREE. LEARNABILITY BECOMES A TYPE PROPERTY, AND THE FIRST FIDELITY EDGE EXISTS. plasticity_engine_step/5 asks the registry whether the edge's type is learnable instead of asking whether Kind == transmissive. A second type is registered: the corpus's motor_effector_relay_connection, keyed to Chapter 26, transmissive and NOT learnable, validated block by block against that chapter's LONG-TERM PLASTICITY and SYSTEMS INTERFACE STATE blocks, in the way slice 40 was validated against the Layer 8 Either-Or Latch. Behaviour for every existing edge is pinned identical because konnectome's own type is learnable. The told story is a network in which one edge learns and one, by its type, proudly does not.

SLICE FOUR. SIGN DISCIPLINE AS A TYPE FIELD, ADDITIVE VALUES ONLY. Two values registered - positive and negative - both expressed through the existing additive fold, with the type asserting the sign the weight must keep and the learning rule forbidden to cross zero. Divisive, symmetric, and field-coupled sign disciplines are REGISTERED AS NAMES AND REFUSED ALOUD, so the registry is honest about the four-fifths of the corpus it cannot yet run. This slice is where Part Four decision three is taken and recorded.

SLICE FIVE. THE JUNCTION-GRAIN MODE REGISTER, NOW THAT IT HAS SOMEWHERE TO LIVE. Each registered type gains its mode register as a field, drawn from the modes companion's universal menu. konnectome's own type gets a register of one; the Chapter 26 fidelity type gets the corpus's actual register of one, stated proudly, keyed to Entry 26. This is the point the Theme One family's junction grain resumes, and it is why slices one to four come first.

SLICE SIX. SHORT-TERM DYNAMICS AS A PER-EDGE STORE. Release probability as a per-type parameter; facilitation and depression as a per-edge-instance store built by the same explicit-stateless-value discipline as plasticity_engine_trace_new/2, threaded through the tick as a value any caller may read. This is where Part Four decision eleven is taken.

SLICE SEVEN. THE SILENT-EDGE LIFE CYCLE, KONNECTOME'S FIRST STRUCTURAL PLASTICITY. Chapter 43's silent_eligible_connection: an edge that transmits exactly zero and accumulates eligibility, and a rule that unsilences it. Part Four decision six - type or mode - must be taken before this slice is written, because it decides whether unsilencing is a transition or a retyping.

SLICE EIGHT. EDGES WHOSE TARGET IS AN EDGE. Chapters 53 and 57's glial_ensheathment_modulation_connection and synapse_inspection_pruning_connection, and with them the learning-authority relation that Chapters 30 and 31 need. This is the family's most ambitious item and it should be the last, because it is the only one that changes what an endpoint is.

NOT IN THIS FAMILY, RECORDED SO IT IS NOT SMUGGLED IN. The template parser that would turn all sixty-four chapters into loadable fact files is family six of the top-down analysis (tooling), not this family, and this family must not widen to include it.


PART EIGHT - WALLS

NONE ROUTED.

Applying the Second and Third Commandment gates strictly: a causalontology change must be a record that needs SHARING across agents as a signed, evidence-graded, content-addressed artifact, not merely computed inside one runtime; a PrologAI change must be a real language gap; and findings are discovered by building, never guessed.

THE CAUSALONTOLOGY GATE. An edge type registry is ordinary pack data. A type is a compile-time-ish declaration read by engines inside one runtime; it is not a claim one agent must sign and hand to another. Nothing in the Layer 7 corpus asks for a junction type to travel between agents as an artifact.

THE PROLOGAI GATE. Every field Part Two names is expressible in ordinary Prolog terms of the shape konnectome already writes. The mode_register pack is the existence proof: a five-block corpus formalism with four cross-checked stores was built with no language change at all.

TWO CANDIDATES CONSIDERED AND REJECTED TODAY, RECORDED SO THE BUILDER WHO REACHES THEM KNOWS THEY WERE WEIGHED.

• THE TYPE-CATALOGUE-AS-SHARED-ARTIFACT CANDIDATE. If konnectome ever mechanically splices all sixty-four chapters into loadable fact files and a second agent must trust that the file it loaded is the file the corpus authorised, a content-addressed, evidence-graded record would be exactly the right shape - the confidence tags the corpus carries on every transfer function are already an evidence grade. It is NOT a wall today because the tooling family that would produce those files does not exist and nothing is being shared. This is a NEW WATCH and is recorded as WATCH-7, the type catalogue artifact.

• THE LEARNING-AUTHORITY CANDIDATE. Chapter 30's teaching edge is "authorised to update" Chapter 31's weights, and authority is the kind of thing that sometimes wants signing. It is NOT a wall today because within one runtime authority is a plain relation between two type names, and the Second Commandment forbids claiming a wall before slice eight actually cannot express it. It is folded into WATCH-7 rather than given its own number.

Sections C, D and E of the causalontology change order remain HELD; nothing in this document releases them.
