SECTION ONE. THE VERDICT

The corpus beneath Theme Two CONFIRMS the theme's central claim and CORRECTS four of its sentences. It reverses nothing about the architecture. It does, however, reverse the theme's own status: the paragraph calls this "the single most central gap the Vision Set names", and as of slice 38 four of the five suggested items in its slice family are CLOSED, which makes Theme Two the most-built of the five themes rather than the largest gap. The paragraph is stale on its own subject, and that is the first thing a reader must know.

WHAT IS CONFIRMED, and confirmed word for word. The theme's warning - "a synthetic mind built from the corpus's parts but run always-on would be a brain denied sleep" - is not a paraphrase. It is the corpus's own closing sentence, at LAYER_11_GLOBAL_STATES_MANUSCRIPT.txt line 4238, and it is preceded at line 4236 by the corpus naming the whole volume an operating-system specification: "a two-process scheduler (Chapters 14 and 15), bistable state switches with a stabiliser (11 through 13), a global configuration bus (16), power and emergency states (17 through 21), cron programmes (22 through 24), and a maintenance cycle (6 through 8) in which the system's own learning is finished." The theme's claim that interleaved replay is aimed expressly at catastrophic forgetting is likewise exact: Chapter 7 calls it "the flagship computation of the whole volume" and names the problem at line 1258. And the theme's claim that the slow-variables volume reaches the same place from below is exact: the volume's closing line 3621 says "the tag-and-capture allocation of this volume and the consolidation states of that one are two halves of a single machine, meeting in the night."

CORRECTION ONE. The theme says "the promise is unbuilt".

That sentence is now FALSE as a statement of fact, and it is false for a reason the corpus cannot supply - the corpus knows nothing about konnectome. Slices 35 through 38 built the operating state on the bus, wired the reward step with capture consuming the spent tags, built the two-process governor, and built the offline works with the catastrophic-forgetting rest-twin telling. Slice family one items (a), (b), (c) and (d) are all recorded CLOSED in /home/ccaitwo/konnectome/docs/konnectome_ledger.txt at lines 1312, 1348, 1389 and 1433. Only item (e) is outstanding. Any reader arriving at Theme Two's paragraph cold will design something that already exists.

CORRECTION TWO. The theme says "the schedule of all of this belongs to a two-process governor driving an alternation".

The corpus corrects "driving". Process S does not drive the alternation and neither does Process C. Chapter 14 at line 2356 is explicit: "It biases, but does not itself operate, the sleep-wake flip-flop of Chapter 11: as Process S climbs it shifts the switch's threshold toward the sleep side". Chapter 15 at line 2487 says the same of the clock: "It does not throw the sleep-wake flip-flop directly but broadcasts a phase signal ... that biases the switch's threshold". The thrower is the flip-flop of Chapter 11, a mutual-inhibition circuit whose signature the corpus says at line 1939 "is not a level but a shape". And a fourth construct holds the switch steady: Chapter 13's orexin stabiliser, whose konnectome note at line 2309 says it "does not select a state but raises the hysteresis of the sleep_wake_toggle_state (Chapter 11), publishing a scalar stability bias onto the configuration bus". The corpus therefore names FOUR separable constructs where the theme names one governor. konnectome's two_process_governor_step/4 fuses all four into a single predicate, which is Finding F1 in Section Five.

CORRECTION THREE. The theme says the alternation is "online computation and offline consolidation", a binary.

The corpus reverses the binary in both directions. On the offline side there are three cognitive sleep states, not one, with different jobs: Chapter 6's N2 packages memory in spindle-gated windows and its konnectome note at line 1124 names it spindle_gated_consolidation_state, "a batched, clocked write from a fast cache to slower durable storage, committing only during scheduled windows"; Chapter 7's N3 runs the deep write; Chapter 8's REM re-weights emotional memory with noradrenaline switched off (line 1475). Their ORDER is itself functional - Chapter 9 at line 1632 says the stages "are arranged across the night on purpose, with deep sleep front-loaded and rapid-eye-movement back-loaded", and at line 1634 that the organism uses the ordering "to run its offline jobs in the right sequence". On the online side, consolidation is not exclusively offline: Chapter 2, the Alpha Idle, at line 318 records "opportunistic early consolidation: quiet waking rest permits hippocampal replay events otherwise reserved for sleep, and post-learning rest measurably improves retention." So the corpus's own answer to "when does consolidation run" is not "offline"; it is "privileged offline, permitted at quiet rest, ordered across the night." The mode-register deep read reached the same wall from its own direction and recorded it in the ledger at line 1620: the online-and-offline atom "is also not extensible by adding atoms, because rapid-eye-movement sleep breaks any linear ordering."

CORRECTION FOUR. The theme says "write-permission over learning is itself a slow stored variable (the freeze fence, the readability gate)".

The parenthesis pairs two things the corpus keeps apart at different scopes with different owners, and it is the imprecision most likely to produce a wrong slice. The plasticity_freeze_fence of Chapter 11 "subscribes to maturation and criticality signals and publishes a write-protection state over converged subnetworks" (line 2242), with a density threshold of 0.4 (line 2288) and the corpus's own warning that "the freeze is a policy, not a fate" (line 2244). The readability_gate of Chapter 15 is not a fence over weights at all: it "subscribes to consolidation-request signals and publishes a per-gene access-permission that raises or lowers how strongly the durable-write path can express a change" (line 3026), computed as writer divided by writer plus eraser against a threshold of 0.6 (lines 3044 to 3048), with the explicit note that "it drifts unless maintained - unlike a permission flag stored once in a table" (line 3030). One is a territorial structural brake over an already-trained subnetwork; the other is a strength-of-expression valve on the durable-write path, downstream of a decision already taken. The mode-register design authority independently found the same shape at its line 210, naming four distinct write-permission kinds and warning that "collapsing these into a single freeze flag would lose both the sign inversion and the scope distinction."

CORRECTION FIVE, the smallest but the one that bears on already-merged code. The theme says "synaptic renormalisation run in that phase".

The corpus does not move renormalisation into the offline phase. Chapter 18 at line 3554 says the renormalisation_daemon "runs on a deliberately slow clock (hours to days) so it never competes with fast learning", and at line 3498 that sleep merely provides "a privileged window for down-scaling" with which the cellular machinery cooperates. Chapter 7's downward assignment at line 1244 is more precise still: N3 "sets cortical synapses to net renormalisation, the down-selection and downscaling of the day's potentiated connections, WHILE PERMITTING TARGETED STRENGTHENING OF REPLAYED TRACES." Net renormalisation with targeted strengthening, not renormalisation instead of learning. konnectome is corpus-correct here and the summary is the imprecise party: plasticity_engine_scaling_step_territory/6 runs by day and again at a raised rate by night, and cognitive_cycle_check_offline_scaling_rate/2 refuses a night bound set below the day's.

A SIXTH ITEM, offered as an observation rather than a correction. The theme's phrase "which events graduate to durable weights" presumes a DURABLE weight to graduate into. konnectome has one weight per interface. The entire slow-variables dossier exists because a fast store and a slow store are different objects - its outline at line 16 states the problem in one sentence, "every protein at a synapse turns over in hours to weeks, while memories last for decades", and at line 123 names the konnectome translation as "checkpointing, write-allocation policy, STORAGE TIERING FROM FAST-VOLATILE TO SLOW-DURABLE, and a renormalisation daemon". The tier split is the load-bearing thing Theme Two's paragraph never names, and Section Six treats it as this read's one scheduling reversal.


SECTION TWO. WHAT THE CORPUS FIXES

These are things konnectome would otherwise have to invent, and must not. All are quoted or transcribed from the passages cited in the reading list above.

• THE FOUR CONSTRUCTS OF THE SCHEDULER, SEPARATED. Process S, an accumulating scalar that rises with uptime and discharges in deep sleep, exponential in shape, saturating after long waking, floored by nothing but its own discharge. Process C, a roughly twenty-four-hour waveform phase-locked to an external light signal, opposing S on purpose. The flip-flop, a mutual-inhibition switch that publishes "a single binary state selection, wake or sleep" with transitions "seconds to a few minutes" and state holds of "hours" (lines 2021, 2033). The stabiliser, which "does not select a state but raises the hysteresis" and publishes a scalar bias.

• THE NAMED QUANTITIES OF THE NIGHT. Cycle length about 90 minutes, range 70 to 120; four to six cycles per night; N3 at 13 to 23 percent of the night and front-loaded to the first one or two cycles; N2 at 45 to 55 percent with spindle density 2 to 5 per minute; REM at 20 to 25 percent, first period under 10 minutes lengthening to about 40; roughly sixteen hours held awake and eight asleep.

• THE TAG SCHEMA, ENTIRE. State: "Tag strength at each synapse, a decaying value between 0 and 1, one per synapse" (line 877). Parameters: "Tag-setting threshold; decay time constant of roughly one to two hours; spatial capture radius along the dendrite" (line 881). Capacity: "one eligibility flag per synapse" (line 839). The binding law, and konnectome already honours it: "an unused address must expire so the synapse does not wrongly capture a later, unrelated shipment" (line 835). Its konnectome name, given by the corpus itself: write_allocation_tag (line 895). Its mode register, from LAYER_04_05_MODES_OUTLINE Entry 5: UNSET (Ineligible Dock), SET (Loading Dock Open), CONSUMED (Cargo Captured). Its runnable defaults, from the chapter's code sample: decay per step 0.8, capture threshold 0.2.

• THE WRITE-ALLOCATION GATE SCHEMA, ENTIRE. Its konnectome name, again the corpus's own: write_allocation_gate, "a per-neuron admission controller that subscribes to integrated activity signals and publishes both a commit-to-long-term signal and an eligibility flag for which cell records the memory" (line 710). Its analogue: "a write-ahead-log commit gate combined with an admission-control policy". Its stated biological difference, which is a design decision konnectome must take and not stumble into: "the gate also raises the host cell's future excitability, biasing which unit wins the next write, a feedback an ordinary commit gate does not have" (line 714). Its mechanism, not merely its effect: signal accumulates as event strength plus baseline and is multiplied by a decay between spaced events, so spacing beats massing because "spacing lets CREB activation accumulate and outlast its own inhibitors" (line 614). Its runnable defaults: threshold 5.0, decay 0.6, baseline as the per-unit allocation bias. Its mode register, from MODES Entry 4: BELOW (Waiting Gate), FIRED (Graduation Pulse), PRIMED (Eligible Cell). Its allocation window: "hours". Its population size: "on the order of a small percentage of neurons in a region winning allocation" (line 654).

• THE CARGO, AND THE ORDER OF THE PIPELINE. Chapter 6 fixes what makes capture possible: "A write is the strong, CREB-engaging event that triggers the immediate-early gene response and launches the product wave into the dendritic tree" (line 993), delivered by broadcast and seized only at tagged synapses, with the timing deliberate - "Individual products are degraded within hours, so the delivery window overlaps the one-to-two-hour life of the synaptic tag BY DESIGN" (line 987) - and uncaptured cargo cleared "so it cannot consolidate the wrong synapse later" (line 1005). The ordering the corpus fixes is: tag set in minutes, gate crossed, immediate-early response in tens of minutes, cargo wave over minutes to an hour, capture inside the tag's one-to-two-hour window, structural change lasting days to a lifetime. Its mode register, from MODES Entry 6: QUIESCENT, BROADCASTING, CAPTURED.

• THE READABILITY GATE SCHEMA. Acetylation equals writer over writer plus eraser; threshold 0.6; two modes, OPEN (Loose Spool) and CLOSED (Tight Spool); publishes "per-gene chromatin readability, raising or lowering transcription of plasticity genes" (line 3004); gates "how much capture cargo an event can manufacture" (line 2974); reversible and drifting.

• THE FREEZE FENCE SCHEMA. Density threshold 0.4; two modes, PERMISSIVE (Fence Down) and FENCED (Fence Up); formation over weeks, enzymatic removal over days, maintenance over years; publishes "plasticity-closed-or-open state of the enclosed circuit" (line 2217); scope is the enclosed converged subnetwork; removable by design.

• THE RENORMALISER SCHEMA. One shared multiplicative factor across a unit's whole weight vector, "so the relative pattern that learning wrote is preserved exactly while the overall level is renormalised" (line 3452); target rate of a few hertz, 5.0 in the sample; sensing and correction "over many hours to days"; three modes from MODES Entry 18: QUIESCENT (Governor Resting), RENORMALISING UP, RENORMALISING DOWN; and an honest unknown the corpus flags itself - "the exact biological set point and how tightly it is defended in human neurons in vivo are not precisely measured" (line 3492).

• TWENTY KONNECTOME MODULE NAMES, given by the corpus and therefore not konnectome's to coin: bistable_weight_latch, write_allocation_tag, write_allocation_gate, plasticity_freeze_fence, readability_gate, durable_configuration_store, renormalisation_daemon, offline_consolidation_state, spindle_gated_consolidation_state, rem_gate_state, sleep_wake_toggle_state, sleep_pressure_accumulator, circadian_clock_governor, two_process_scheduler, ultradian_cycle_sequencer, arousal_stabiliser_governor, state_broadcast_service, online_task_state, generative_idle_state, degraded_prestandby_state.

• THE CLOSING INSTRUCTION, which is the corpus telling konnectome the shape of the whole family: "For konnectome: implement the bistable_weight_latch, the write_allocation_tag, the durable_configuration_store, and the renormalisation_daemon, WIRE THEM UNDER THE OFFLINE STATES OF THE SCHEDULER" (line 3623). Two of those four are built. Two are not, and both of the unbuilt two are storage-tier constructs.


SECTION THREE. WHAT THE CORPUS DOES NOT SETTLE

These are konnectome's own decisions. The corpus cannot take them, because taking them requires knowing what konnectome is. This is the highest-value section of this document and every item here is a candidate for a recorded decision in the ledger, whether or not it becomes a slice.

1. WHAT A TAG IS ATTACHED TO, GIVEN THAT KONNECTOME HAS NO SYNAPSES. The corpus's tag is one flag per synapse and a neuron holds thousands of independently decaying tags, which the chapter says at line 839 "is what gives tagging its addressing power." konnectome's trace store holds one value per learnable interface, keyed (From-To). Whether one tag per edge pair is a faithful reading of "one per synapse" or a silent collapse of the corpus's addressing power is konnectome's call, and it decides whether the eligibility store ever needs a second key.

2. WHETHER CONSUMED IS A STATE OR A RETURN TO ZERO. plasticity_engine_reward_capture_step/6 zeroes a spent tag. The corpus's MODES Entry 5 makes CONSUMED (Cargo Captured) a THIRD mode distinct from UNSET (Ineligible Dock), and the manuscript never says how long a consumed synapse stays consumed, nor whether it may be re-tagged within the same window. konnectome must decide whether a spent tag is observably different from a never-set one. This is the cheapest decision in the list and it is a prerequisite of item 4.

3. WHO OWNS THE WRITE-ALLOCATION GATE IN A SYSTEM WITH NO NUCLEUS. The corpus's gate is per neuron and its output is a commit signal plus a per-cell eligibility flag. konnectome's constructs are not cells, have no gene programme, and have no nucleus to petition. Whether the gate sits per construct, per territory, or as a bus-published permission is undecidable from the corpus and is the first structural choice item (e) must make.

4. WHETHER THE ALLOCATION BIAS IS BUILT AT ALL. The corpus flags the gate raising its host's future excitability as the very thing that distinguishes it from an ordinary commit gate (line 714), and MODES Entry 4 gives it a mode name, PRIMED (Eligible Cell). It is also the construct in this whole theme that comes closest to a self-modifying scheduler, because it lets a past write bias which unit wins the next one. Building it, or recording a reasoned refusal of it against the never-invent invariant, is konnectome's own and must be decided explicitly rather than skipped.

5. WHAT A GENE IS. The readability gate publishes a PER-GENE access permission. konnectome has no genes. Whether the readability gate's key is a rule identity, a construct kind, a territory, or the durable-write path taken as a whole is not derivable from the corpus and determines whether the gate is one scalar or a store.

6. WHAT THE FENCE'S SCOPE UNIT IS. The corpus fences "converged subnetworks" and the cells a net encloses. konnectome has territories (already the key of the bus's per-territory levels and of the per-territory scaling targets) and it will one day have named subgraph views (Theme Four, unbuilt). Choosing the territory makes the fence cheap and immediately expressible in the diffuse-field law's exact shape; choosing the subgraph view makes it faithful but couples item (e) to Theme Four. The corpus does not choose.

7. WHAT THROWS THE FENCE. The corpus's fence subscribes to "developmental stage; sensory experience; parvalbumin-interneuron maturation" (line 2213). konnectome has no developmental clock and no convergence or criticality measure. Inventing one is a konnectome decision with the never-invent invariant standing over it, and the honest alternative - an externally set fence with no automatic thrower - is also konnectome's to choose.

8. COMMAND VERSUS BIAS, IN ITS THEME TWO FORM. The mode-register design authority's Part Seven records that the corpus is genuinely silent on whether a bus-thrown transition commands or biases. The same silence covers the offline state: the corpus says what the state ASSIGNS at each layer but never says whether an assignee may weigh the assignment against its own condition. Since the offline broadcast is the only assignment konnectome currently makes, this decision lands here first and should be recorded here, consistently with the Theme One answer.

9. WHETHER THE OFFLINE PHASE BECOMES THREE. The corpus gives N2, N3 and REM distinct jobs and a functional order, and Chapter 12's konnectome note fixes the SHAPE of any extension - rem_gate_state is "a nested two-position governor that runs only while the parent sleep_wake_toggle_state (Chapter 11) is in the sleep position". So the corpus settles that nesting rather than a flat atom list is the answer, but it cannot settle whether konnectome needs the nesting at all, nor which of the three offline jobs konnectome has any use for. Slice 38 already read and deliberately did not build Chapters 8 and 9; that decision stands and should be re-taken, not inherited.

10. WHETHER QUIET WAKING REPLAYS. Chapter 2 line 318 says quiet waking rest permits replay otherwise reserved for sleep. konnectome's night program closes the sensory gate absolutely and there is no third program. Whether the Alpha Idle becomes a state, and what would even distinguish it from the online state in a system with no default mode network, is konnectome's.

11. WHAT A MEMORY IS, AND HOW MANY ARE KEPT. konnectome replays activation snapshots and the memory store is unbounded within a run, recorded as a named honest limit in the slice-38 ledger note. The corpus replays hippocampal traces and offers no bound, no eviction policy, and no statement of what a trace must contain. Any bound konnectome adopts is konnectome's own.

12. THE REPLAY DOSE, AND THE REPLAY ORDER. Two separate decisions the corpus does not take. On order, the corpus's own N3 sample SHUFFLES the batch (line 1352) while konnectome deterministically alternates and declares the simplification - determinism is forced on konnectome by the regression gate, and that is a konnectome constraint the corpus cannot know about. On dose, slice 38's deepest finding - that the replay dose is shared across the batch rather than paid once per pattern, so it is invariant to store size - was reasoned from Chapter 7's "net renormalisation" and not from any stated rule. The corpus states no dose law. That decision is konnectome's own, is load-bearing, and must be re-checked if replay ever becomes spindle-grained.


SECTION FOUR. WHAT KONNECTOME HAS, SCORED HONESTLY

Block by block against the corpus's own schema. The score is the honest one, not the flattering one.

• PROCESS S. BUILT and faithful in shape. two_process_governor_next_pressure/5 rises by one rate per waking tick and discharges by a steeper rate per sleeping tick, floored at zero. DIVERGENCE, declared: the corpus's build and discharge are "roughly exponentially" (line 2350) and konnectome's are linear. MISSING: the corpus's S-saturation mode (The Flattening Shoulder, MODES Entry 14), which a linear counter cannot express.

• PROCESS C. BUILT. two_process_governor_wave/4 is a triangle wave, zero at midnight and full amplitude at midday, declared in the module comment as "an honest simplification of the corpus's smooth waveform". MISSING and unremarked: the corpus's Process C subscribes to LIGHT and is re-entrained over several days after a phase shift; konnectome's clock is free-running with no input at all and cannot be shifted. The corpus's three modes (alerting crest, circadian trough, day ramps) are not expressed.

• THE FLIP-FLOP. BUILT, and built with genuine hysteresis: two_process_governor_select/5 snaps offline at or past the sleep threshold and online only at or below a strictly lower wake threshold, with an inverted band refused by name at build time. This is corpus-correct. STRUCTURAL DIVERGENCE: it is not a construct. It is three lines inside two_process_governor_step/4, where the corpus keeps it a separate circuit that S and C only bias.

• THE STABILISER. ABSENT AS A CONSTRUCT. It exists as two numbers, SleepThreshold and WakeThreshold, inside two_process_parameters/6. The corpus's arousal_stabiliser_governor publishes a scalar stability bias onto the bus and is gated by circadian phase; nothing in konnectome can modulate the hysteresis at run time. Already logged from the other direction as OBSERVATION-8 in the ledger.

• THE STATE ANNOUNCEMENT SERVICE. BUILT, and built at the corpus's own address. neuromodulator_bus_broadcast_operating_state/3 and neuromodulator_bus_operating_state/2 carry the state under the compound key global(operating_state), defaulting to online, with neuromodulator_bus_check_operating_state/1 refusing any third value. Chapter 16's konnectome note names the bus the state_broadcast_service and the corpus's own ancestor of it. SCORE ON THE DOWNWARD HALF: zero. The corpus's announcement carries a coordinated assignment of modes at four layers; konnectome's carries one boolean that one predicate reads, which the ledger at line 1556 already states in exactly those words.

• INTERLEAVED REPLAY. BUILT and honest. offline_consolidation_interleave/2 splits the store oldest-first and alternates the newer half against the older, giving the odd extra to the older half "as the standing cortex outweighs one day's hippocampus"; offline_consolidation_replay_round/4 shares one dose across the batch and folds the patterns over the cortex in interleaved order; offline_consolidation_replay_interface/4 strengthens through plasticity_engine_weight_change/5 with the third factor held at one, justified by Chapter 7's low-aminergic permissive climate. The catastrophic-forgetting claim is pinned by a rest-twin telling. DECLARED DIVERGENCES: deterministic alternation against the corpus's shuffle; one round per offline tick against the corpus's per-spindle grain.

• RENORMALISATION. BUILT and corpus-correct on the point that matters. plasticity_engine_scaling_step_territory/6 multiplies a receiving construct's incoming weights by one shared factor derived from its target-minus-average error, which is Chapter 18's multiplicative ideal preserving every pairwise ratio; offline_consolidation_night_step/8 pins the order, replay first and the raised bound second. NAMED LIMIT, from the ledger: the night's bound is OPEN-LOOP, judging the day's standing averages because averages do not move through the dark hours, where the corpus's daemon senses continuously. MISSING: the three-mode register (QUIESCENT, UP, DOWN) and the glial up-scaling partner.

• TAG AND CAPTURE. BUILT, PARTIAL. plasticity_engine_trace_new/2, plasticity_engine_trace_step/5 and plasticity_engine_reward_capture_step/6 give a decaying per-edge eligibility value that is spent into the weight and consumed when served - and the corpus's own konnectome note at line 895 says the synaptic tag IS an eligibility trace, so this is not an analogy konnectome invented. THE GAP, and it is structural rather than cosmetic: in the corpus, capture requires TWO things, a live tag AND cargo that exists only because a gate fired. konnectome's second condition is nonzero dopamine at the receiving territory, which is the corpus's THIRD FACTOR, not its cargo. There is no upstream admission decision at all.

• THE WRITE-ALLOCATION GATE. ABSENT. Item (e).

• THE READABILITY GATE. ABSENT, and not currently named in item (e)'s text even though Theme Two's own paragraph names it.

• THE PLASTICITY FREEZE FENCE. ABSENT. Item (e).

• THE BISTABLE WEIGHT LATCH AND THE DURABLE CONFIGURATION STORE. ABSENT, and unlisted anywhere in the analysis's Part Six. konnectome has exactly one weight per interface and therefore no fast-volatile-to-slow-durable tier. Two of the four constructs the slow-variables volume's closing line instructs konnectome to implement are these two.

SECTION FIVE. CONSTRAINTS AND CONTRADICTIONS ON THE CURRENT DESIGN

Each is stated as a finding about merged, green, passing code. None is a bug and none is a licence to change anything speculatively.

F1. SECTION SIX. SCHEDULING FINDINGS

Theme One's deep read produced one reversal of the analysis's Part Six ordering. This read produces one insertion and one split, and one honest negative.

FINDING A, THE INSERTION, and it is this read's principal scheduling result. A DURABLE WEIGHT TIER SHOULD BE SCHEDULED AHEAD OF ITEM (e), AND IT IS LISTED NOWHERE IN PART SIX. The whole slow-variables dossier exists to specify a fast-volatile-to-slow-durable split; its outline names storage tiering as one of four konnectome translations and its closing line instructs konnectome to implement the bistable_weight_latch and the durable_configuration_store. FINDING B, THE SPLIT, and it is not the split the mode-register read already recorded. That read split item (e) by permission KIND, into four kinds at different scopes. The corpus's pipeline splits it again by DEPENDENCY, and the two splits are orthogonal. FINDING C, THE HONEST NEGATIVE. Nothing in this read reorders families two through six relative to one another. Theme One's elevation to architectural spine stands untouched. Theme Three's precedence over the junction-grain mode register stands untouched. This read found no reason to move family one relative to family two, and specifically no reason to hold family one's remainder behind the mode register: items (e1) through (e3) are ordinary pack work that can proceed beside the register family, with the one caveat of Finding F8, that the edge type registry will touch the night engine when it lands.

FINDING D, a smaller note offered without a recommendation.
