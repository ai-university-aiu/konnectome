WHAT IS IN HERE

The documents that say what konnectome SHOULD be, and how far it currently falls short of that. Three kinds, and the distinction is worth keeping:

A DESIGN AUTHORITY is the deep read that a slice family is designed AGAINST. It is written before the code, it is named in the ledger entry of every slice that answers it, and it is the document a reviewer opens to ask whether a slice did what it promised. Numbers 07 through 10, and the rung-five machine design at 12, are these.

A GAP ANALYSIS is the honest measurement of the distance between konnectome as built and konnectome as the north star describes it. It classifies each finding by kind - architectural gap versus content gap - so a reader can tell a missing predicate from a missing idea. Numbers 02, 03 and 06 are these.

A DIRECTION DOCUMENT reads the vision set or the published cognitive-architecture study set from the top down, and says where the build should go next. Numbers 01, 04 and 11 are these, with 04 and 05 doubling as the two quality controls on 01.


THE NUMBERS, AND WHAT THEY MEAN

The word konnectome is dropped from every filename - it was pure repetition inside konnectome's own repository, and it pushed the part that actually distinguishes one document from another off to the right where filenames are hardest to scan.

In its place each file carries a two-digit number, 01 to 12, and THE NUMBER IS THE READING ORDER AND THE FULFILMENT ORDER AT ONCE. A session that opens this directory cold should read downward and find each document's authority already established by the one above it.

01 THE VISION-SET TOP-DOWN ANALYSIS. First because it is the ROOT. It is the Eighth Commandment's analysis, and it divides the corpus into the numbered THEMES that four of the design authorities below are each a deep read beneath. Nothing else here can be judged without it.

02 THE NORTH-STAR GAP ANALYSIS. Second because it is the constitutional QUEUE DOCUMENT. The Twentieth Commandment's deliverable, and its Part Nine is the standing statement of the build queue - the hand-off protocol points a new session at it to learn what gets built next.

03 THE TWO-LIBRARY GAP ANALYSIS. Added 2026-08-13. Third because it is the most CURRENT general analysis: the first written since the First Commandment widened to the whole principles library at Constitution version 30, it holds BOTH libraries - docs/principles/ refreshed with 2026 research, and docs/neuroscience/ - against the seventy-four-slice build, and its Part Five carries the renumbering map for this directory. It ranks and corroborates the queue; it does not displace 02 as the queue of record.

04 THE FIDELITY AUDIT. The QUALITY CONTROL ON 01. It re-measured every countable claim in the analysis by direct grep rather than from memory. A reader who trusts 01 should know what this found before relying on it.

05 THE FULFILMENT AUDIT. The SECOND quality control on 01 and the companion of 04, now sitting beside it as its own entry always said it should - the two ask different questions of the same document. 04 asks whether 01's CLAIMS ARE TRUE, by re-measuring every countable one. 05 asks whether 01's FINDINGS WERE ACTED ON, by tracing each part of it through thirty-two slices of ledger and build log. It is the owner's scheduled audit of 2026-08-09, commissioned in the ledger's SCHEDULED AUDIT note, run once its trigger had passed by six hand-offs, and it lived at number 11 from its addition at slice 70 until the 2026-08-13 renumbering discharged the standing rule its entry carried.

06 THE NEUROSCIENCE-CORPUS QA GAP ANALYSIS. It audits the SOURCE that 01 and 02 both read from. Same role as 04, one level further back.

07 to 10 THE FOUR DESIGN AUTHORITIES, in the order of the themes they sit beneath and in the order the build has taken them. 07 the mode register (Theme One - THE HUB DOCUMENT OF THIS DIRECTORY, and the companion the other three cite. A MODE is the discrete operating regime a construct is in, as against its STATE, which is the continuous quantity moving within that mode; the full formalism is the HYBRID AUTOMATON, being the mode register plus per-mode transfer functions plus a transition table plus fault regimes. Its Part Nine family ran slices 39 THROUGH 47 - it planned seven and built nine - and further registers landed at slices 50 and 60. CORRECTED at slice 64: this line read 'built at slices 39 and 40', which understated the family by seven slices. AND READ ITS PART FIVE WITH ITS DATE IN MIND: that part is a status report written at slice 39 and most of what it calls absent has since been built); 08 the two-shift principle and offline consolidation (Theme Two, built at slices 37 and 38); 09 the edge type system (Theme Three, whose registry is Family Three in the queue and now has its first consumer outside its own design authority); 10 the motif catalogue and the global workspace (Themes Four and Five, the furthest out, and the one whose pilot must answer five questions before it may merge).

11 THE COGNITIVE-ARCHITECTURES COVERAGE SURVEY. Late because it INFORMS without GATING. It is the Ninth Commandment's survey of published architectures outside this build - valuable comparison, but no slice waits on it.

12 THE RUNG-FIVE REAL MACHINE DESIGN. Last because it is the LAST RUNG OF THE LADDER, and because it is the only document here that waits on the owner rather than on the build: it names decisions about real hardware and authorizes no purchase, claim, or slice by itself.

THE NUMBERS ARE A JUDGEMENT AND NOT A LAW. They record how the build reads today. Renumbering costs nothing but the rename and a pointer sweep, and if the queue turns, they should be renumbered rather than quietly left to lie. The 2026-08-13 renumbering is the first exercise of that rule: the old-to-new map is in Part Five of 03 and in the ledger's renumbering note, and any older document citing these files by their pre-renumbering numbers is a record of a dated act, resolved by that map rather than by editing the record.
