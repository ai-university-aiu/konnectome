WHAT IS IN HERE

The documents that say what konnectome SHOULD be, and how far it currently falls short of that. Three kinds, and the distinction is worth keeping:

A DESIGN AUTHORITY is the deep read that a slice family is designed AGAINST. It is written before the code, it is named in the ledger entry of every slice that answers it, and it is the document a reviewer opens to ask whether a slice did what it promised. Numbers 05 through 08, and the rung-five machine design at 10, are these.

A GAP ANALYSIS is the honest measurement of the distance between konnectome as built and konnectome as the north star describes it. It classifies each finding by kind - architectural gap versus content gap - so a reader can tell a missing predicate from a missing idea. Numbers 02 and 04 are these.

A DIRECTION DOCUMENT reads the vision set or the published cognitive-architecture study set from the top down, and says where the build should go next. Numbers 01, 03 and 09 are these.


THE NUMBERS, AND WHAT THEY MEAN

The word konnectome is dropped from every filename - it was pure repetition inside konnectome's own repository, and it pushed the part that actually distinguishes one document from another off to the right where filenames are hardest to scan.

In its place each file carries a two-digit number, 01 to 10, and THE NUMBER IS THE READING ORDER AND THE FULFILMENT ORDER AT ONCE. A session that opens this directory cold should read downward and find each document's authority already established by the one above it.

01 THE VISION-SET TOP-DOWN ANALYSIS. First because it is the ROOT. It is the Eighth Commandment's analysis, and it divides the corpus into the numbered THEMES that four of the design authorities below are each a deep read beneath. Nothing else here can be judged without it.

02 THE NORTH-STAR GAP ANALYSIS. Second because it is the most CURRENT. The Twentieth Commandment's deliverable, and its Part Nine is the standing statement of the build queue - the hand-off protocol points a new session at it to learn what gets built next.

03 THE FIDELITY AUDIT. Third because it is the QUALITY CONTROL ON 01. It re-measured every countable claim in the analysis by direct grep rather than from memory. A reader who trusts 01 should know what this found before relying on it.

04 THE NEUROSCIENCE-CORPUS QA GAP ANALYSIS. Fourth because it audits the SOURCE that 01 and 02 both read from. Same role as 03, one level further back.

05 to 08 THE FOUR DESIGN AUTHORITIES, in the order of the themes they sit beneath and in the order the build has taken them. 05 the mode register (Theme One, built at slices 39 and 40, and the companion the other three cite); 06 the two-shift principle and offline consolidation (Theme Two, built at slices 37 and 38); 07 the edge type system (Theme Three, whose registry is Family Three in the queue and now has its first consumer outside its own design authority); 08 the motif catalogue and the global workspace (Themes Four and Five, the furthest out, and the one whose pilot must answer five questions before it may merge).

09 THE COGNITIVE-ARCHITECTURES COVERAGE SURVEY. Ninth because it INFORMS without GATING. It is the Ninth Commandment's survey of published architectures outside this build - valuable comparison, but no slice waits on it.

10 THE RUNG-FIVE REAL MACHINE DESIGN. Last because it is the LAST RUNG OF THE LADDER, and because it is the only document here that waits on the owner rather than on the build: it names decisions about real hardware and authorizes no purchase, claim, or slice by itself.

THE NUMBERS ARE A JUDGEMENT AND NOT A LAW. They record how the build reads today. Renumbering costs nothing but the rename and a pointer sweep, and if the queue turns, they should be renumbered rather than quietly left to lie.


WHAT IS NOT IN HERE

The DSPARCD Fileset, which has its own directory and its own rule in the Constitution. The ledger and the tutorial, which are working documents and live in /docs/ . The hand-offs, which live under the two-location rule in /docs/hand-off/ . The north-star corpus itself, which lives in /docs/neuroscience/ and /docs/principles/ .

SUPERSEDED VERSIONS DO NOT GO INTO docs/design/archive/ , because there is no such directory. The Eleventh Commandment names exactly one archive, at docs/archive/ , and every superseded version of every document in here goes there.


A NOTE ON THIS DIRECTORY AND THE CONSTITUTION

This directory was made by tidying, not by a Commandment. The Constitution names the directories it creates - docs/archive/ , docs/DSPARCD/ , docs/neuroscience/ , docs/hand-off/ - and docs/design/ is not yet among them. The constitutional pointers that used to name these files at /docs/ were repointed to /docs/design/ in the same change, so no pointer rots, and NO RULE CHANGED. If the owner wants this directory written into the Constitution proper, that is an amendment, and it has not been made here.
