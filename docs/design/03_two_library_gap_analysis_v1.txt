WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT - READ THIS BEFORE BUILDING FROM ANYTHING BELOW

It holds the same TWO KINDS OF CONTENT as its siblings, under the rule the Eighth and Twentieth
Commandments both state.

FIRST KIND - THE SUMMARIES. Where this document summarises a library file, it is DERIVED and carries

SECOND KIND - THE FINDINGS. Where this document says konnectome LACKS something, that is konnectome's

PART TWO - METHOD

The refreshed principles documents were read in full at the outline level and at the changed
sections. The neuroscience library was not re-read in full - document 05's fulfilment audit stands
as the last full read, and its findings are incorporated by reference rather than repeated. The
codebase was surveyed fresh for this analysis: pack inventory, test counts, gate status, the queue
of record in the current hand-off, and the ledger's open observations.

Each finding below is classified two ways, as the Twentieth Commandment requires. FIRST by KIND:
an ARCHITECTURAL gap means konnectome could not express the thing without a change to its shape, to
causalontology, or to PrologAI; a CONTENT gap means konnectome could express it and has not built it.
SECOND by CLASS, one of the Commandment's seven: function, process, architecture, design element,
structure, idea, or inspiration.


PART THREE - THE FINDINGS

FINDING TL-1. THE INTERACTIVE EVALUATION GAP. Class: IDEA. Kind: CONTENT.
The principles library now describes ARC-AGI-3: an evaluation in which a system is dropped into a
small unfamiliar world with no instructions and must explore, infer the goal, and plan - and on
which every frontier model scored under one percent at launch while humans scored one hundred.
konnectome's capstone demonstration is scripted: it shows the machine doing what the script exercises
and never asks it to discover a goal. The library's own argument (Chollet: intelligence is
skill-acquisition efficiency) is the argument the Layer 12 faculty specification already gestures at.
WHAT THIS FINDING IS NOT: it is not a proposal to wire konnectome to the public ARC-AGI-3 service,
and the Safety Gate's ARC-AGI-1 and ARC-AGI-2 regressions are untouched. It is a finding that
konnectome LACKS ANY INTERACTIVE, GOAL-DISCOVERY DEMONSTRATION of its own, and that the corpus-side
machinery for one exists (perturbation_interface, simulated_body, drive_system, prediction_loop).
ROUTING: ledger first, then a slice-family candidate - a small novel-world demonstration in which
the running loops must discover the goal rather than be told it.

FINDING TL-2. THE EFFICIENCY MEASURE. Class: DESIGN ELEMENT. Kind: CONTENT.
The refreshed library reports that ARC-AGI-2 scores now carry a MANDATORY COST MEASURE, because an
expensive brute-force success must not masquerade as intelligence, and the roadmap's Gap Seven
(energy efficiency) makes the same point biologically. konnectome's demonstrations report
correctness and never cost: no demonstration records ticks consumed, calls made, or work done per
result. ROUTING: ledger; a cheap slice - the capstone and any future interactive demonstration
report a cost line beside every result line.

FINDING TL-3. THE TIME-HORIZON MEASURE. Class: IDEA. Kind: CONTENT.
The library now carries the METR time horizon: the length of autonomous work a system completes at
fifty percent reliability, tracked over time. konnectome has no analogous longitudinal measure of
its own autonomy - how long the cognitive_cycle runs unattended before something (watchdog,
supervisor, conflict_monitor) must intervene. The instrumentation exists; the measure does not.
NOTE THE COLLISION WITH OBSERVATION-23: the tick calibration conflict (two calibrations three
hundred and sixty thousand fold apart) makes any time measure ambiguous until it is decided. This
finding therefore SUPPORTS the queue of record's item one rather than competing with it: decide the
tick, then the horizon measure becomes definable.
ROUTING: ledger, annotated to OBSERVATION-23.

FINDING TL-4. CONTINUAL LEARNING IS NOW THE LIBRARIES' LOUDEST SHARED DEMAND. Class: PROCESS.
Kind: CONTENT.
The refreshed principles library says it three ways at once: the roadmap's Gap Two (catastrophic
forgetting), the psychometric definition of AGI reporting long-term memory and continual learning as
the severest deficits of the systems it scored, and the new reading subsection's continual-learning
entries. The neuroscience library has said it all along (complementary learning systems; the slow
variables of Layers 4 and 5; consolidation). konnectome's state: plasticity_engine exists,
offline_consolidation exists, and yet the FIRST LEARNING RULE is still queued, the eligibility trace
has no consumer, and no durable tier exists. THIS FINDING RANKS RATHER THAN DISCOVERS: the queue of
record already holds these items; the two-library read says they are not one family among many but
the point where both libraries converge, and a queue reordering in their favour is defensible the
next time the queue is judged.
ROUTING: no new ledger entry; this is corroboration of open queue items, recorded here.

FINDING TL-5. SELF-IMPROVEMENT IS DELIBERATELY ABSENT, AND THE ABSENCE SHOULD BE A DECLARED
DECISION RATHER THAN A SILENCE. Class: ARCHITECTURE. Kind: ARCHITECTURAL, in the honest sense that
expressing it safely would change konnectome's shape.
The principles library now documents an empirical recursive self-improvement program (Self-Taught
Optimizer, Godel Agent, Darwin Godel Machine, SEAL, and the rest) and its consistent bottleneck, THE
EVALUATOR - a system improves only as far as something can reliably judge that a change is an
improvement. konnectome has no self-modification anywhere (self_provenance is provenance;
plasticity_engine is synaptic), and nothing in either library obliges it to acquire any. But the
evaluator bottleneck is konnectome's own oldest discipline wearing a new name: the Safety Gate is an
evaluator, the declared-invention protocol is an evaluator's paper trail, and the fulfilment audit
is an evaluator run by hand. THE FINDING: konnectome's position on self-modification is currently
implicit. It should be a NUMBERED DECISION in the ledger - stating that self-modification of packs
by packs is out of scope, under which Commandments, and what would have to be true before that
changed - so that a later session meets a decision rather than a silence.
ROUTING: ledger, as a decision candidate for the owner.

FINDING TL-6. THE CURIOSITY GAP. Class: FUNCTION. Kind: CONTENT.
Both libraries name intrinsic motivation: the roadmap's Chapter 9 (dopamine, curiosity), AGI FOR
EVERYONE's motivation topics, and the neuroscience library's drive and neuromodulator material.
konnectome has drive_system and arousal_regulation - pressure and gain - but nothing that seeks
novelty or prefers the unexplored, and the interactive demonstration of TL-1 would need exactly
that. ROUTING: ledger; slice-family candidate, downstream of TL-1.

FINDING TL-7. THE TEN-DOMAIN ACCEPTANCE LENS. Class: INSPIRATION. Kind: CONTENT.
The library's new psychometric definition of AGI scores a system across ten cognitive domains and
reports the JAGGED PROFILE - strength in knowledge, deficit in memory - as its headline result. The
Layer 12 faculty specification is konnectome's acceptance-test document, and the thirteen faculty
suites are queued. The inspiration, and it is only that: when the faculty suites are built, publish
the RESULTS AS A PROFILE rather than a pass count, so konnectome's jaggedness is visible at a glance
and the deficit domains rank the next work. ROUTING: recorded here; no ledger entry needed until
the faculty suites are taken up.

FINDING TL-8. LANGUAGE REMAINS THE FARTHEST GAP, AND NOTHING NEW CHANGES ITS RANK. Class:
FUNCTION. Kind: CONTENT.
The principles library's grounding-problem chapters are unchanged in substance by the refresh, and
konnectome's nearest pack is symbol_exchange. No new finding; the gap is restated so this analysis
is complete rather than selective, and it stays behind memory, attention, and evaluation in every
ordering this document supports.

FINDING TL-9. THE GOVERNANCE MATERIAL IS OUT OF SCOPE FOR THE BUILD, ON PURPOSE. Class: CONTEXT,
recorded as an idea. Kind: neither - not a gap.
The refreshed library carries substantial 2025-2026 governance content (safety institutes, the
international report, state laws). konnectome is a single-owner research build; none of that content
names a konnectome obligation, and this analysis records that it was READ AND NOT ROUTED, so the
next session does not re-derive the same conclusion.


PART FOUR - WHAT THIS ANALYSIS DOES NOT CHANGE

THE QUEUE OF RECORD STANDS. The current hand-off's Part Six ordering is not displaced by this
