KONNECTOME LEDGER - THE RUNNING SCOREBOARD OF THE BUILD  (v1)

This is the single, canonical, navigable record of every wall the konnectome build
hits, exactly as PrologAI_Requirements_Ledger_v1.txt was for the language. Every wall
becomes an entry here FIRST; from here it is routed to exactly one destination: the
causalontology change order, the PrologAI change order, or a konnectome construct.
A wall shown to be UNNECESSARY is recorded as honestly as a wall that needed building:
honest non-closure is a real result, not a failure. Findings are DISCOVERED by
building, never guessed from the armchair (Second, Third, and Fourth Commandments).

Author: D. R. Dison / ai-university-aiu.

Acronyms are spelled out in full on first use, with the acronym in parentheses, and the
bare acronym thereafter. External proper names are kept verbatim (SWI-Prolog, Ed25519,
Git, GitHub, Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus). A causal_relation_object is a
Causalontology structure record, spelled in full per the Twelfth Commandment. konnectome
is a "Whole-Word System": no abbreviation series, no single-letter series - Loop-1 not
L1, Native-1 not N1, Proto-1 not P1.


================================================================================
SECTION ONE - WHAT THIS IS AND HOW TO READ IT
================================================================================

PART ONE - THE LEDGER'S ROLE

konnectome is a Proto-Artificial-General-Intelligence (Proto-AGI) prototype written in
PrologAI, storing its thoughts as Causalontology records (3.0.0 at the build's opening;
4.0.0 since the slice-12 closure), and built from the
appendices of Nature's Cognitive Architecture. The build method is enforce-then-build,
inherited from the PrologAI program: a wall the build hits becomes an entry here; a
load-bearing entry is routed to exactly one place; the next slice rests on the result
rather than re-inventing it. This ledger is the product's honest scoreboard.

PART TWO - STATUS MEANINGS

 - OPEN: recorded, not yet resolved. The build hit a wall; no destination decided yet.
 - ROUTED: the finding passed its gate and was written to one destination - the
   causalontology change order (Second Commandment), the PrologAI change order (Third
   Commandment), or a named konnectome construct (Fourth Commandment).
 - CLOSED: the destination delivered - a Causalontology change-order entry, a PrologAI
   change-order entry, or a konnectome module - and the build now rests on it. Every
   CLOSED entry names what closed it.
 - UNNECESSARY: the wall was shown, by building, not to need a change at all. Recorded
   as honestly as a wall that needed building.

PART THREE - THE THREE ROUTING DESTINATIONS (route each finding to EXACTLY ONE)

 - DATA-STRUCTURE gap (must be SHARED across agents or repositories as a signed,
   evidence-graded, content-addressed record, not merely computed inside one konnectome
   runtime) -> docs/Causalontology_4_0_0_CHANGE_ORDER_v3.txt. Gated by the Thirteenth
   Commandment: must keep the conformance suite green (one hundred nineteen vectors at
   3.0.0; one hundred thirty-seven since the 4.0.0 re-freeze - see Section Two).
 - LANGUAGE gap (a real, load-bearing PrologAI-language wall) ->
   docs/PrologAI_CHANGE_ORDER_v2.txt. Additive only, gated by the Thirteenth
   Commandment: must not regress ARC-AGI-1 (400 of 400) or ARC-AGI-2 (120 of 120)
   through Mentova.
 - konnectome-SOLVABLE gap (expressible inside konnectome's own code) -> a named
   konnectome pack or module, recorded in the coverage list of Section Four.

Wall-3 (2026-07-22, from slice 12, the closure build itself) - the closure's own wall, discovered
and CLOSED the same day. With Causalontology 4.0.0 released - its closed kind list grown from
eighteen to twenty-one, now carrying attitude, predicted_occurrence, and prediction_error, and its
conformance suite re-frozen at 137 vectors - konnectome turned to MINT those three kinds through its
reused library, and could not: PrologAI's causal_core 1.0.0 implemented only the eighteen 3.0.0
kinds, and causal_core_identity_fields/2 carried no rows for the three new ones, so
causal_core_identify failed on an attitude, a predicted_occurrence, and a prediction_error alike.
This was neither a data-structure gap (the standard already carried the kinds) nor a
konnectome-solvable gap (konnectome may not edit its cousins); it was a LANGUAGE-LIBRARY gap - the
PrologAI library had not yet caught up to the released standard - so it routed to the PrologAI change
order rather than the Causalontology one. It is the third routing destination the Ledger names, used
for the first time. CONSUMER-VERSUS-ONTOLOGY TEST: not applicable - the ontology already carried the
kinds; the gap was purely the language library's additive lag behind a shipped standard. STATUS:
OPEN (2026-07-22) -> ROUTED (2026-07-22) to docs/PrologAI_CHANGE_ORDER_v2.txt, additive only, gated
by the Thirteenth Commandment -> CLOSED (2026-07-22) by causal_core 1.1.0 (PrologAI pull request 595,
merge commit cd74f86), which added the three causal_core_identity_fields/2 rows additively, kept the
vendored conformance suite green at 137 of 137, and passed its honest mini regression - ARC-AGI-1 40
of 40 and ARC-AGI-2 12 of 12, a 10 percent spot-check with the full regression deferred, never
rounded up. Discovered by building, delivered through a gated change order, and released the same day.

Modes deep-read note (2026-08-09). THE MODES DEEP READ IS EXECUTED, and the design authority for
the construct-level mode register is docs/konnectome_mode_register_design_authority_v1.txt. The

THE HEADLINE: KONNECTOME DOES NOT HAVE TO DESIGN A MODE REGISTER, BECAUSE THE CORPUS SPECIFIES ONE,
in identical words, at every grain: "STATE is the continuous instantaneous condition; MODE is the
discrete operating regime; the formalism is the hybrid automaton - mode register, per-mode transfer
functions, transition table, fault watchdogs - under the admission rule that a regime enters the
register only if the healthy system uses it." A mode entry carries three fields (formal name, coined
plain-English name, one-clause gloss) and nothing else; rules, parameters and confidence live in a
parallel transfer-function block; transitions are a separate table of trigger, direction, TIMESCALE
and AGENCY. Guards are not a field, and konnectome must not design a column the corpus cannot fill.

THE CHEAPEST FINDING: THE REGISTER IS SMALL. At the cell grain the distribution across 386 entries is
hard-bounded - 19 entries of one mode, 196 of two, 149 of three, 22 of four, none of zero and none of
five or more. Median two. And a register of one is FIRST-CLASS, not degenerate: "A register of one,
stated proudly", said three times. konnectome's existing single-rule constructs are therefore valid
mode-poor entries rather than a debt, and the whole repository can become a valid register system
without changing a number.

THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL FINDING: THE NESTING QUESTION IS ANSWERED, AND THE ANSWER IS CHEAP. There is
NO derivation function from a parent construct's mode to a child's, and the corpus denies the
reduction twice ("emerging from - but not reducible to"; and a composite mode "exists only when the
cell's mode, the reticular gate's state, and the cortical context align", which runs upward, not
downward). What the corpus describes is common-cause coordination: one broadcast written once and
read many times, plus an admissibility guard on composite modes. konnectome already owns the
broadcast - the neuromodulator bus, with its global level, per-territory level and diffuse fallback,
is structurally the corpus's own state_broadcast_service and needs no new component.

THE HONEST SCORE: THE OPERATING-SYSTEM SPECIFICATION HAS BEEN IMPLEMENTED AS A POWER-MANAGEMENT
DRIVER. The state machinery of slices 35 through 38 is built well and to the corpus's own design -
roughly four and a half of Layer 11's twenty-six entries, and all of them machinery rather than
states - while the register itself is absent. Nothing writes a per-construct mode to any target: the
offline engine READS the state atom, which is a subscriber pulling rather than a register pushing,
and the corpus's contrast is exact - "the hardware itself is retuned by the broadcast, every cell
shifting mode, rather than merely being clocked faster". The online/offline atom is also not
extensible by adding atoms, because rapid-eye-movement sleep breaks any linear ordering.

TOPOLOGY (values directed and broadcast) and reserve MODE for the corpus sense. VERIFIED DURING THIS

Observation-6 (2026-08-09, from the owner's question). A CAPABILITY QUESTION OF UTMOST IMPORTANCE,
so designated by the owner and recorded here to be TRACKED and RE-ANSWERED rather than answered

THE HONEST ANSWER TODAY, IN TWO HALVES, BECAUSE THE QUESTION SPLITS.

(1) IN THE CONVENTIONAL SENSE - classifying arbitrary human text as positive, negative or neutral -
NO, and not soon. konnectome has no language model and no sentiment lexicon. Its language stands at
Rung Two: first grounded words, a handful of them. The Ninth Commandment survey's COVERAGE BUG SIX,
language production beyond the word, is the standing entry that owns this gap, and nothing on the
current queue closes it quickly.

(2) IN THE DEEPER SENSE - attributing an affective stance to a situation or to another agent, and
justifying the attribution - YES, AND IT IS ALREADY BUILT, by slices 16 and 19. situation_appraisal
reads a situation's valence straight from the drives' own set-point error: a violated set-point is a
threat the mind FEARS, a met or improving one is good and DESIRED, and no bespoke emotion pathway
was invented, because the feeling machinery IS the drive machinery. empathy attributes affect to
ANOTHER agent: modelling another's distress lowers the mind's own belonging, and the attribution is
minted as a shareable attitude - the agent fears her connection severed - asserted at observation
grade and RETRACTED when misattributed.

THE INVERSION, WHICH IS THE ARCHITECTURALLY INTERESTING PART AND THE REASON THIS IS WORTH TRACKING.
Conventional sentiment analysis runs text into a statistical classifier and out to a label, with no
account of why the label is right and no mechanism for withdrawing it. konnectome runs a modelled
situation into a drive-error appraisal and out to an ATTITUDE RECORD carrying its own provenance, an
evidence grade, and a retraction path. It does not RECOGNIZE sentiment from surface features; it
OCCUPIES a state and reports it, and it can hand a reader the content-addressed record that
justifies the attribution. That is a stronger claim than a classifier can make and a far narrower
one, and the narrowness is the trade the whole project has already chosen.

OBSERVATION-8 (2026-08-09, from slice 40's validation against the corpus). KONNECTOME'S GATE IS A
SYMMETRIC TOGGLE, NOT A HYSTERETIC LATCH, AND THE CORPUS'S IS THE LATTER.

WHAT WAS FOUND. Slice 40 validated the gate's register block by block against the Layer 8 modes
volume's Entry 36, the mutual-inhibition flip-flop, coined the Either-Or Latch and described as "a
hysteretic toggle whose modes are simply its two self-holding states". Every block matched except
one. The corpus's latch is HYSTERETIC: over a range of inputs the output has two stable solutions and
which one is realised depends on history, so it turns on at one boundary and off only at a lower one.
The Layer 6 volume states the same property in plainer words at the cell grain - "switching on takes
more input than keeping it on, the defining hysteresis". konnectome's archetype_gate/4 compares one
switching drive against ONE threshold in both directions, so its two transition rows honestly share a
single trigger and the same drive that closes an open gate opens a closed one. The pack's own oldest
test is named gate_resists_flipping and its comment says the gate "then resists flipping back", but
what it actually demonstrates is only that a SUB-threshold drive does not flip it - which is true of
any threshold switch and is not hysteresis.

WHY IT IS NOT FIXED HERE, and this is the substance of the observation rather than an excuse. The
corpus itself locates the stickiness OUTSIDE the switch: the Layer 11 reading is explicit that "this
is a stabiliser, not a switch, so it has no poles of its own", and that it publishes a scalar
stability bias onto the configuration bus, gated by circadian phase and hunger. Building a second
threshold inside archetype_gate/4 would therefore model the corpus WRONGLY as well as breaking slice
40's behaviour-pinned promise - it would bury in the switch the very thing the corpus insists is a
separate construct. The design authority's Part Nine already schedules THE STABILISER AS A SEPARATE
CONSTRUCT as its slice six, publishing a modulable hysteresis scalar onto the bus and replacing the
two constants currently sitting inside the two-process governor's parameter block.

STATUS: LOGGED, NOT ROUTED, NOT FIXED. No wall was hit; this is konnectome-internal work under the
Fourth Commandment. THE CLOSER: the stabiliser slice (design authority Part Nine slice six). When it
lands it should give the gate its second threshold by READING the published stability bias rather
than by carrying a constant, add the second trigger to the gate's transition table as its own row,
and repair the comment on gate_resists_flipping so the test's name and its claim agree. A session
that builds the stabiliser for the two-process governor alone and does not return to the gate has

DECISION-1 (2026-08-09, slice 41). COMMAND VERSUS BIAS: A THROWN MODE IS A COMMAND AT THE DISCRETE
GRAIN, AND BIAS IS THE CHANNEL KONNECTOME ALREADY HAS. THIS IS KONNECTOME'S OWN DECISION AND MUST
NEVER BE PRESENTED AS THE CORPUS'S INSTRUCTION.

WHY A DECISION WAS REQUIRED. The mode-register design authority's Part Seven item 1 records the
corpus as genuinely silent, with both readings appearing literally and sometimes in the same volume:
"keeps it in the Pinpoint Report, set from above" is command-like, "biases it toward flashing" is
bias-like. The Theme Two read raised the same fork independently at item 8 for the offline broadcast,
and the Theme Four read raised it a third time. THE ONLY CONCRETE EVIDENCE ANY OF THE THREE FOUND is
Layer 8 Chapter 16's "neuromodulators move the circuit between regimes by scaling recurrent gain",
which is BIAS IN FORM - a gain scalar, not a mode name - and which arrives at a DIFFERENT GRAIN from
the one where the decision must be made. It is therefore evidence and not an instruction, and it is
recorded here so that a later session cannot mistake it for one.

THE THREE REASONS, ALL KONNECTOME'S OWN. FIRST, IT UN-CONFLATES TWO OPERATIONS THE DESIGN AUTHORITY'S
PART EIGHT SAYS ARE CURRENTLY CONFLATED - continuous modulation and discrete throw on one channel.
konnectome ALREADY OWNS BIAS, built and running since slice 4: relay_modulated reads a level off this
same bus and scales its gain where it stands. A bias-shaped throw would have left konnectome with two
bias mechanisms, no command mechanism, and a permanent conflation. SECOND, A COMMAND COSTS NO
INVENTED NUMBER AND A BIAS COSTS AT LEAST ONE: a bias moves a threshold BY some amount the corpus
does not supply at this grain, and this family's standing rule is that no number in the pack is
konnectome's invention. THIRD, COMMAND IS THE REVERSIBLE CHOICE: a bias agency can later write rows
into the same table beside broadcast_thrown, and nothing built on the command has to move; the
reverse would require inventing the threshold anyway.

DECISION-1's COROLLARY, THE CONFLICT RULE (Part Seven item 2, also konnectome's own). THE THROW
PREEMPTS SELF-SELECTION WHILE IT STANDS. The corpus shows preemption in one place and negotiated
arbitration in another and states no general rule. konnectome's stated reason for preemption is not a
claim about biology: preemption is the rule that can be READ WITHOUT RUNNING THE CONSTRUCT, which the
owner's standing visualization request demands of every slice in this family. NEGOTIATED ARBITRATION
IS DEFERRED BY NAME, NOT DENIED, and the channel deliberately holds exactly ONE standing throw per
kind - newest replacing older, as every broadcast on this bus does - so the two-sources case cannot
arise unnoticed.

WHAT DECISION-1 DOES NOT DECIDE, stated so a later session does not inherit more than was chosen. It
does not decide whether a throw may be addressed to an INDIVIDUAL construct: it may not, here,
because a broadcast that names one construct is a wire. It does not decide whether a throw may be
TERRITORIAL: no reading has asked for one, and the key shape would extend cleanly if one ever did. It
does not decide overlay-versus-replacement at the global grain (Part Seven item 3), which the master
register slice must still invent. And it does not decide anything about learnability, which the
Theme Three read establishes is carried by the TYPE and not by a mode.

THE THROW IS A ROW, NOT A DOOR - the structural consequence worth carrying. Slice 40 made the
transition table the sole authority on direction and predicted the throw would be a row in it. It is.
There is no override flag beside the register and no second entry point into the gate, so A CONSTRUCT
THAT DECLARES NO THROWN ROW SIMPLY CANNOT BE COMMANDED, and it says so through its own register
rather than through a list kept elsewhere of who may command whom. archetype STILL DOES NOT IMPORT
THE BUS: a rule library that reached for a global channel would stop being one, so the throw arrives
as an argument and the join belongs to the caller, with the archetype suite standing in for one.

THE THREE SILENCES, AND WHY THEY ARE THREE AND NOT ONE. A channel nobody has written reads back the
reserved atom no_mode_thrown, and the WRITE of that name is refused aloud, so a register declaring a
mode of the same name could never be commanded by accident. A throw naming the mode already held is
satisfied without a transition, which is what makes a standing broadcast safe to read on EVERY tick
rather than only on the tick it was written - a property a bus with no clock of its own must have. A
throw naming a mode the register does not declare is refused aloud. Three silences would have been a
defect; two silences and a refusal is the design.

AND A COMMAND ENDS. neuromodulator_bus_release_mode_throw/3 withdraws a standing throw and returns
the kind to its own self-selection. Without it the first broadcast would have taken a construct's
self-selection permanently, which is a far larger claim than this slice makes.

A NEW NAMED LENS, EARNED THIS SLICE: THE DECLARATION-ORDER LENS. A LOOKUP THAT IS RIGHT BECAUSE OF
THE ORDER THINGS WERE WRITTEN IN IS NOT RIGHT, IT IS LUCKY. Slice 40's departure lookup took the
FIRST row leaving a mode. That was correct while every row shared one agency, and it REMAINS correct

THE DIRECTIVE IS NOW A REFUSAL RATHER THAN A HABIT. The Layer 6 template forbids admitting a fault as

indefinitely. A machine with an official way to be broken never reports being broken.

THE THIRD OUTCOME IS THE DESIGN. A regime is WARNED, CLEAN, or UNWATCHED, and the third is the one a
two-outcome design would lose. An empty warning list cannot distinguish "watched and healthy" from
"nobody looked", and the second is the dangerous one. The unwatched block makes silence name itself.
The same reasoning governs the channel's default: a construct kind nobody has watched reads back as
two empty blocks, which is the statement that no watch happened - never a clean bill of health.

for tidiness. The bus is Component 4, the chemical broadcast field read by every construct's update
and learning rule. A warning is not a chemical and is not addressed to the constructs at all; it is
addressed OUTWARD. Putting it on the bus would make every construct's update rule a potential reader
of its own failure report.

channel as a routing candidate IF a published warning must be a shareable record rather than a

seconds cannot be stated in a build with no seconds. THE RULE THIS APPLIES, worth carrying: A CHEAP
ITEM THAT ARRIVES FROM A THIRD INDEPENDENT DIRECTION HAS STOPPED BEING A QUEUE ITEM AND HAS BECOME A
DEPENDENCY. It was promoted, not re-prioritised on taste.

WHAT WAS BUILT, AND IT IS DELIBERATELY SMALL. tick_engine gains three exported predicates and no new
pack: tick_engine_ticks_per_second/1, the convention itself, stated once; and the two conversions
tick_engine_ticks_from_milliseconds/2 and tick_engine_milliseconds_from_ticks/2. Seven Prolog Unit
(PLUnit) tests, tick_engine growing from eight to fifteen; SEVEN HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN tests across
THIRTY-ONE packs; four gates at zero; the capstone story byte-identical against a main worktree.

DECISION-2 (2026-08-09, slice 43). THE TICKS-PER-SECOND CONVENTION: ONE HUNDRED TICKS TO THE NOMINAL
SECOND, SO ONE TICK IS TEN NOMINAL MILLISECONDS, AND THE SCHEDULER STAYS DIMENSIONLESS. THIS IS
KONNECTOME'S OWN DECISION AND MUST NEVER BE PRESENTED AS THE CORPUS'S INSTRUCTION.

WHY A DECISION WAS REQUIRED. The corpus specifies the cognitive cluster down to its constants and
every one of them is wall-clock - ignition near three hundred milliseconds, admission in the low
hundreds, unrehearsed decay in seconds, a board wiped on task switch in under a second - while
konnectome's tick is dimensionless-ordinal by the observer's own written declaration. The corpus says
nothing whatever about how often a synthetic mind should update, because it is describing a
continuous system and not a scheduler, so THERE IS NO READING OF THE CORPUS THAT COULD SUPPLY THIS
NUMBER. It is not a silence to be resolved by reading harder; it is a question the source does not
contain. That is precisely the situation the Fourth Commandment's ledger exists for.

THE THREE REASONS, ALL KONNECTOME'S OWN. FIRST, IT MAKES EVERY CORPUS CONSTANT A WHOLE NUMBER OF
TICKS: three hundred milliseconds is thirty ticks exactly, one hundred is ten, one second is one
hundred, half a second is fifty. A rate that left a corpus constant sitting between two ticks would
force a rounding at every site that used it, AND A ROUNDING IS AN INVENTED NUMBER WEARING A SMALL
COAT - the constant would leave the corpus as the corpus's and arrive in the pack as konnectome's,
with nobody having decided anything. SECOND, IT IS THE COARSEST RATE THAT ACHIEVES THE FIRST, and
coarsest is the honest choice rather than the convenient one: the corpus states these quantities to a
hundred-millisecond grain, so a millisecond tick would claim a precision the source does not have and
would invite later slices to write constants finer than anything they could justify. THIRD, IT COSTS
THE RUNNING MACHINE NOTHING AND IS THEREFORE REVERSIBLE: no existing behaviour moves by a single
count, the capstone story is byte-identical, and every duration konnectome ever expresses in real
units passes through ONE conversion predicate - so a session that must restate the rate has exactly
one predicate to change and one test to re-read, rather than a search across thirty-one packs.

THE REFUSAL IS THE LOAD-BEARING HALF, and it is the half that makes this a decision with teeth rather
than a constant in a file. A DURATION THAT IS NOT A WHOLE NUMBER OF TICKS IS REFUSED ALOUD:
domain_error(whole_tick_duration, Milliseconds), never rounded. Rounding is exactly how invented
numbers enter a careful build - a caller asks for five milliseconds, is handed one tick without being
told, and a number that was the corpus's has silently become konnectome's. The refusal forces that
decision into the open, where it can be recorded. Both conversions also refuse an UNBOUND argument,
the unbound-wrong-judgement lens paying for an ELEVENTH SLICE RUNNING (33 through 43): an unbound
duration would have invented both a duration and a tick count for it.

WHAT DECISION-2 DOES NOT DECIDE, stated so a later session does not inherit more than was chosen. IT
DOES NOT MAKE KONNECTOME REAL-TIME: nothing sleeps, nothing measures, nothing consults a wall clock,
and the scheduler remains a counter of two-pass synchronous updates. A tick is not ten milliseconds
long; ten milliseconds is what a tick is CALLED when a duration must be stated. IT DOES NOT DECIDE
THAT ANY PARTICULAR CORPUS CONSTANT IS RIGHT - each remains the corpus's claim, and a slice that
adopts one still cites where it came from. IT DOES NOT DECIDE THE OBSERVER'S STAMP, which keeps its
own nominal one-second-per-tick carrier and is now numerically at odds with the convention - see
OBSERVATION-10, recorded rather than quietly fixed. IT DOES NOT DECIDE A WINDOW LENGTH for any
watchdog: it supplies the UNIT a window can be stated in, and OBSERVATION-9's closer still has to
choose the number and say why. And it does not decide whether any construct may have a rate of its
own that differs from the engine's, which the corpus's several timescales will eventually raise.

OBSERVATION-10 (2026-08-09, from slice 43). THE OBSERVER'S CLOCK AND THE CONVENTION NOW DISAGREE, AND
THE DISAGREEMENT IS RECORDED RATHER THAN FIXED. packs/observer/prolog/observer.pl advances its
absolute instant by ONE NOMINAL SECOND PER ORDINAL TICK, and says in its own comment that this is a
nominal, reproducible carrier for a token_occurrence's mandatory absolute interval and nothing more.
Under DECISION-2 a tick is ten nominal milliseconds, so the observer's timestamps run one hundred
times faster than the convention's durations. NOTHING IS WRONG TODAY, because the observer's instant
is an IDENTITY CARRIER and never a duration: no konnectome predicate subtracts two of them, and the
record's only requirement is that each tick get a distinct, reproducible instant. THE DANGER IS
NAMED: the first reader that treats the trace as a timeline and measures an interval across it will
get an answer one hundred times too large, and will get it silently. THE CLOSER IS NAMED AND ITS COST
IS NAMED WITH IT: the slice that restates the observer's stamp in the convention's units RE-MINTS
EVERY CONTENT-ADDRESSED TOKEN OCCURRENCE IDENTIFIER IN THE REPOSITORY, because the instant is one of
the two identity-bearing fields. That is a real cost and it is why this slice does not pay it
speculatively. The cheaper closer, and probably the right one, is the opposite direction: state in
the observer that its instant is an ORDINAL CARRIER WHICH MAY NEVER BE READ AS A DURATION, and make
that a refusal rather than a comment. Either way the choice belongs to a slice that has a reason.

WHAT THIS SLICE DELIBERATELY DID NOT BUILD, so each gap is a decision. No construct was given a
duration: adopting a corpus constant is the working-memory slice's job and doing it here would have
smuggled a faculty in under a convention. No per-construct rate. No window and no watchdog, so
OBSERVATION-9 stands exactly where slice 42 left it - this slice removes its blocker and closes
nothing. No change to the observer, per OBSERVATION-10. And no change to the scheduler at all, which
is pinned by a test rather than asserted: the heartbeat still counts ticks, and five ticks still
reads five.

THE CONSTITUTION'S NUMBER RULE IS WHAT THIS SLICE IS REALLY ABOUT. konnectome's standing discipline
is that no number in a pack is konnectome's invention. That discipline was about to be broken
QUIETLY, not loudly: not by somebody inventing a constant, but by everybody rounding one. A
convention declared in the open, with its reasons and its refusal, is how a build keeps a promise it
would otherwise break by accident.

SLICE 44 NOTE (2026-08-09). THE STABILISER - the design authority's Part Nine slice six, THE CLOSER
OF OBSERVATION-8, and the THIRD numbered konnectome decision.

WHAT WAS BUILT. A new pack, stabiliser, at layer zero: the finger on the switch, which holds no pole
of its own. It publishes ONE scalar - the stability bias - onto the neuromodulator bus, and it owns
the band that two switches used to carry as constants of their own. archetype gains
archetype_gate_hysteretic/5 and archetype_gate_effective_threshold/4, the gate as the corpus's LATCH.
Eighteen new Prolog Unit (PLUnit) tests in the stabiliser and eight more in archetype (forty-two to
fifty); SEVEN HUNDRED AND THIRTY-NINE tests across THIRTY-TWO packs; four gates at zero with the
naming gate passing its own self-test; the capstone story byte-identical against a main worktree.

WHY IT IS A PACK AND NOT A CONSTANT. The corpus is explicit that hysteresis is not part of the
switch: "this is a stabiliser, not a switch, so it has no poles of its own", publishing a scalar
stability bias onto the configuration bus. konnectome's hysteresis was two constants inside the
two-process governor's parameter block and one symmetric threshold inside the gate - the stickiness
buried in the switch, which is the arrangement the corpus names and rejects. OBSERVATION-8 recorded
in slice 40 that a second threshold could NOT honestly be added inside archetype_gate/4, because that
would have modelled the corpus wrongly while appearing to fix it. This slice is the closer that
observation named, and it closes it from the outside, which is where the corpus put it.

WHAT A STABILITY BIAS IS, AND ITS UNITS ARE STATED because a scalar without stated units is an
invitation to misuse. IT IS HOW FAR APART THE TWO BOUNDARIES SIT: half the width of the hysteresis
band, in the units of whatever quantity the switch it steadies compares. A gate reads it as an offset
on its threshold; a governor reads it as the half-width of its band about a midpoint. One meaning,
two consumers, no conversion.

DECISION-1 IS APPLIED AND NOT RE-OPENED, exactly as the Iridium hand-off instructed. A hysteresis
scalar is a BIAS, so it travels as a LEVEL under a plain-atom key on the channel konnectome has had
since slice 4, and never as a thrown mode. The suite proves it is a level rather than asserting it:
the published value reads back through the bus's ORDINARY level read, with nothing about it needing
the stabiliser to interpret it.

AND THE STABILISER'S AGENCY IS STATED, which the hand-off also required, because after slice 41 a
publisher has two shapes available and they are not the same thing. ITS AGENCY IS broadcast_bias, AND
IT IS AN AGENCY IN NO TRANSITION TABLE - pinned by a test that reads the gate's table, checks the
table is not empty so the test cannot pass vacuously, and asserts the stabiliser's name is absent.
THAT IS THE SHARPEST AVAILABLE STATEMENT OF WHAT A BIAS IS. Since slice 40 a construct is moved only
by a row of its own register's table. The stabiliser moves nothing. It changes WHERE A BOUNDARY SITS,
after which the construct's own row fires, or does not, by its own rule.

DECISION-3 (2026-08-09, slice 44). WHICH GRAINS THE PUBLISHED SCALAR GOVERNS: THE CONSTRUCT GRAIN
ONLY. THIS IS KONNECTOME'S OWN DECISION AND MUST NEVER BE PRESENTED AS THE CORPUS'S INSTRUCTION.

WHY A DECISION WAS REQUIRED. The version 2 analysis named this the sharpest ambiguity of its visit:
THE CORPUS ANSWERS THE HYSTERESIS QUESTION DIFFERENTLY AT TWO GRAINS. At the construct grain the
Layer 11 reading puts the stickiness OUTSIDE the switch - a stabiliser with no poles of its own. At
the motif grain, Layer 8 Chapter 16 gives winner-take-all's transfer function as a hysteretic max
operation with an explicitly hysteretic Commitment Mode - stickiness INSIDE the motif, part of the
transfer function itself. Both are literal and neither is a misreading.

THE THREE REASONS, ALL KONNECTOME'S OWN. FIRST, THE TWO READINGS STOP CONFLICTING ONCE THE GRAIN IS
NAMED: a scalar published to steady a whole configuration and a hysteresis written into one motif's
transfer function are different mechanisms doing similar work at different scales, and collapsing
them would force konnectome to pick a winner where the corpus supplies two. SECOND, A BIAS THAT
REACHED INSIDE A TRANSFER FUNCTION WOULD MAKE A MOTIF'S OWN LAW DEPEND ON A GLOBAL VALUE, which is
the coupling this build has spent slices avoiding: a transfer function is what a construct IS while a
mode holds, and a global channel must not be able to rewrite it. THIRD, IT IS THE REVERSIBLE CHOICE:
the motif catalogue is unbuilt, so a later slice can extend the roster of governed grains, whereas a
scalar already reaching everywhere could not be withdrawn without breaking whatever depended on it.

DECISION-3 IS A REFUSAL AND NOT A COMMENT. stabiliser_check_grain/1 turns the motif grain away by
name with domain_error(stabiliser_governed_grain, motif_grain). A decision written as prose is a
sentence people forget; written as a refusal it is one the code keeps.

WHAT DECISION-3 DOES NOT DECIDE. It does not decide what the motif grain's hysteresis SHOULD be -
that belongs to the motif catalogue family, which must decide it as konnectome's own. It does not
decide the region or system grains, neither of which has a construct that could hold a register yet.
And it does not decide whether a second, differently-scoped stabiliser may exist later.

OBSERVATION-8 IS CLOSED, AND HOW IT WAS CLOSED IS THE INTERESTING PART. archetype_gate_hysteretic/5
gives the gate two boundaries where it had one. AT A BIAS OF ZERO THE TWO COINCIDE AND THE LATCH IS
THE OLD SYMMETRIC TOGGLE EXACTLY, in every combination of mode and drive, which is pinned by a test -
and since the bus answers zero for a bias nobody has published, EVERY SWITCH IN THE REPOSITORY CAN
CONSULT THE STABILISER WITHOUT ANY OF THEM CHANGING BEHAVIOUR. That is what made the slice safe, and
it is why the capstone story is byte-identical.

THE MAPPING KONNECTOME CHOSE, AND THE ONE IT REJECTED, WHICH IS THE MOST INSTRUCTIVE THING IN THIS
SLICE. The corpus states hysteresis for a LEVEL comparator - a quantity rising past one boundary and
falling past a lower one - while archetype's gate compares a SWITCHING DRIVE, which is a push rather
than a level. The two do not map by themselves. KONNECTOME CHOSE: LEAVING A MODE COSTS THE RAISED
BOUNDARY, WHICHEVER MODE IT IS, so over the whole range between the threshold and the threshold plus
the bias BOTH MODES ARE STABLE AND HISTORY DECIDES WHICH IS REALISED - the corpus's own definition of
the property, quoted almost word for word.

THE REJECTED READING IS RECORDED BECAUSE IT IS THE ONE A LATER SESSION WILL REACH FOR FIRST. Making
turn-on cost Threshold plus the bias and turn-off cost Threshold MINUS it reads exactly like the
corpus's higher-and-lower boundaries, and it was tried. With a push-drive rather than a level it
makes CLOSED ABSORBING over the whole middle range: every mid-sized push closes the gate and none
reopens it. A GATE THAT SETTLES AND NEVER LEAVES IS THE CORPUS'S OWN STUCK SWITCH FAULT SIGNATURE,
which this pack has listed in its fault block since slice 42. A HYSTERESIS DESIGN THAT MANUFACTURES A
FAULT REGIME IS NOT A HYSTERESIS DESIGN. It was caught by a test written before the code was trusted,
and the non-absorbing property is now pinned for every mode at every bias.

THE BOUNDARY IS WRITTEN PER MODE EVEN THOUGH THE TWO CLAUSES AGREE TODAY. That is deliberate and it
serves the owner's standing visualization request: a watcher holding nothing but a mode name and a
bias can read that mode's boundary without running the construct, and any future asymmetry has to be
written down mode by mode rather than smuggled into one expression.

THE GOVERNOR'S TWO CONSTANTS ARE NOW DERIVED RATHER THAN DECLARED, AND NOT ONE NUMBER CHANGED. The
sleep threshold of sixteen and the wake threshold of two are restated as what they always were: a
band about a midpoint, a midpoint of nine and a half-width of seven. THEY WERE NOT REPLACED BY
DIFFERENT NUMBERS, because different numbers would be konnectome inventing where it had no need to.
The suite proves the derivation reproduces the original block field for field AND that a governor
standing on the derived band runs an identical twenty-four-tick day, state for state - because
identical parameters are only evidence and identical behaviour is the proof.

A HONEST INTERACTION, PINNED RATHER THAN LEFT TO BE DISCOVERED: THE TWO CONSUMERS HAVE DIFFERENT
FLOORS. A gate reads a bias of zero as "no hysteresis" and behaves as it always did. THE GOVERNOR
CANNOT, because its own guard has refused a flat or inverted band since it was built, and a flat band
chatters. So the same scalar is legal at zero for one consumer and refused at zero by the other, and
a test pins the governor's refusal by its own name rather than leaving somebody to meet it as a
surprise. This is not a defect; it is what it looks like when two constructs share a scalar and each
keeps its own domain.

TWO REFUSALS FOR ONE VALUE, DELIBERATELY NAMED SEPARATELY. stabiliser_check_bias/1 refuses a negative
bias as domain_error(stabiliser_bias, Bias); archetype_gate_check_stability_bias/1 refuses the same
value as domain_error(archetype_gate_stability_bias, Bias). ARCHETYPE STILL DOES NOT IMPORT THE BUS
AND NOW ALSO DOES NOT IMPORT THE STABILISER - a rule library that reached for a global channel would
stop being one - so it must judge what it is handed, and the two names are kept apart so neither
refusal can hide the other in a stack trace.

THE UNBOUND-WRONG-JUDGEMENT LENS PAID FOR A TWELFTH SLICE RUNNING (33 through 44), four times in this
slice's new code: an unbound bias at the band, at the publication, at the governor derivation and at
the gate would each have been bound by arithmetic and would each have invented a band.

WHAT THIS SLICE DELIBERATELY DID NOT BUILD, so each gap is a decision. THE CORPUS GATES THE
STABILISER BY CIRCADIAN PHASE AND HUNGER, AND NEITHER GATING IS BUILT. Hunger has no construct in
konnectome at all, and gating by phase would require a function the corpus does not supply at this
grain - so the scalar is published by a caller who chooses it, and the gating is deferred BY NAME
rather than invented. No construct was rewired to consult the stabiliser: the gate and the governor
can now be handed a bias, and joining them to the bus belongs to the caller, as every join in this
family has since slice 41. No motif-grain hysteresis, per DECISION-3. And OBSERVATION-9 stands where
slice 42 left it - the stabiliser is not a watchdog and counts nothing.

SLICE 45 NOTE (2026-08-09). THE FIRST REAL WATCHDOG - and with it THE FOURTH NUMBERED KONNECTOME
DECISION, and THE CLOSURE OF OBSERVATION-9, which is the second observation this build has ever
closed and the second in two consecutive slices.

WHAT WAS BUILT. One new pack, watchdog, at layer zero, carrying ten exported predicates and
twenty-nine Prolog Unit (PLUnit) tests. NO EXISTING PACK WAS EDITED, no world dict key was added, no
number was invented and no number was changed. SEVEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT tests across THIRTY-THREE
packs; four gates at zero plus the naming gate's self-test; the capstone story byte-identical.

WHAT A WATCHDOG IS HERE. A history is a list of observation(Tick, Mode) terms held newest first,
threaded through the caller as A VALUE THE WORLD CARRIES, in the explicit-stateless-value discipline
the eligibility-trace store, the running-average store and the per-territory target store already
follow. From it the pack reads two things and only two: HOW MANY TIMES THE MODE CHANGED inside a
trailing window, and HOW MANY TICKS THE CURRENT MODE HAS BEEN HELD inside it. Both are then offered
under the signatures archetype's own fault block declares - oscillation and stuck_switch - as
supervisor_reading/3 terms the supervisor built at slice 42 already knows how to judge.

NEITHER BOUNDARY IS KONNECTOME'S INVENTION. Both are the corpus's, from the Layer 8 modes volume's

has carried both by name with their watchdogs named since slice 42. This pack is what those two names

DECISION-4 (2026-08-09, slice 45). THE WINDOW BELONGS TO THE CALLER, AND A WINDOW THE HISTORY DOES
NOT YET SPAN IS REPORTED AS NOT-YET-MEASURED, NEVER AS CLEAN. THIS IS KONNECTOME'S OWN DECISION AND
MUST NEVER BE PRESENTED AS THE CORPUS'S INSTRUCTION.

WHY A DECISION WAS REQUIRED, AND WHY IT IS NOT THE DECISION THE HAND-OFF EXPECTED. The continuation's
first task recorded that this slice OWED A NUMBER: DECISION-2 supplied the unit a window is stated in
and left the length to whoever chose it. The slice looked for a warrant to choose one and reports
what it found, because the search is the finding. THE CORPUS'S ONLY QUANTITATIVE STATEMENT ABOUT THIS
LATCH'S TIMESCALES IS ENTRY 36'S OWN: human sleep-wake transitions take seconds to minutes while the
states last minutes to hours, the signature of a sticky latch. That is a claim about ONE latch - the
sleep-wake flip-flop - and konnectome installs the gate archetype in places that have nothing
whatever to do with sleep. Adopting minutes-to-hours as a default window would put it at tens of
thousands of ticks, which would make the capstone's own ten-tick heartbeat permanently unmeasurable,
and it would do so WHILE WEARING A CORPUS CITATION THAT DOES NOT COVER THE CASE. THE GENERAL FORM,
worth carrying: A DEFAULT WITH A BORROWED WARRANT IS AN INVENTED NUMBER THAT HAS LEARNED TO CITE.
Slice 43 met the same shape from the other side and named it - a rounding is an invented number
wearing a small coat - and the two together are one rule seen twice.

THE THREE REASONS, ALL KONNECTOME'S OWN. FIRST, CONSISTENCY WITH A SHAPE ALREADY DECIDED: slice 42
put the ALLOWANCE in the caller's hands for exactly this reason, and a pack that takes its allowance
from the caller and its window from itself would be splitting one judgement across two owners. The
caller that owns a watchdog owns its numbers, both of them. SECOND, THE NUMBER IS NOT ONE NUMBER: a
window that suits the capstone's ten-tick heartbeat cannot suit a governor's twenty-four-tick day,
and a single default would be wrong for at least one of them while looking authoritative for both.
THIRD, IT IS REVERSIBLE AND ITS ALTERNATIVE IS NOT: a later slice with a real caller and a real
reason can declare a default and hand it to callers who want one, whereas a default declared here
would be inherited silently by every caller that never thought about it.

THE SECOND HALF IS THE LOAD-BEARING ONE, AND IT IS WHAT MAKES THIS A DECISION WITH TEETH RATHER THAN
A DEFERRAL. A HISTORY THAT DOES NOT YET SPAN ITS WINDOW YIELDS NO READING AT ALL. Consider what the
alternative would have done: a history four ticks long, asked for flips over a window of twenty,
could perfectly honestly answer "one flip so far" - and a supervisor comparing one against an
allowance of three would then publish A CLEAN BILL OF HEALTH FOR A BOUNDARY NOBODY HAS WATCHED LONG
ENOUGH TO JUDGE. That is precisely the failure the supervisor's THIRD OUTCOME was built to prevent,
arriving one layer further down and defeating it from underneath. So the short history hands back
nothing, plus a named statement per boundary carrying the window asked for and the span actually
held, and both boundaries then flow through the supervisor's UNWATCHED block - which is where they
belong and which is true. THE GENERAL LESSON: A SYSTEM THAT ONLY KNOWS HOW TO ANSWER WILL ANSWER WHEN
IT SHOULD NOT, AND THE DEFENCE IS NOT A BETTER ANSWER BUT A THIRD OUTCOME - SAID TWICE NOW, AT TWO
LAYERS, BY TWO PACKS.

WHAT DECISION-4 DOES NOT DECIDE, stated so a later session does not inherit more than was chosen. IT
DOES NOT DECIDE ANY WINDOW FOR ANY CONSTRUCT: the first slice that wires a real gate to a real
history must choose one and justify it, and that is a slice of its own. IT DOES NOT DECIDE AN
ESCALATION POLICY - what to DO about a warning remains exactly as undecided as slice 42 left it. IT
DOES NOT DECIDE THAT FLIPS AND HELD-TICKS ARE THE ONLY THINGS WORTH WATCHING; they are the two the
corpus names for this one construct, and a construct whose fault regimes the corpus does not supply
still gets none. IT DOES NOT DECIDE HOW OFTEN A CALLER MUST OBSERVE - see the sampling note below.
And it does not decide whether a history should ever be bounded in length, which is the same
unbounded-store question slice 38's memory store left open and which this pack inherits unchanged.

OBSERVATION-9 IS CLOSED (opened slice 42, unblocked slice 43, closed slice 45). konnectome had a
watcher and no watchdogs; it now has one. The closure is proved at the WATCHER rather than asserted

closure is therefore two-sided: the watchdog can watch, and it still knows when it has not.

OBSERVATION-9 NAMED TWO POSSIBLE CLOSERS AND THIS SLICE CHOSE THE FIRST, WITH THE REASON RECORDED SO
THE CHOICE IS NOT MISTAKEN FOR THE ONLY OPTION. The alternatives were a windowed per-construct
history, or a per-construct counter inside the tick engine. The counter was rejected: it would have
put a construct's history where only the engine could read it, and Theme One's addendum - the owner's
standing visualization-panel constraint, binding from the first mode-register slice onward - forbids
exactly that, requiring every such fact to be a value the world carries and readable without stepping
the construct. The engine-counter design would have been smaller and would have foreclosed the panel.

A SAMPLING PROPERTY, NAMED RATHER THAN HIDDEN, BECAUSE IT IS THE ONE PLACE THIS PACK COULD MISLEAD. A
window is counted in TICKS, but flips are counted between consecutive OBSERVATIONS, and a history
need not hold an observation for every tick. A construct that flips and flips back between two
observations therefore shows NO FLIP AT ALL. This is a property of sampling and not a defect of the
count - the watchdog measures what it was shown - but a caller that wants every flip seen must
observe every tick, and a caller that samples is measuring a lower bound on flips while measuring
ticks-held exactly. Both facts are pinned by tests. THE SHARP EDGE FOR A LATER SLICE: the two
readings do NOT degrade the same way under sampling, so a caller that samples is applying two
different standards of evidence to two allowances it probably chose together.

TIME DOES NOT RUN BACKWARDS AND IT DOES NOT STAND STILL, AND BOTH ARE REFUSALS. An observation at a
tick not strictly later than the newest one held is refused with domain_error(watchdog_advancing_tick,
Tick). The backwards case would rewrite history; the equal case would double-count one tick in every
reading taken afterwards. Both would look like ordinary use while corrupting every verdict
downstream - which is the exact class of silent fault this pack exists to detect in other constructs,
and it would have been careless to leave it undefended in the detector.

THE WATCHDOG AND THE SUPERVISOR MEET AT A TERM AND NOT AT AN IMPORT. watchdog builds
supervisor_reading/3 terms and does NOT import supervisor. Both sit at layer zero so an import would
break no rule, but it would make the watchdog depend on the watcher when the honest direction of
dependence is neither: they meet at a value the world carries, as every store in this repository
does. The coupling is therefore BY SHAPE, and because a coupling by shape is exactly the kind that
rots silently, the shape is pinned by tests in this pack that drive the real supervisor end to end.

written with the obvious three clauses - empty, one element, two-or-more - which is correct and left a
CHOICE POINT behind on every reading, because first-argument indexing sees only the principal functor
and cannot tell a one-element list from a longer one. Eight tests passed WITH A WARNING that they had
succeeded with a choice point, which is the test framework doing the lens's job out loud. The walk
was split in two so each predicate decides on the SHAPE of its first argument alone. THE GENERAL

declaration-order lens - right for the wrong reason is not right - arriving at CLAUSE SELECTION

DECISION-5 (2026-08-09, slice 46). THE POLE IS PRESERVED, THE SUB-MODE IS ADDED BENEATH IT, AND THE
DOWNWARD ASSIGNMENT IS KEYED ON THE WHOLE STATE. THIS IS KONNECTOME'S OWN DECISION AND MUST NEVER BE
PRESENTED AS THE CORPUS'S INSTRUCTION.

THE FIRST HALF. The design authority asked for "a named global state plus a sub-mode" and did not say
whether the existing binary survives. It does, and it becomes the POLE. THREE REASONS, ALL
KONNECTOME'S OWN. FIRST, THE CORPUS'S OWN STRUCTURE: the binary was never an approximation waiting to
be replaced - the corpus catalogues THE SLEEP-WAKE FLIP-FLOP as its own entry, "engineered for fast,
complete, bistable transitions", and catalogues the waking states and the sleep states as two
FAMILIES on either side of it. Dissolving the binary into a flat list of state names would have
discarded a distinction the corpus draws with a dedicated chapter, and would have left konnectome's
own flip-flop - the two-process governor of slice 37 - with nothing to be a flip-flop BETWEEN.
SECOND, IT COSTS NO EXISTING CALLER ANYTHING: every predicate written since slice 35 that asks the
bus for the operating state means "am I awake or asleep" and keeps getting exactly that, because the
channel's original reader hands back THE POLE ALONE and always will. THIRD, IT IS THE REVERSIBLE HALF
OF AN IRREVERSIBLE-LOOKING CHANGE: a sub-mode roster can be extended or corrected later, whereas a
third POLE could not be added without revisiting every construct that has read this channel since
slice 35. Widening BENEATH the binary is additive; widening THROUGH it is not.

THE SECOND HALF ANSWERS THE DESIGN AUTHORITY'S PART SEVEN ITEM 4, which records that the corpus does
not settle whether sub-modes CHANGE the downward assignment or only SCALE its parameters. KONNECTOME
DECIDES THAT THE ASSIGNMENT IS KEYED ON THE WHOLE STATE, POLE AND SUB-MODE TOGETHER, SO A SUB-MODE
MAY CHANGE IT. THE REASON IS MEASURED RATHER THAN PREFERRED: the corpus's own assignments for two
sub-modes of the same pole are not scalings of one another and cannot be made into one. N2 assigns
the thalamus to "its rhythmic spindling mode" and opens a plasticity window for spindle-timed
reactivation; N3 assigns the up-down slow oscillation, systems consolidation and synaptic
renormalisation. Those are different mode names at the same grain, not one assignment at two
strengths. Keying on the pole alone would have made every sub-mode decorative BY CONSTRUCTION. THE
COST IS ACCEPTED OPENLY: the assignment table now grows with the roster rather than with the poles.

WHAT DECISION-5 DOES NOT DECIDE. It does not decide the OVERLAY question of Part Seven item 3 - how
two simultaneously active states compose - because konnectome holds ONE standing state and cannot yet
reach that case; the corpus's arousal, stress and sickness states, which are the ones that plainly
overlay a base state, are unbuilt and the composition rule is deferred WITH them rather than invented
ahead of them. It does not decide the conflict rule between an assignment and a construct's own
transition, because DECISION-1 already did - the throw preempts - and this slice INHERITS that rather
than re-opening it, which is now the third time a numbered decision has been inherited instead of
relitigated. It does not decide that four sub-modes per pole are the right four. And it does not make
konnectome real-time: a sub-mode is a name and nothing here has a duration.

THE ROSTER IS THE CORPUS'S AND THE CUT IS KONNECTOME'S, AND THE TWO ARE SEPARATED SO NEITHER IS
MISTAKEN FOR THE OTHER. Eight entries, from the Layer 11 outline's Parts One and Two, in the
outline's own order, each carrying the outline's own coined formal name. THE CUT FROM TWENTY-SIX TO
EIGHT IS KONNECTOME'S: the other eighteen are state MACHINERY (the flip-flops, the two processes, the

engineered and pathological configurations the corpus flags separately. Taking all twenty-six would
have put a governor and a disease into a register of operating states, which is slice 42's
fault-is-not-a-mode error arriving one grain higher up.

THE TWO DEFAULTS ARE NOT KONNECTOME'S EITHER, AND THAT IS WHY THEY ARE SAFE. Every announcement made

DECISION-6 (2026-08-09, slice 47). THE WINDOW IS NOT A NUMBER - IT IS THE GOVERNOR'S OWN DAY, READ

HAND-OFF PROTOCOL NOTE (2026-08-10, Constitution version 13). THE PROTOCOL GAINS A TWO-LOCATION RULE
AND A SWEEP. THE CURRENT HAND-OFF LIVES IN /docs/ AND EVERY SUPERSEDED ONE LIVES IN /docs/hand-off/ ,
so exactly one Context Hand-Off document sits in /docs/ at any moment and it is always the one to
read. Step 2 becomes Steps 2a and 2b - SWEEP, THEN WRITE - and Step 4 requires both in one commit.
Not a slice: no pack touched, no test written, no hand-off edited.

WHY THE TWO LOCATIONS. A hand-off is TWO DOCUMENTS IN ONE FILE and this repository spent a day
learning it: a dated REPORT of a session, which is a historical claim and must never be edited
afterwards, and a live INSTRUCTION to the next session - a reading list, a first-task menu, and
sometimes a command to run. ONLY THE NEWEST IS EVER READ AS INSTRUCTION. Putting it somewhere the
others are not makes that distinction a fact on disk rather than a rule somebody has to know. It also
makes hand-offs follow the house rule the ELEVENTH COMMANDMENT already sets for every other document
series here - only the current one lives outside its archive directory - rather than being the single
series with a convention of its own.

AND IT REMOVES A DERIVATION THAT COULD GO WRONG SILENTLY, which is the practical half. "The latest
hand-off" was previously something a fresh session had to WORK OUT by sorting filenames on date and
serial number. That derivation is fallible in three ways this repository has already met: same-day
hand-offs with serial numbers, a serial sequence that is per-date rather than global, and session-
close markers that look exactly like units of work. It is now simply the only dated file in /docs/ .

THE SWEEP IS A PRECONDITION RATHER THAN A POSTCONDITION AND THAT IS THE LOAD-BEARING HALF. The
obvious phrasing - write the new one, then move the old one - reads more naturally and FAILS
SILENTLY: a session that skips the second half leaves two hand-offs in /docs/ , and the next session
reads whichever it happens to open, with nothing anywhere to notice. Sweeping FIRST is SELF-HEALING,
because it moves whatever it finds however many there are, so a skipped step costs one session of
clutter instead of a wrong read - and the clutter is visible at the top of /docs/ rather than buried.

THAT IS THE THIRD TIME IN ONE DAY THIS REPOSITORY HAS CHOSEN THE SAME WAY, and the three together are
now a rule worth stating on its own: PREFER THE FORM WHOSE FAILURE MODE IS VISIBLE OVER THE FORM
WHOSE FAILURE MODE IS A PLAUSIBLE ANSWER. The fulfilment-audit trigger was rewritten from a glob to a
recursive find, because a glob that misses returns ZERO and zero is a plausible count. The Hand-Off
Protocol's write path was called out at version 12 as the most dangerous stale pointer in the
repository, because Step 2 CREATES a file rather than reading one, so a stale path there succeeds in
the wrong place forever rather than failing loudly. And now the sweep is ordered before the write for
the same reason. THREE INSTANCES IS A PATTERN AND NOT A COINCIDENCE: the failures that survive in a
careful repository are the ones that look like success.

WHAT MOVED AND WHAT DID NOT. The current hand-off - Tungsten Nightwarden, 2026-08-10_1 - was swept
back up into /docs/ , which is the first application of the new rule and is itself a pure rename. The
twenty-eight superseded hand-offs stay in /docs/hand-off/ , unedited. The directory's README.txt is
rewritten to say that the current hand-off is NOT in it and where to start instead. The Nineteenth
Commandment's own list-of-important-files bullet now points a hand-off author at that README.

AND ONE SENTENCE ELSEWHERE IN THE COMMANDMENT BECAME LITERALLY TRUE. It promises that a fresh session
"re-loads state from docs/ - the ledger, the DSPARCD Fileset, the tutorial, and the latest hand-off".
That was approximately true and is now exact: the latest hand-off is in docs/, is the only one there,
and needs no working out. A rule that makes an existing promise exact is a better rule than one that
adds a new promise.
