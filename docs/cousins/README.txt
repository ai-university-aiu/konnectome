DOCS / COUSINS - THE DOCUMENTS AIMED AT THE OTHER TWO PROJECTS

Created 2026-08-11.


WHAT IS IN HERE

Documents konnectome WRITES but does not act on itself.

Everywhere else under /docs/ describes konnectome. These describe what konnectome needs FROM its two cousin projects - PrologAI, the language it is written in, and Causalontology, the frozen data structure it records its thoughts in - and they are addressed to those projects' maintainers rather than to konnectome's builders.

That is the whole distinction this directory draws, and it is why these documents do not belong beside the design authorities in /docs/design/ . A design authority tells a konnectome slice what to build. A change order tells a cousin what konnectome cannot build until the cousin moves.

• PrologAI_CHANGE_ORDER_v2.txt - additive gaps in the PrologAI language, raised under the Third Commandment.

• Causalontology_4_0_0_CHANGE_ORDER_v3.txt - data-structure gaps in the frozen ontology. Sections A and B shipped as 4.0.0; Sections C, D and E remain HELD.

• causalontology_thought_combination_AICA.txt - a 3.0.0-era reference document, kept and marked stale, not a change order.


WHY A CHANGE ORDER IS NOT A WISH LIST

The Third Commandment lets konnectome record a gap in a cousin rather than work around it in konnectome, and the Fifteenth gates whether that gap may be acted on at all.

So a section of a change order has a STANDING: shipped, or held. Held means written down, agreed to be real, and deliberately not built - and the held sections are the reason these documents stay live rather than moving to the archive. They are not finished, they are waiting.

A section's standing changes only when a cousin ships, and the ledger records the crossing.


VERSIONING AND ARCHIVING

These documents carry the _vN suffix of the Twelfth Commandment, and their superseded versions go to docs/archive/ under the Eleventh - NOT to docs/cousins/archive/ , because there is no such directory and the Eleventh Commandment names exactly one archive.


A NOTE ON THIS DIRECTORY AND THE CONSTITUTION

Like docs/design/ , this directory was made by tidying rather than by a Commandment, and the Constitution does not yet name it. The two constitutional pointers at the change orders were repointed into it in the same change, so no pointer rots, and NO RULE CHANGED. Writing docs/cousins/ into the Constitution proper would be an amendment, and no amendment has been made here.
