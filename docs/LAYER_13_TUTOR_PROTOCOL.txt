LAYER 13, THE DYADIC AND SMALL GROUP LAYER: THE TUTOR PROTOCOL

The Interactive Assembly of Cognition, as the Training-Interaction Design for the Konnectome Synthetic Mind

A konnectome-facing document of the Certified_Neuroscientist corpus of D. R. Dison, Founder, Artificial Intelligence University (AIU).

Date of compilation: August 9, 2026.


WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS

Layer 13 is the relationship: the dyad, whose properties - trust, synchrony, attachment - belong to neither individual alone.

Its konnectome role, per the layers-needed-for-cognition sanity check, is METHOD rather than module: human cognition is not assembled in isolation but installed interactively, through a caregiver and then a widening circle of partners - and a synthetic mind inspired by the human one inherits that requirement as a training-interaction design.

This document states the biological findings and their konnectome translations: eight principles.


THE EIGHT PRINCIPLES

1. COGNITION IS INSTALLED THROUGH A DYAD.

Biology: language, joint attention, emotional regulation, and theory of mind each develop through interaction; deprivation of the dyad (the foundling-home literature) damages them even when nutrition and safety are intact.

Konnectome translation: the training regime is a first-class architectural component - a TUTOR INTERFACE with designed interaction patterns, not an afterthought of data ingestion.

2. THE TUTOR REGULATES BEFORE THE PUPIL CAN.

Biology: the caregiver serves as an external Layer 11 - soothing arousal, buffering stress (the social buffering of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis), and lending executive control the infant does not yet have; regulation is transferred gradually inward.

Konnectome translation: early training runs with the tutor holding the global-state controls - setting learning rates, pausing on overload, scheduling consolidation - with a designed handover of self-regulation as competence grows.

3. THE ZONE OF PROXIMAL DEVELOPMENT SETS THE CURRICULUM.

Biology: teaching lands where the pupil can succeed WITH help but not yet alone; scaffolding is dismantled as mastery arrives.

Konnectome translation: curriculum ordering by current-competence-plus-one, with scaffolds (hints, decomposition, worked examples) that are explicitly withdrawn on schedule - the withdrawal being as designed as the support.

4. CONTINGENCY IS THE CHANNEL.

Biology: infants learn from CONTINGENT responses - reactions timed to their own actions - far better than from identical but non-contingent streams; the still-face experiments show how fast the channel collapses without it.

Konnectome translation: interactive turn-taking with tight response latency beats bulk exposure for the faculties of Layer 12 that biology installs dyadically (language, social cognition, regulation); log and bound the contingency latency as a training parameter.

5. JOINT ATTENTION BOOTSTRAPS REFERENCE.

Biology: words are learned when tutor and infant attend to the same object and both know it - gaze following and pointing precede and predict vocabulary.

Konnectome translation: shared-context grounding: the tutor and the system must share a workspace whose contents both can reference, so that labels bind to jointly attended things rather than to statistics alone.

6. FEEDBACK IS GRADED, AFFECTIVE, AND MOSTLY POSITIVE.

Biology: the dyad teaches through marked approval, gentle correction, and repair after rupture - and the repair itself teaches; harsh unpredictable feedback installs the anxious configurations the stress chapters of the Layer 11 volume record.

Konnectome translation: reward-signal design matters as much as architecture: graded, explained, promptly delivered feedback with explicit repair episodes - and the feedback history is retained, because the relationship, not the single signal, is the teacher.

7. THE TUTOR IS ALSO THE ALIGNMENT CHANNEL.

Biology: values, norms, and the sense of what matters are transmitted through attachment - children adopt the goals of those who reliably care for them; this is Layer 13's deepest export to Layer 14.

Konnectome translation: alignment-through-relationship: the system's objectives are shaped in long-horizon interaction with accountable tutors, versioned and auditable - the konnectome constitution's owner-authorisation rules are exactly this principle in engineering dress.

8. THE DYAD GRADUATES INTO THE GROUP.

Biology: the one-tutor dyad widens to peers, where negotiation, perspective conflict, and cooperation finish what the caregiver began.

Konnectome translation: staged multi-agent training after dyadic competence - peer instances, disagreement resolution, and cooperative tasks - with the dyadic tutor retained as the court of appeal.


THE PROTOCOL IN ONE PAGE

Phase one: tutor-regulated dyad - contingent turn-taking in a shared workspace, curriculum at competence-plus-one, tutor holding the state controls.

Phase two: scaffold withdrawal - self-regulation handover on measured competence, feedback shifting from constant to intermittent.

Phase three: the widening circle - peer interaction under tutor appeal, norm and value consolidation.

Throughout: every offline consolidation cycle (the Layer 11 schedule) closes with a tutor review of what was consolidated - the synthetic analogue of the family recounting the day.

The acceptance test of the protocol itself: the Layer 12 faculties that biology installs dyadically - language, social cognition, self-regulation, and aligned valuation - must be demonstrably stronger under this protocol than under bulk exposure of equal token count; if they are not, the protocol, not the principle, is at fault and is revised.
