LAYER 10, THE NEURAL AND NEUROENDOCRINE SYSTEMS LAYER: MODES AND MODE TRANSITIONS - THE OUTLINE

The per-system mode registers, extracted from LAYER_10_MODES_MANUSCRIPT (which holds the full entries: registers, per-mode transfer functions, transition tables, and fault regimes).

Each entry below: the system, its source chapter key, and its named mode register.

Date of compilation: August 9, 2026.


ENTRY 1. THE VISUAL SYSTEM, DORSAL AND VENTRAL STREAMS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 1)

◦ Foveal Scrutiny - The Close Reader - ventral-stream-led analysis of one attended object at high spatial frequency, the focal mode of the two-mode vision literature.

◦ Ambient Vigilance - The Wide Watch - dorsal-stream and peripheral low-spatial-frequency layout monitoring for orienting, posture, and navigation.

◦ Offline Replay - The Night Projector - slow-wave-sleep replay of recent patterns with the geniculate gate closed in burst mode.

◦ Endogenous Imagery - The Dream Screen - rapid-eye-movement (REM) top-down image generation with no retinal input.

ENTRY 2. THE AUDITORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 2)

◦ Speech-Tuned Analysis - The Conversation Lock - attention-steered foreground extraction of one voice with cortical fields retuned to its features.

◦ Scene Analysis - The Soundscape Sorter - broadband segregation of concurrent streams by pitch, timbre, and location without a single foreground.

◦ Sentinel Listening - The Night Watch - the sleep configuration in which the geniculate gate attenuates but the deviance and alarm channels stay open.

◦ Startle Priming - The Hair Trigger - stress posture favouring the geniculo-amygdala low road with lowered startle thresholds.

ENTRY 3. THE SOMATOSENSORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 3)

◦ Exploratory Touch - The Active Reader - attention-boosted discriminative touch during haptic exploration.

◦ Background Monitoring - The Idle Skin - low-gain default posture reporting only change and threat.

◦ Descending Analgesia - The Battlefield Gate - stress posture in which the periaqueductal grey and rostral ventromedial medulla close the dorsal horn gate hard.

◦ Sleep Damping - The Muffled Sheet - thalamically damped touch with privileged pain entry.

ENTRY 4. THE OLFACTORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 4)

◦ Sampling Sniff - The Deliberate Nose - active high-flow sniffing that interrogates an odour source.

◦ Passive Watch - The Resting Nostril - low-flow breathing with detection only of strong or novel odours.

◦ Appetitive Sharpening - The Pre-Meal Whet - ghrelin-driven sensitisation of food odour channels before meals.

◦ Bulbar Disconnection - The Sleeping Nose - slow-wave-sleep decoupling of piriform cortex from the bulb.

ENTRY 5. THE GUSTATORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 5)

◦ Ingestive Appraisal - The Mouthful Auditor - active tasting during eating with value updated mouthful by mouthful.

◦ Sated Discounting - The Full-Table Shrug - post-meal posture in which orbitofrontal taste value collapses for the eaten food.

◦ Standby - The Closed Kitchen - the idle state between meals and throughout sleep, thalamic gate closed with the rest.

ENTRY 6. THE VESTIBULAR SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 6)

◦ Reafference-Cancelled Drive - The Trusted Pilot - voluntary-movement posture with predicted self-motion partially subtracted.

◦ Full-Gain Stabilisation - The Grabbed Gyroscope - passive or unexpected motion handled at full reflex gain.

◦ Sleep Idle - The Parked Gimbal - the still-head idle of slow-wave sleep.

◦ Endogenous Burst Drive - The Dream Spinner - rapid-eye-movement (REM) bursting of the vestibular nuclei that drives the rapid eye movements and injects flying and falling into dreams.

ENTRY 7. THE INTEROCEPTIVE SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 7)

◦ Background Publication - The Quiet Bulletin - default low-resolution publication of the feeling background that weights ongoing choice.

◦ Attentive Introspection - The Body Scan - attention-opened insular gain making the same visceral input vividly felt.

◦ Visceral Alarm - The Override Siren - parabrachial takeover for air hunger and severe visceral threat, gate-proof by design.

◦ Sleep Autonomy - The Unwatched Engine Room - reflex floors running under uncontested brainstem control while insular access closes.

ENTRY 8. THE CORTICOSPINAL SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 8)

◦ Gated Transmission - The Open Line - awake availability, with release decided upstream by the basal ganglia and loudness set at the spinal end.

◦ Central Suppression - The Quiet Line - slow-wave-sleep posture with descending drive low and voluntary movement centrally suppressed.

◦ Atonic Block - The Cut Line - rapid-eye-movement (REM) posture in which cortical output cells fire freely but the command is blocked at the spinal end by active atonia.

ENTRY 9. THE CORTICO-BASAL-GANGLIA-THALAMO-CORTICAL LOOP SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 9)

◦ Deliberative Selection - The Weighed Vote - goal-directed selection with outcome value consulted before the brake lifts.

◦ Habitual Release - The Rubber Stamp - stimulus-triggered release of well-practised sequences with minimal deliberation.

◦ Offline Replay - The Night Rehearsal - slow-wave-sleep participation in replay that consolidates procedural memory while overt action is suppressed.

◦ Free-Wheeling Selection - The Dream Chooser - rapid-eye-movement (REM) selection with dopamine and limbic drive high and motor output blocked downstream.

ENTRY 10. THE CEREBELLAR SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 10)

◦ Calibrated Prediction - The Steady Hand - continuous forward-model prediction and correction with learning quiescent.

◦ Error-Driven Recalibration - The Tuning Session - olive-gated plasticity while errors are being signalled.

◦ Offline Consolidation - The Night Bench - sleep participation in consolidating newly learned motor skills.

ENTRY 11. THE OCULOMOTOR SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 11)

◦ Saccadic Acquisition - The Snap Aim - ballistic target acquisition by collicular burst.

◦ Smooth Pursuit - The Camera Dolly - continuous velocity matching of a moving target.

◦ Fixation Hold - The Locked Tripod - active suppression of saccades while detail is extracted.

◦ Endogenous Saccade Storm - The Dream Darter - rapid-eye-movement (REM) internally driven bursts firing the movements that name the state.

ENTRY 12. THE AUTONOMIC MOTOR SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 12)

◦ Sympathetic-Led Engagement - The Working Tilt - awake, active posture with the balance shifted toward sympathetic tone.

◦ Parasympathetic Restoration - The Night Housekeeping - slow-wave-sleep dominance of the vagal hand while the body restores itself.

◦ Autonomic Storm - The Dream Squall - rapid-eye-movement (REM) irregularity with surges of both divisions.

◦ Fight-or-Flight Mobilisation - The Full Alarm - acute-stress mobilisation of the whole sympathetic and adrenal apparatus at once.

ENTRY 13. THE EPISODIC MEMORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 13)

◦ Encoding Posture - The Open Shutter - storage-favouring configuration under high acetylcholine.

◦ Retrieval Posture - The Playback Head - completion-favouring configuration under low acetylcholine.

◦ Ripple Consolidation - The Nightly Copy - slow-wave-sleep replay via sharp-wave ripples performing the hippocampo-cortical transfer.

◦ Theta Integration - The Dream Re-Tagger - rapid-eye-movement (REM) theta with cortex partially disconnected, favouring integration and emotional re-tagging.

ENTRY 14. THE SEMANTIC MEMORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 14)

◦ Precision Retrieval - The Narrow Tap - frontally controlled selection of the task-relevant feature under sharp cholinergic tone.

◦ Associative Spread - The Wide Ladle - loosened retrieval admitting remote associations, characteristic of rest and rapid-eye-movement (REM) sleep.

◦ Overnight Distillation - The Slow Still - slow-wave-sleep receipt of episodic replay, weights adjusted toward new knowledge.

ENTRY 15. THE PROCEDURAL MEMORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 15)

◦ Forging - The Hot Anvil - dopamine-gated skill acquisition during practice.

◦ Fluent Execution - The Cold Stamp - fast, automatic execution of consolidated skills.

◦ Spindle Consolidation - The Night Shift - sleep replay locking in the day's motor gains.

◦ Stress Takeover - The Emergency Foreman - stress-driven assumption of control from the goal-directed system.

ENTRY 16. THE WORKING MEMORY SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 16)

◦ Robust Maintenance - The Held Chalk - attractors locked against distraction while contents are used.

◦ Gated Updating - The Fresh Slate - basal-ganglia-gated admission of new content, old content released.

◦ Erased Idle - The Wiped Board - slow-wave-sleep erasure, delay activity being incompatible with the slow oscillation's silent states.

◦ Ungoverned Flicker - The Doodling Board - rapid-eye-movement (REM) fragments igniting without gate control, one account of dream incoherence.

ENTRY 17. THE REWARD AND MOTIVATION SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 17)

◦ Exploitative Pricing - The Known-Vendor Round - valuation and pursuit of established rewards under stable dopamine prediction errors.

◦ Exploratory Sampling - The Market Stroll - novelty-weighted sampling of alternatives when established values decay or uncertainty rises.

◦ Hedonic Consummation - The Tasting Table - opioid-hotspot liking during consumption, dissociable from wanting.

◦ Replay Bookkeeping - The Night Audit - sleep replay preferentially consolidating rewarded experience via accumbens participation.

ENTRY 18. THE LANGUAGE SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 18)

◦ Comprehension Weave - The Listening Loom - sound-to-meaning mapping during listening and reading.

◦ Production Weave - The Speaking Loom - meaning-to-articulation mapping with online self-monitoring.

◦ Inner Speech - The Silent Loom - production run covertly with articulation withheld, the medium of much thought.

◦ Unmonitored Dream Speech - The Loose Loom - rapid-eye-movement (REM) production that is fluent but unmonitored.

ENTRY 19. THE EXECUTIVE CONTROL SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 19)

◦ Focused Exploitation - The Closed Session - stable task set held against distraction, phasic-noradrenaline regime.

◦ Divergent Exploration - The Open Floor - loosened task set sampling alternatives, tonic-noradrenaline regime.

◦ Devolved Emergency Rule - The Caretaker Cabinet - stress handover of control to habit and amygdala.

◦ Offline Recess - The Adjourned House - sleep withdrawal of executive function.

ENTRY 20. THE SOCIAL COGNITION SYSTEM (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 20)

◦ Mentalising Stance - The Deep Read - full model-building of another's beliefs, desires, and intentions.

◦ Fast Face-Intent Reading - The Glance Check - rapid appraisal of identity, gaze, and expression without deep modelling.

◦ Threat-Contracted Reading - The Narrowed Eye - stress posture contracted to threat detection with empathy narrowed.

◦ Social Simulation - The Night Rehearsal Stage - rapid-eye-movement (REM) activity generating densely populated social dream scenarios.

ENTRY 21. THE DEFAULT MODE NETWORK (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 21)

◦ Narrative Rest - The Fireside Teller - dominant quiet-waking self-referential simulation of past, future, and others.

◦ Task Suppression - The Hushed Teller - anticorrelated quiescence while outward task networks hold the floor.

◦ Dream Narration - The Unchecked Teller - the altered REM configuration narrating without executive supervision.

◦ Fragmented Idle - The Scattered Teller - deep slow-wave fragmentation of network coherence.

ENTRY 22. THE SALIENCE / VENTRAL ATTENTION NETWORK (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 22)

◦ Calibrated Vigilance - The Attentive Operator - normal waking posture, monitoring all channels and switching networks on genuine relevance.

◦ Damped Watch - The Night Operator - slow-wave-sleep posture with raised thresholds but preserved alarm entry.

◦ Stress Lock - The Jammed Switchboard - hyperactive posture locking the brain into the outward mode.

ENTRY 23. THE DORSAL ATTENTION NETWORK (The frontoparietal orienting network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 23)

◦ Engaged-Orienting - Spotlight On - top-down gain aimed at a chosen place or feature to select targets for eye and hand.

◦ Disengaged-Standby - Spotlight Off - awake but unaimed, holding no priority, ready to be recruited.

◦ Offline-Sleep - Lights Out - taken down in sleep when voluntary orienting has no role.

ENTRY 24. THE FRONTOPARIETAL CONTROL NETWORK (The central executive network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 24)

◦ Goal-Holding - Governor Steering - a task set and its contents kept alive against distraction, biasing attention, sensory, and motor systems.

◦ Task-Switching - Regear - the brief reconfiguration when the goal changes.

◦ Idle-Deactivated - Governor Resting - awake but off-task, control relaxed.

◦ Offline-Sleep - Governor Asleep - largely down in slow-wave and rapid-eye-movement (REM) sleep, one reason dreams go unexamined.

ENTRY 25. THE CINGULO-OPERCULAR NETWORK (The task-set maintenance network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 25)

◦ Tonic-Maintained - Steady Helm - one task-set held across whole blocks of minutes with sustained alertness.

◦ Phasic-Error - Course Correction - a brief signal thrown by a mistake that re-arms the set.

◦ Offline-Collapse - Helm Abandoned - the tonic signal falls at sleep onset.

ENTRY 26. THE LANGUAGE NETWORK (The perisylvian language network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 26)

◦ Comprehending - Sound-to-Sense - incoming words parsed into meaning.

◦ Producing - Sense-to-Sound - meaning unwoven into articulatory programs.

◦ Inner-Speech-Idle - Silent Loom - internally generated language at rest, loosely active in REM dream dialogue.

ENTRY 27. THE SENSORIMOTOR NETWORK (The perception-action loop network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 27)

◦ Active-Executing - Hands Working - commands out and feedback in, closing the loop in tens of milliseconds.

◦ Spindle-Consolidating - Rehearsing Offline - stage-two sleep spindles run over it to consolidate motor memory.

◦ Atonia-Locked - Paralysed at the Gate - actively paralysed at its spinal exit during REM sleep.

ENTRY 28. THE VISUAL NETWORK (The occipital hierarchy network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 28)

◦ Retinal-Driven - Eyes-On Refinery - the waking hierarchy transforming real optic input.

◦ Replay-Consolidating - Offline Rerun - slow-wave replay of the day's visual experience.

◦ Internally-Generated - Dreaming Screen - REM imagery rendered with no retinal input.

ENTRY 29. THE AUDITORY NETWORK (The temporal hearing network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 29)

◦ Attentive-Sifting - Cocktail-Party Filter - one attended stream sharpened at the expense of others.

◦ Ambient-Monitoring - Open Ear - passive waking listening with no selected stream.

◦ Sleep-Sentry - Night Watch - reduced but still evaluating salient sounds during sleep.

ENTRY 30. THE LIMBIC NETWORK (The orbitofrontal-temporopolar valuation network) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 30)

◦ Appraising - Snap Verdict - a fast value-and-affect tag on the current stimulus.

◦ Mood-Toning - Background Weather - a slow affective tone held for hours.

◦ Replay-Emotional - Night Rework - reprocessing of emotional memory in sleep.

ENTRY 31. THE DOPAMINE SYSTEM (The Mesostriatal and Mesocorticolimbic Dopaminergic Projection) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 31)

◦ Tonic-Vigour - Idle Throttle - a slow background level setting how briskly the organism will move.

◦ Phasic-Teaching - Surprise Pulse - a burst or dip broadcasting the reward prediction error.

◦ Sleep-Quiescent - Night Trickle - phasic teaching largely off, tonic persisting, with a REM rise.

ENTRY 32. THE NORADRENALINE SYSTEM (The Locus Coeruleus Noradrenergic Projection) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 32)

◦ Phasic-Exploit - Sharp Focus - moderate tonic firing with crisp event-locked bursts supporting focused, exploitative attention on a task.

◦ Tonic-Explore - Restless Scan - high tonic firing without bursts supporting distractible, exploratory scanning.

◦ Reset-Pulse - Slate-Wipe - a large phasic burst that interrupts and reconfigures target networks.

◦ Sleep-Silent - Blue Dark - low in slow-wave and near-silent in REM sleep.

ENTRY 33. THE SEROTONIN SYSTEM (The Raphe Serotonergic Projection) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 33)

◦ Tonic-Patience - Long-Horizon Hum - a steady level plus a signal that rises while waiting, biasing toward waiting over grabbing.

◦ Mood-Setting - Slow Weather - the hours-to-weeks background tone antidepressants target.

◦ Sleep-Graded - Dimmed Seam - slowed in slow-wave sleep and essentially silent in REM.

ENTRY 34. THE ACETYLCHOLINE SYSTEM (The Basal Forebrain and Brainstem Cholinergic Projection) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 34)

◦ High-Tonic-Encoding - Record Button On - waking high acetylcholine boosting feedforward sensory input and suppressing feedback, favouring writing the new.

◦ Low-Tonic-Retrieval - Playback Mode - slow-wave-sleep low acetylcholine releasing the cortex to replay and consolidate.

◦ Phasic-Salience - Attention Ping - a fast, restricted transient flagging an attention-worthy cue.

◦ REM-Paradox - Dreaming Chemistry - acetylcholine back at waking levels while the aminergic systems stay silent.

ENTRY 35. THE HISTAMINE SYSTEM (The Tuberomammillary Histaminergic Projection) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 35)

◦ Wake-On - Lights Up - tonic firing that sustains the aroused waking state, strongest in attentive waking.

◦ Sleep-Off - Lights Down - silence in both slow-wave and REM sleep.

ENTRY 36. THE OREXIN SYSTEM (The Lateral Hypothalamic Orexinergic Projection) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 36)

◦ Wake-Stabilising - Latch Held - tonic firing that excites all arousal nuclei at once, deepening the wake state.

◦ Motivated-Boost - Foraging Gear - stronger firing during active, motivated behaviour.

◦ Sleep-Silent - Latch Released - silence in both slow-wave and REM sleep, permitting sleep to consolidate.

ENTRY 37. THE HYPOTHALAMIC-PITUITARY-ADRENAL (HPA) AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 37)

◦ Circadian-Envelope - Daily Tide - the 24-hour programme peaking near waking and troughing late at night.

◦ Basal-Ultradian - Hourly Drip - roughly one cortisol pulse per hour riding that envelope.

◦ Acute-Stress-Surge - Alarm Flood - a fast cortisol rise answering a challenge.

ENTRY 38. THE HYPOTHALAMIC-PITUITARY-GONADAL (HPG) AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 38)

◦ Slow-Pulse-FSH - Low-Gear Tick - slow gonadotropin-releasing-hormone pulses favouring follicle-stimulating hormone.

◦ Fast-Pulse-LH - High-Gear Tick - fast pulses favouring luteinising hormone.

◦ Positive-Feedback-Surge - Ovulatory Trigger - the mid-cycle sign-switch producing the luteinising-hormone surge.

◦ Quiescent - Generator Idle - the childhood pause and stress or energy-deficit suspension.

ENTRY 39. THE HYPOTHALAMIC-PITUITARY-THYROID (HPT) AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 39)

◦ Set-Point-Hold - Steady Dial - the defended individual thyroid-hormone level.

◦ Circadian-Ripple - Night Nudge - a gentle thyroid-stimulating-hormone nocturnal rise.

◦ Cold-Upregulated - Warm-Up Turn - a raised setting to generate heat.

◦ Illness-Downregulated - Idle Down - the suppressed low-metabolic state of non-thyroidal illness.

ENTRY 40. THE GROWTH HORMONE AND SOMATOSTATIN AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 40)

◦ Somatostatin-Braked - Brake On - the inhibited baseline between pulses, output low.

◦ Sleep-Pulse - Night Build - the large growth-hormone pulse locked to the first slow-wave-sleep episode.

◦ Daytime-Sparse-Pulse - Odd-Hours Trickle - the few smaller pulses scattered across the day.

ENTRY 41. THE MELATONIN AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 41)

◦ Night-Secreting - Darkness Stamp - melatonin released across the biological night.

◦ Day-Suppressed - Light Off - synthesis shut off by day and by light at night.

◦ Phase-Shifting - Clock-Setter - the dusk and dawn windows in which the hormone resets its own clock.

ENTRY 42. THE GLUCOSE HOMEOSTASIS AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 42)

◦ Fed-Insulin - Store Mode - rising glucose raising insulin and lowering glucagon to store fuel.

◦ Fasted-Glucagon - Release Mode - falling glucose reversing the balance toward hepatic glucose release.

◦ Counter-Regulatory-Defence - Fuel Alarm - the fast cascade recruited when glucose falls too low.

◦ Basal-Pulsatile - Idle Ledger - the small five-minute insulin pulses on baseline between meals.

ENTRY 43. THE GUT-BRAIN AXIS (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 43)

◦ Fed-Digesting - Table Set - active digestion, satiety signalling, and post-meal gut-hormone waves.

◦ Fasted-Housekeeping - Sweep Cycle - the migrating motor complex sweeping the empty gut.

◦ Hunger-Signalling - Empty Belly - ghrelin-driven appetite drive telegraphed upward.

◦ Stress-Rerouted - Nervous Gut - altered motility and secretion under stress.

ENTRY 44. THE GLOBAL WORKSPACE DYNAMICS (The Ignition and Broadcast System) (keyed to systems catalogue Chapter 44)

◦ Subliminal - Below the Line - bottom-up activation too weak to trigger a large-scale reverberating state, content inaccessible (web-confirmed).

◦ Preconscious - In the Waiting Room - activation strong enough but not yet attended, held back by the availability of top-down attention (web-confirmed).

◦ Ignited-Conscious - Lit and Broadcast - a single content amplified in an all-or-none surge and broadcast brain-wide.

◦ Offline-Collapsed - Broadcast Down - the loss of ignition in slow-wave sleep and anaesthesia.
