TWO. THE ONTOGENETIC AXIS. Complete. 960 lines. Banner removed.

Parts Three and Four were written from two fully sourced research passes that had completed before the limit hit. Writing them to disk immediately was deliberate: durable state lives on disk and never only in a conversation, and research held in a session's context is exactly the state that principle exists to prevent.

Part Three writes out the developmental timetable, critical and sensitive periods, activity-dependent refinement, the lifetime schedule of the stability-versus-plasticity trade-off, sexual differentiation, and ageing. Part Four costs each of the three resolutions in concrete work so they can be compared by effort rather than rhetoric.

Several widely repeated figures are recorded as NOT supported, with reasons: that the prefrontal cortex matures at 25, which is a rounding artefact of cohort age limits; the 17.4-year second-language figure, which a 2022 reanalysis found to be no discontinuity at all for most learner types; any specific rate for adult human hippocampal neurogenesis; and absolute peak synaptic density, which could not be verified against the primary text and is therefore not stated at all.

Its most important single finding, because popular accounts omit it: critical-period reopening is robust and replicated in rodents and its human translation record is close to zero. Fluoxetine was the most translatable candidate, was properly tested in adult amblyopia, and failed.

It decides nothing. It states which resolution it favours in one marked paragraph and says plainly that the choice belongs to the corpus's author and to the owner.

A NOTE ON METHOD, WORTH KEEPING

The research this session produced is unusually good and its best property is that it argues with its own sources.

It corrected the brief on multiple points of fact: the bumetanide autism trials were published in Autism Research and not Lancet Psychiatry; the NEMO neonatal trial is 2015 and not 2021; the 1992 sodium-channel experiment used single mutations and not a full ring conversion; the tyrosine of the potassium channel signature sequence is NOT required for selectivity, contrary to a common textbook claim; the famous hundred-million-fold amplification figure is a whole-pathway hepatic number of contested provenance that modern nanodomain measurements make untenable.

It repeatedly found that a famous constant had been wrong by orders of magnitude for decades - nitric oxide concentration by roughly a thousandfold, hydrogen sulphide by roughly three thousandfold, and in both cases the correction came from the original author or from a better assay, and in both cases a generation of pharmacology had been designed around the wrong number.

That is exactly the discipline this corpus is supposed to embody, and it is why the documents that came out of it are worth keeping even where they are incomplete.
