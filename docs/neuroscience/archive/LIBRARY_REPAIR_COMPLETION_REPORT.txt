HOW THE WORK WAS DONE

The four items were decomposed into eleven independent writing tasks and run in parallel, each by its own research-and-writing agent. Each agent was held to one shared brief carrying the project's own governance and this corpus's plain-text house style as hard gates. Each verified its load-bearing figures by web search before stating them, and each was instructed to refuse a value aloud rather than invent one where the literature does not settle it.

The eleven tasks were: the 85 non-neuronal cell types in three blocks; the 139 Layer 9 regions in three blocks; the 301 neuron chapters grouped into 27 developmental lineage classes with a keyed index; the time constants companion in two halves; the peripheral and efferent expansion; and the transcriptomic cross-walk.

All eleven returned clean. Every deliverable passed three gates at zero violations: no em-dashes or en-dashes, no markdown characters, and the assigned entry count exact.


THE FOUR FILES, AND WHERE THEY LIVE

ONE. LAYER_06_09_DEVELOPMENT_AND_DISEASE_COMPANION.txt - finished, banner removed. About 3,095 lines.

The front matter this file already carried is unchanged. Beneath it now sit three parts.

Part One covers all 85 non-neuronal cell types, chapter by chapter, each with a DEVELOPMENT AND ASSEMBLY block (lineage and germ layer, specifying transcription factors where known, birth and maturation timings with rodent data marked as rodent, replaceability, and any critical period) and a DISEASES AND DISORDERS block classifying the cell per disorder as a CAUSE, a CASUALTY, or a MARKER.

Part Two covers all 139 Layer 9 regional headings at the same grain.

Part Three covers the 301 neuron chapters by 27 developmental lineage classes, each with a full development and disease treatment, followed by a keyed index that maps every one of the 301 chapters to its class and notes departures. This is a complete treatment at the grain of the lineage, which is where developmental origin actually lives, rather than 301 thin repetitions.

The banner's removal test was that the entries are written. They are, so the banner came off, exactly as the convention requires and without a matter of judgement being involved.

TWO. LAYER_09_TIME_CONSTANTS_COMPANION.txt - new. About 918 lines, 139 entries.

Every entry states one to several characteristic timescales, and every value carries one of four markings: CITED to a corpus layer that already states it, DERIVED with the working shown from the layers either side, LITERATURE with a named web source that the corpus outranks, or REFUSED aloud where no honest value can be given. The refusals are deliberate. The document states on its own face that a companion managing to state a timescale for all 139 headings would be one that had invented some.

THREE. LAYER_09_PERIPHERAL_AND_EFFERENT_EXPANSION.txt - new. About 866 lines.

It supplies the roster of the twelve cranial nerves the corpus lacked, each with nuclei of origin and termination, brainstem locations, cargo as INPUTS and OUTPUTS in the manuscript's own style, modality mix, the Layer 10 system chains it belongs to, and a clinical failure signature. It then brings the thin sections 2.5 through 2.9 - cerebellum, spinal cord, peripheral ganglia, enteric nervous system, endocrine glands - up toward the depth the cortex enjoys, aiming at proportionality where Layer 10 chains need Layer 9 addresses rather than parity everywhere.

FOUR. THE_TRANSCRIPTOMIC_CROSS_WALK.txt - new. About 359 lines.

It joins the morphology-and-physiology cell catalogue to the field's current molecular index, naming and dating six public censuses and grading every mapping EXACT, COARSER, FINER, ABSENT, or PERIPHERAL. Where a morphological type has no molecular counterpart, ABSENT is stated plainly rather than a mapping forced.


WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT DONE

No existing corpus manuscript was edited. All four deliverables are additive companions, keyed to the parent manuscripts' own chapter and section numbers in paste-ready form, so that a future merge is a mechanical splice and not an act of interpretation. This is the same discipline the MODES companions used and it is kept for the same reason.

The version 4 revision of the Layer 6 chapter template - which would add DEVELOPMENT AND ASSEMBLY and DISEASES AND DISORDERS as native aspect headers so this companion's blocks have somewhere of their own to live - is named in the development companion's front matter and remains a decision for the corpus's author.

The per-chapter development and disease blocks for each of the 301 neuron types individually, at the fine grain used for the 85 non-neuronal types, are still owed by a future pass. The class treatment gives the lineage, the cascade, the birth window and the class-level disease profile; what it cannot give is per-type birth order, maturation rate, and selective vulnerability within a class. This limit is stated on the companion's own face.


ONE FINDING FOR THE CORPUS AUTHOR

The peripheral-expansion work surfaced a possible factual issue in the guiding book that this session did NOT edit, because correcting a claim about the biology is authorship rather than maintenance. The manuscript's enteric nervous system entry describes that system as holding more neurons than the spinal cord. The modern stereological literature puts the two closer together and possibly the other way round: the enteric count is now estimated by Michel and colleagues in 2022 at roughly 168 million, against spinal cord estimates of about 197 to 222 million from Bahney and von Bartheld in 2018, where the older enteric figure of 400 to 600 million was an extrapolation. This is flagged here and in the expansion document for the author to weigh. It may be a correctness repair or it may turn on a definition; either way the corpus was left as it stands and the decision is left with its owner.
