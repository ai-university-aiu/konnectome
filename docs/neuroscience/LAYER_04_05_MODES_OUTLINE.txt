LAYERS 4 AND 5, THE MACROMOLECULAR AND ORGANELLAR LAYERS: MODES AND MODE TRANSITIONS - THE OUTLINE

The per-mechanism mode registers, extracted from LAYER_04_05_MODES_MANUSCRIPT (which holds the full entries: registers, per-mode transfer functions, transition tables, and fault regimes).

Each entry below: the mechanism, its source chapter key, and its named mode register.

Date of compilation: August 9, 2026.


ENTRY 1. THE CAMKII SWITCH (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 1)

◦ OFF (Basal Ring) - the ring rests mostly unphosphorylated at threonine 286, reporting the synapse as basal weight.

◦ ON (Stamped Ring) - the ring rests mostly autophosphorylated and self-restamping, reporting the synapse as potentiated.

ENTRY 2. THE PKM-ZETA LOOP (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 2)

◦ IDLE (Loop Dark) - synthesis at baseline, no self-sustaining production, synapse held only by upstream stores.

◦ RUNNING (Loop Lit) - self-sustaining synthesis loop latched on, holding elevated AMPA-receptor number for days to weeks.

ENTRY 3. THE CPEB PRION-LIKE SWITCH (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 3)

◦ SOLUBLE (Clamped Repressor) - CPEB3 soluble and repressing, target plasticity messengers untranslated.

◦ AGGREGATED (Templating Stamp) - CPEB3 in a self-templating aggregate that keeps its target messengers translated.

ENTRY 4. THE CREB GATE (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 4)

◦ BELOW (Waiting Gate) - phosphorylated CREB under threshold, no gene programme launched.

◦ FIRED (Graduation Pulse) - threshold crossed, memory gene programme triggered and downstream renewal loops armed.

◦ PRIMED (Eligible Cell) - a raised-excitability window during which this neuron is preferentially recruited into the engram.

ENTRY 5. THE SYNAPTIC TAG (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 5)

◦ UNSET (Ineligible Dock) - no tag, synapse cannot capture plasticity products.

◦ SET (Loading Dock Open) - tag present, synapse entitled to capture shared plasticity products.

◦ CONSUMED (Cargo Captured) - tag has captured cargo and installed durable change, the address spent.

ENTRY 6. THE PLASTICITY PRODUCTS (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 6)

◦ QUIESCENT (No Shipment) - no product wave in the dendritic tree.

◦ BROADCASTING (Cell-Wide Shipment) - a wave of newly made products, including the activity-regulated cytoskeleton-associated protein (abbreviated Arc) and brain-derived neurotrophic factor (abbreviated BDNF), diffusing tree-wide.

◦ CAPTURED (Payload Spent) - products seized at a tagged synapse and consumed into durable change.

ENTRY 7. THE LOCAL TRANSLATION STATIONS (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 7)

◦ DORMANT (Stocked and Idle) - messengers pre-positioned but translationally repressed, ribosomes idling.

◦ FABRICATING (Workshop Awake) - specific messengers released and ribosomes actively reading, building kinases, scaffolds and cytoskeletal parts on the spot.

ENTRY 8. THE SLOT AND TRAFFICKING SYSTEM (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 8)

◦ HOLDING (Rack Steady) - slot count maintained, receptors trapped and refreshed from the reserve pool at a steady set point.

◦ FILLING (Rack Up) - net insertion of receptors and recruitment of slots, raising the count.

◦ EMPTYING (Rack Down) - net endocytosis of receptors and loss of slots, lowering the count.

ENTRY 9. THE SPINE ACTIN SKELETON (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 9)

◦ SMALL (Baseline Frame) - small spine, small postsynaptic density, few slots.

◦ REMODELLING (Transient Swell) - a brief enlargement phase during active polymerisation.

◦ ENLARGED (Shape Locked) - a stable cross-braced enlarged frame held by treadmilling.

ENTRY 10. THE TRANS-SYNAPTIC ADHESION COMPLEX (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 10)

◦ UNBOLTED (No Join) - no matched adhesion pair, no aligned junction.

◦ BOLTED (In Register) - neurexin and neuroligin clasped, release site held over receptor cluster, redundant bonds re-clasped under load.

◦ DISBANDING (Bolts Cut) - bonds broken and the junction being disassembled.

ENTRY 11. THE PERINEURONAL NET (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 11)

◦ PERMISSIVE (Fence Down) - no condensed net, enclosed synapses free to change, as in the juvenile critical period.

◦ FENCED (Fence Up) - condensed lattice in place, enclosed wiring write-protected against structural change.

ENTRY 12. THE MITOCHONDRIAL PROVISIONING SYSTEM (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 12)

◦ UNDOCKED (No Dock) - no mitochondrion stationed at a given synapse.

◦ DOCKED (Power Dock) - a mitochondrion anchored beneath an active spine or terminal, supplying the energy currency and buffering calcium.

ENTRY 13. THE SYNAPSE-TO-NUCLEUS SIGNALLING RELAY (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 13)

◦ IDLE (No Petition) - no request in flight.

◦ IN TRANSIT (Courier Walking) - a phosphorylated cargo locked in a protective complex walking the microtubule to the nucleus.

◦ DELIVERED (Petition Read) - cargo released at the nucleus and acting on transcription factors.

ENTRY 14. THE IMMEDIATE-EARLY GENE RESPONSE (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 14)

◦ POISED (Pre-Armed Trigger) - first-response machinery loaded, no transcription, zero start-up latency.

◦ PULSING (Fast Ledger Writing) - immediate-early genes such as c-Fos, Arc, Egr1 and Npas4 transcribed, a whole-cell nuclear event.

◦ DECAYED (Ledger Fading) - messengers and proteins clearing, record resetting.

ENTRY 15. THE HISTONE CODE (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 15)

◦ OPEN (Loose Spool) - acetylated histones, chromatin loosened, plasticity genes readable.

◦ CLOSED (Tight Spool) - certain methyl marks dominant, chromatin tightened, genes silenced.

ENTRY 16. THE DNA METHYLATION STORE (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 16)

◦ UNMETHYLATED (Bare Letter) - gene available, higher activity.

◦ METHYLATED (Inked Letter) - methyl group on the cytosine, gene durably quietened.

ENTRY 17. THE TRANSPOSON AND SOMATIC MOSAICISM QUESTION (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 17)

◦ UNINSERTED (Reference Genome) - the neuron's genome unchanged at that locus; the normal operating state.

◦ INSERTED (Scarred Genome) - a permanent retrotransposition insertion, present but with no known controlled use.

ENTRY 18. THE HOMEOSTATIC SET-POINT MACHINERY (keyed to slow variables catalogue Chapter 18)

◦ QUIESCENT (Governor Resting) - average firing near target, gain held steady, no scaling underway.

◦ RENORMALISING UP (Fair Governor Lifting) - prolonged under-activity driving every excitatory synapse up by one shared factor.

◦ RENORMALISING DOWN (Fair Governor Trimming) - prolonged over-activity driving every synapse down by one shared factor.

END OF OUTLINE
