ENTRY 2.1.1. PRIMARY VISUAL CORTEX (V1, STRIATE CORTEX, BRODMANN AREA 17) (keyed to Nature's Cognitive Architecture section 2.1.1)

◦ Waking Desynchronised Mode - The Open Aperture - low-amplitude fast local field potential in the beta-gamma range, faithful relay of retinal contours to the cortex.

◦ Attentive High-Gain Sub-mode - The Widened Pupil - a within-wake amplification in which attention or locomotion raises the gain on the driven channels.

◦ Slow-Wave Up-Down Mode - The Tidal Cortex - the non-REM (rapid-eye-movement) sleep regime of synchronised up and down states below one hertz.

◦ REM Activation Mode - The Ghost Screen - a wake-like desynchronised regime running on internally generated rather than retinal drive.

ENTRY 2.1.2. SECONDARY AND HIGHER VISUAL AREAS (V2, V3, V4, V5/MT) (keyed to Nature's Cognitive Architecture section 2.1.2)

◦ Waking Feedforward Mode - The Assembly Line - desynchronised processing passing form and motion up the ventral and dorsal streams.

◦ Attentive Selection Mode - The Spotlight Stage - feature-based and spatial attention biases the competition among representations.

◦ Slow-Wave Up-Down Mode - The Tidal Cortex - non-rapid-eye-movement (non-REM) synchronised alternation.

◦ REM Activation Mode - The Ghost Screen - internally driven wake-like activity; note that secondary and associative visual areas tend to desynchronise more fully in REM than primary areas do.

ENTRY 2.1.3. PRIMARY AUDITORY CORTEX (A1, HESCHL'S GYRUS, BRODMANN AREAS 41 AND 42) (keyed to Nature's Cognitive Architecture section 2.1.3)

◦ Waking Listening Mode - The Open Ear - desynchronised tonotopic relay of medial-geniculate input.

◦ Attentive Tracking Sub-mode - The Cocked Ear - attention raises gain on an attended stream or frequency band.

◦ Slow-Wave Up-Down Mode - The Tidal Cortex - non-REM synchronised alternation.

◦ rapid-eye-movement (REM) Activation Mode - The Half-Waking Ear - wake-like desynchronisation that in auditory cortex is often incomplete, with residual superficial slow waves.

ENTRY 2.4.4. THE ASCENDING AROUSAL SYSTEM NUCLEI (keyed to Nature's Cognitive Architecture section 2.4.4)
