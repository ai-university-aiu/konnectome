LAYER 11, THE WHOLE NERVOUS AND ENDOCRINE SYSTEM LAYER: THE GLOBAL STATES

A Complete, Comprehensive, and Thorough List and Outline of the Global Operating States of the Human Nervous and Endocrine System

Companion outline to the Layer 6 through Layer 9 catalogues in the Certified_Neuroscientist corpus of D. R. Dison, Founder, Artificial Intelligence University (AIU).

Date of compilation: August 9, 2026.


HOW TO READ THIS OUTLINE

Layer 11 is the whole integrated regulatory organ - and its principal phenomenon, the subject of this outline, is the GLOBAL STATE: a coordinated operating configuration of the entire nervous and endocrine system at once.

The corpus's modes volumes prepared the ground: at every layer below, the mode transitions kept being "thrown from above."

Layer 11 is the thrower - and in the corpus's own vocabulary, a global state IS a coordinated assignment of modes across every cell, junction, microcircuit, and region in every catalogue.

Layer 11 is therefore the MASTER MODE REGISTER of the architecture, and this outline catalogues its entries: twenty-six global states and state-machinery constructs, in six parts.

For an engineering realisation, this is the operating system layer: the scheduler, the power management, the offline maintenance cycle, and the global configuration broadcasts - the components no computer ships without.

Every entry carries one of two tags, with the corpus's usual meaning.

• (COG) means the state or machinery participates in COGNITION - computation, consolidation, memory, attention, or learning runs IN it or THROUGH it.

• (NON-COG) means the entry serves substrate, protective, metabolic, or regulatory roles.

A note on admission, sharpened for this layer: an entry is a STATE if the healthy system enters it as part of normal operation; the engineered and pathological configurations of Part Six are catalogued separately and honestly flagged, because a map of the state space is incomplete without its boundaries.


PART ONE. THE WAKING STATES

Waking is not one state; it is a family of operating points on the arousal and attention axes.

1. Alert Task-Engaged Waking (COG).

The desynchronised, acetylcholine- and noradrenaline-rich configuration in which cortex runs fast and asynchronous, task-positive networks lead, and the organism computes on the world - the default assumption of nearly every cognitive experiment ever run.

2. Relaxed Wakefulness (The Alpha Idle) (COG).

Eyes closed, posterior alpha rhythm dominant, default mode network forward - the idling-but-ready state in which mind-wandering, autobiographical simulation, and incubation run; idle is not off.

3. Focused Absorption (The Flow Configuration) (COG).

The high-engagement, low-self-monitoring variant of alert waking - locus coeruleus at optimal mid-tonic gain, transient hypofrontality of self-referential circuits - the state of skilled effortless performance; admitted with a moderate-confidence note, as its boundaries are less crisp than the classical stages.

4. Drowsy Waking (The Descending Platform) (NON-COG).

The genuine intermediate on the way down: slowed alpha, rolling eye movements, microsleeps intruding - operationally the state in which vigilance failures kill drivers; a real state, not merely a boundary.


PART TWO. THE SLEEP STATES

The nightly tour through four qualitatively distinct whole-organism configurations, in a roughly ninety-minute cycle repeated four to six times.

A note on names: the three non-rapid-eye-movement stages are named N1, N2, and N3 (N for non-rapid-eye-movement, or non-REM), by the standard sleep-staging convention.

5. Stage N1 (Sleep Onset, The Hypnagogic Threshold) (NON-COG).

The brief vestibule: theta replaces alpha, hypnagogic imagery flickers, muscle tone dips - two to five percent of the night, the state of the hypnic jerk.

6. Stage N2 (Spindled Light Sleep) (COG).

The plurality state of human sleep - sleep spindles and K-complexes on a theta background - and not a mere corridor: spindles are the thalamocortical packaging events of memory consolidation, and their density tracks overnight learning.

7. Stage N3 (Slow-Wave Sleep, The Deep Works) (COG).

The up-down alternation at less than one cycle per second sweeping the whole cortex; the state of systems consolidation (hippocampus-to-cortex replay dialogue), of synaptic renormalisation, of growth hormone release, and of glymphatic clearance running at full flow - the night shift during which the plant is both cleaned and rebuilt.

8. Rapid-Eye-Movement (REM) Sleep (The Paradoxical Theatre) (COG).

Cortex awake-like, body paralysed, aminergic broadcasts silenced while acetylcholine runs - the state of vivid dreaming, of emotional memory recalibration, and of schema integration; itself two sub-modes (phasic, with eye movement bursts, and tonic) as its modes entry will record.

9. The Ultradian Sleep Cycle (The Ninety-Minute Wheel) (NON-COG).

The architecture that sequences entries 5 through 8 - N3-dominant cycles early in the night, REM-dominant cycles toward morning - a whole-night program, not a state, catalogued here because the sequence itself is functional: consolidation stages appear to be ordered on purpose.

10. Sleep Inertia (The Cold-Start Penalty) (NON-COG).

The genuine transitional state after waking - minutes to half an hour of degraded cognition while the cortex warms to waking configuration - proof that state transitions at Layer 11 have real time constants and real costs.


PART THREE. THE STATE MACHINERY

The circuitry and governors that hold, switch, and schedule the states of Parts One and Two.

11. The Sleep-Wake Flip-Flop (NON-COG).

The mutual-inhibition switch between the ventrolateral preoptic sleep-promoting population and the ascending arousal nuclei - the Layer 8 flip-flop motif installed at whole-organism scale, engineered for fast, complete, bistable transitions.

12. The REM Switch (NON-COG).

The second flip-flop, between REM-on and REM-off populations in the pons, gated by aminergic and cholinergic tone - the machinery that makes REM discrete rather than graded.

13. The Orexin Stabiliser (NON-COG).

The lateral hypothalamic orexin system as the finger holding the switch: tonic waking drive that prevents inappropriate flips - its loss is narcolepsy, the disease of unstable state boundaries.

14. Process S, the Homeostatic Sleep Pressure (NON-COG).

The rising cost-counter of waking - adenosine and synaptic load accumulating hour by hour, discharged by slow-wave sleep - one half of the two-process governor.

15. Process C, the Circadian Drive (NON-COG).

The suprachiasmatic clock's twenty-four-hour arousal waveform, opposing and shaping Process S - the other half of the governor; the two-process model is the corpus's cleanest example of a whole-organism control law.

16. The Ascending Broadcast System (The State Announcement Service) (NON-COG).

The five neuromodulatory systems plus orexin considered as one Layer 11 organ: the channels through which a state decision, once taken, is announced to every cell in every catalogue - the physical implementation of "thrown from above," and the direct ancestor of an engineered global configuration bus.


PART FOUR. THE AROUSAL, STRESS, AND DEFENCE STATES

The emergency and vigilance configurations, layered over waking.

17. Phasic Arousal (The Startle Spike) (NON-COG).

The sub-second, locus-coeruleus-driven whole-system alerting burst - pupils, heart, cortex re-tuned within a heartbeat; the interrupt request of the architecture.

18. The Acute Stress State (The Sympatho-Adrenal Surge) (NON-COG).

Fight-or-flight proper: sympathetic mass discharge plus adrenaline within seconds, cortisol following in minutes - a coherent whole-body reconfiguration that sharpens some cognition while narrowing it.

19. The Sustained Stress State (The Cortisol Plateau) (NON-COG).

The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis holding glucocorticoids high across hours to weeks - adaptive mobilisation shading, when unrelieved, into the allostatic-load fault the entry's watchdog section records.

20. The Sickness Behaviour State (The Infection Protocol) (NON-COG).

The cytokine-commanded reconfiguration - lethargy, withdrawal, anhedonia, fever, sleep changes - the framework's neuroimmune channel operating a genuine, adaptive, reversible whole-organism state; the corpus's clearest proof that immune signals sit at the state controls.

21. The Fed and Fasted Metabolic States (NON-COG).

The insulin-led absorptive configuration versus the glucagon-and-ghrelin-led fasting configuration, each retuning hypothalamic drives, vagal traffic, and even memory circuits - the metabolic duty cycle underneath all cognition.


PART FIVE. THE CIRCADIAN AND INFRADIAN PROGRAMMES

The scheduled state programmes that carry the organism through its days and cycles.

22. The Circadian Day Programme and Night Programme (NON-COG).

The whole-organism twenty-four-hour configuration pair - hormone phases, temperature rhythm, alertness waveform, clock-gene phase in every tissue - the master schedule within which every other state is booked.

23. The Menstrual (Ovarian) Cycle Programme (NON-COG).

The roughly twenty-eight-day whole-system programme - follicular and luteal configurations with distinct hormonal climates, thermoregulation, sleep architecture, and documented cognitive-affective modulation - the corpus's clearest infradian state programme.

24. The Basic Rest-Activity Cycle (The Waking Ultradian) (NON-COG).

The roughly ninety-minute waxing and waning of alertness across the waking day - the sleep cycle's daytime shadow; admitted with a low-confidence note, as its independence remains debated.


PART SIX. THE ENGINEERED AND BOUNDARY STATES

Configurations the healthy system does not schedule, catalogued because the state space's boundaries teach its design.

25. General Anaesthesia (The Engineered Off-Switch) (NON-COG).

The pharmacological state family that reversibly abolishes consciousness - burst suppression, thalamocortical disconnection, breakdown of cortical integration - not a natural state but the single most informative probe of what the natural states are made of.

26. The Disorders of Consciousness Spectrum (The Broken State Machine) (NON-COG).

Coma, the vegetative or unresponsive wakefulness state, and the minimally conscious state - configurations in which the state machinery itself is lesioned, catalogued as the fault space of Layer 11 with its watchdogs (the arousal nuclei) and its measurement science.


THE COGNITIVE SCOREBOARD

Of the twenty-six entries, six carry the (COG) tag - and they are a revealing six.

• The three waking states (1, 2, 3): cognition's online configurations, including the idle that is secretly generative.

• Three of the four sleep stages (6, 7, 8): the offline shift, where consolidation - which is cognition - runs while the organism appears switched off.

Everything else is machinery, protection, scheduling, and metabolism: the governors that decide WHICH cognition runs WHEN.

The layer's lesson for the engineer is the one the whole corpus has been building toward.

A mind is not a computation that is always on; it is a scheduled alternation of online computation and offline consolidation, governed by a two-process controller, announced by a broadcast bus, stabilised by dedicated switch circuitry, and interruptible by emergency states - and every one of those components is now catalogued.
