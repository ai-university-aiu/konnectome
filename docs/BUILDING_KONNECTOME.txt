HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

This document is written in two voices at once, and both are load-bearing.

For the scientist: every claim in this log carries its number. Test counts are exact, dates are absolute, commit hashes are quoted, and a result that was not achieved is recorded as plainly as one that was. Nothing here is rounded up. Where the build failed, paused, or declined to build something, the entry says so and says why, because in this program an honest non-closure is a result, not an embarrassment.

For the reader: this is the story of a mind being built in the open - a small, glass-box mind called konnectome, written in a language designed to be read (PrologAI), thinking in a data structure designed to be shared (Causalontology), governed by a written constitution, and grown one honest slice at a time. It has a heartbeat. It has drives. It gets surprised. It models other minds, including their false beliefs. And as of the day this log begins, it grades the evidence behind its own claims, stands behind them under its own name, and takes them back - on the record - when the world proves it wrong.

The two voices are not in tension. The story is only good because the numbers are real.


PROLOGUE - THE STORY SO FAR (2026-07-19 TO 2026-07-26)

Every build in this organization begins the same way: with law, not code.

On the nineteenth of July, twenty twenty-six, the konnectome repository was created holding little more than a written CONSTITUTION.md - at first fourteen commandments, later sixteen, now seventeen - binding the build to a guiding book (Nature's Cognitive Architecture), a frozen thought-structure standard (Causalontology, changeable only through a gated change order), a readable reference language (PrologAI, changeable only the same way), a running ledger of every wall the build would ever hit, and a six-phase waterfall method called SPARCD: Specification, Pseudocode, Architecture, Refinement, Completion, Demonstration.

The rule underneath all the other rules is this: findings are discovered by building, never guessed from the armchair.

On the twentieth of July, in one long day, the first ten slices landed. A scheduler and tick engine gave the mind a heartbeat - a two-pass synchronous update, compute-then-commit, so that no construct ever sees a neighbour's future. Six dynamical archetypes - relay, integrator, oscillator, attractor, gate, comparator - gave it the only six kinds of moving part it is allowed. A connection graph gave it wiring; a neuromodulatory bus gave it broadcast chemistry; a drive system gave it wants, error signals, and reward; an action selector - forbidden by construction from inventing actions, because a selector that generates is the homunculus creeping back in - gave it choice; an override controller gave it one inviolable safety property (no deliberation may ever suppress the breath drive to self-termination); a plasticity engine gave it three-factor learning; an observer gave it a memory of every tick as a content-addressed record; a perturbation interface gave the scientists a way in; and a cognitive cycle wired all ten components into one faithful tick. A tenth-slice pack taught it to combine thoughts into chains and trees as Causal Relation Objects.

On the twenty-second of July the build hit its first two walls, minutes apart, and the program showed what it is made of. Slice 11 tried to give the mind a theory of other minds and found the shared data structure had no kind of record for a belief - a mind could state a fact but could not attribute a false one. That same slice tried to give it expectation and found a forecast minted as a fact was byte-for-byte identical to a claim of fact. Two walls, honestly recorded in the ledger, routed through the gated change order, and answered the same day by Causalontology 4.0.0: three new kinds of record (attitude, predicted_occurrence, prediction_error), twenty-one kinds in all, a conformance suite re-frozen at one hundred thirty-seven vectors with every old identifier preserved byte-for-byte. A third wall - the language library could not yet mint the new kinds - was routed to PrologAI and closed the same day by causal_core 1.1.0. By nightfall the mind held a false belief about a marble in a basket, expected a hidden object to still exist, and measured its own surprise, signed, when a screen lifted on nothing.

On the twenty-sixth of July, slice 13 gave the mind responsibility. A new pack, self_provenance, taught konnectome to mint an assertion about its own records - carrying its own public source identity, an evidence grade from a fixed ladder (intervention above observation above simulation above derivation above human_hint above imported), and a confidence - to supersede a simulation-graded forecast with an observation-graded confirmation, and, when a forecast proved wrong, to strike the honest cancel-mark: a retraction, signed by the same source, on the record. Seventeen tests, all green. One honest limit, logged and not routed: the records are complete, content-addressed, and shareable, but unsigned - the Ed25519 private key is a secret, secrets are barred from the code by the specification's own constraint, and so cryptographic signing belongs to whoever deploys the mind, not to the mind itself.

Thirteen slices. Fifteen packs. One hundred twelve tests, all green. Three walls, all closed. Zero shortcuts taken, and every one of those numbers checkable from the files beside this one.

That is the story so far. The log proper begins below.

ENTRY 2 - 2026-07-26 - SLICE 14: THE MIND RUNS, AND ITS OWN RECORDS TELL THE STORY

For thirteen slices, konnectome's evidence lived where evidence usually lives in serious software: in test suites. One hundred twelve tests, four gate scripts, all green - and all invisible to anyone who does not run a test harness. A newcomer who asked to SEE the mind had to be handed a stack of files. Slice 14 ends that. It is the capstone the owner chose first from the menu, and it is the smallest slice imaginable in one sense - it adds no capability at all - and the largest in another: it makes every capability watchable at once.

The method, for the scientist. A sixteenth pack, capstone_demonstration (version 0.1.0), was built test-first: nineteen Prolog Unit tests written before the module, all green after it, and a twentieth test added by the pre-merge review told below, bringing the suite to one hundred thirty-two tests across sixteen packs. The pack's name was checked against every module filename in PrologAI's three hundred six packs before it was created - the library path gives the cousin's directories priority, so a name collision silently loads the wrong code - and it was clear. The pack declares layer zero, imports only its five konnectome siblings (cognitive_cycle, thought_combination, other_minds, prediction_loop, self_provenance) through the library path, and adds one script, bin/run_capstone_demonstration.sh, the first command in the repository that RUNS the mind rather than checking it. The three gates report zero violations, and the Causalontology conformance suite was re-measured - not merely inherited - at one hundred thirty-seven of one hundred thirty-seven vectors in both cousins this same day. No wall was hit. Nothing in any cousin was touched.

The story, for the reader. Run the one command and the mind boots with its body too warm: temperature forty against a defended set-point of thirty-seven. Tick by tick you watch the drive's distress become reward, the reward broadcast as dopamine, one action released each tick under an armed-but-resting safety override, and one content-addressed record minted per tick by the observer. By tick three the body reaches thirty-seven and stays; by tick ten the learnable connection has grown from one half to about two thirds, strengthened only in the moments when both its ends fired and dopamine said the moment mattered. Then the ladder: a hidden object, a forecast held on the record, a screen that lifts kindly (signed error zero - and the mind upgrades its own claim from simulation-grade to observation-grade) and a screen that lifts on nothing (signed error minus one - and the mind takes its claim back, on the record, under its own name). A chain of thought: rain causes wet ground causes a slippery path, three thoughts and two combinations, the chain checkable from the records alone. Sally's false belief, held beside the truth without corrupting it. And at the close, the story does the most konnectome thing possible: it lists what it did NOT demonstrate.

Two design decisions deserve the record. First, the story cannot flatter the mind. The narration checks the dynamics before claiming them - it will not print that the body settled unless the body settled - and two of the nineteen tests re-run the same boot world OUTSIDE the narration and measure the temperature and the weight directly, so the storyteller is audited by instruments it does not hold. Second, the story is deterministic to the byte: told twice, it is identical, down to every sixty-four-character identifier, because every instant is fixed and every record is named by its own contents. A demonstration a skeptic cannot reproduce is theater; this one is an experiment.

Before the merge, the slice faced an independent adversarial review - three reviewers working three lenses, code correctness, constitutional compliance, and document consistency, each finding then handed to a separate verifier told to refute it. Eight findings survived verification, and every one was applied. The finest of them deserves its own paragraph, because it is exactly the kind of subtle dishonesty this build exists to catch: the story told two different take-backs - the kind world's grade upgrade withdrawing the old stance, and the cruel world's outright retraction - but both had been stamped at the same instant, and since a retraction's reason is advisory rather than identity-bearing, the two supposedly different acts had minted ONE byte-identical record. The story said "checkable from the records alone," and by the records alone the two worlds could not be told apart. The fix stamps the cruel world's retraction one tick after the reveal - the mind meets the surprise, then disowns the claim - giving it its own identity; and the defect became a permanent twentieth test, so the suite can never again pass while the two take-backs coincide. The other fixes: the runner script now refuses a tick count below six on its usage path instead of dying raw; Rung One now displays the reveal-tick forecast - the very record the error grades and the provenance layer asserts about - so the printed record chain closes; an oversized narration predicate was split to honor the fifty-line bound; the tutorial and README ladders were renumbered to the book's own zero-based rungs; and two stale ledger claims that predated this slice - a three-versions-old data-structure reference, and the word "signed" where Observation-3 says the signature is deferred - were repaired.

The documents moved in lockstep, as the Tenth Commandment orders: the six SPARCD files to version 15, each with a slice-14 entry in its own register; the tutorial to version 12, with a new part on the capstone and its commandment count corrected to seventeen; the ledger with a new coverage row, the slice-14 note, and a stale one-hundred-nineteen-vector reference repaired; and the README re-counted - fourteen slices, sixteen packs, one hundred thirty-two tests - with its status badge finally telling the truth a newcomer most needs: the mind runs, and tells its story.
